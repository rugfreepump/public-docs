## 7. TU — Token Unlocked Sale Model Risks

### 7.1 TU Model Operation

Under the Token Unlocked ("TU") sale model, purchased token allocations become eligible for claiming after successful liquidity migration, subject to the applicable smart-contract conditions.

TU does not mean that purchased tokens are automatically transferred to the Buyer's wallet at the moment of purchase.

### 7.2 Dependence on Liquidity Migration

If liquidity migration is delayed, fails, or cannot be completed, the Buyer may be unable to claim the purchased TU allocation through the ordinary claiming mechanism.

The absence of a scheduled post-migration vesting restriction does not eliminate the requirement for successful migration.

### 7.3 Market Risk Following Unlocking

Once TU tokens become claimable and transferable, holders may attempt to sell their tokens.

Large or concentrated sales may create significant downward price pressure.

Early trading activity may be volatile, particularly where available liquidity or demand is limited.

### 7.4 No Guaranteed Exit

Immediate claim eligibility after migration does not guarantee that a Buyer can sell the claimed tokens without substantial slippage or financial loss.

**TU means the applicable purchased allocation is not subject to the TL scheduled unlocking mechanism; it does not mean that liquidity or profitability is guaranteed.**

## 8. TL — Token Locked Sale Model Risks

### 8.1 TL Model Operation

Under the Token Locked ("TL") sale model, purchased token allocations are subject to a scheduled unlocking mechanism.

The schedule is determined by the applicable sale-specific on-chain parameters.

These may include:

- The interval between token unlock cycles.
- The total number of unlock cycles.
- The unlock schedule's starting timestamp.

### 8.2 Unlock Schedule Start

For the implemented TL model, the unlock schedule begins when liquidity migration is successfully finalized.

**The unlock schedule does not begin merely because a Buyer has purchased tokens or because the bonding-curve sale has finalized.**

If migration is delayed, the beginning of the unlock schedule is correspondingly delayed.

If migration cannot be completed, the ordinary unlock schedule may not begin.

### 8.3 Restricted Access to Purchased Tokens

A TL Buyer may be unable to claim the entire purchased allocation immediately after liquidity migration.

Only the amount made claimable under the applicable smart-contract schedule and accounting rules may be available at a given time.

This restriction may prevent the Buyer from transferring or selling tokens during favorable market conditions.

### 8.4 Unlock Cycle Calculations

Claimable allocations depend on elapsed unlock cycles, previously claimed allocations, and the applicable smart-contract calculations.

Token amounts may be subject to integer arithmetic, rounding, and other programmed accounting rules.

Users should not assume that every claim will produce an identical token amount or that an arbitrary partial period creates a proportionate unlock entitlement.

### 8.5 Sale-Specific Schedule Variations

Administrative default unlock settings may be updated for future token sales.

Individual TL sales may retain the parameters recorded when they were created.

**Users must not assume that every TL sale uses the same unlock interval, total cycle count, or unlocking duration.**

Buyers should review the relevant sale-specific parameters before purchasing.

### 8.6 Price Changes During Lock Periods

Token prices may decline significantly while purchased allocations remain locked.

The ability to claim tokens gradually does not guarantee that those tokens will retain their value.

A Buyer may be unable to sell locked allocations before a market downturn.

### 8.7 Unlock-Related Selling Pressure

Scheduled unlocking may increase the number of tokens available for transfer or sale.

If substantial allocations become claimable around the same time, market selling pressure may increase.

The existence of an unlock schedule does not guarantee stable token prices or orderly trading.

### 8.8 No Guarantee of Future Claim Availability

Even where a scheduled unlock cycle has elapsed, successful claiming may depend on the continued operation of the relevant blockchain, smart contract, and supporting infrastructure.

**A scheduled unlock is not a guarantee of successful token delivery, market liquidity, or economic value.**
