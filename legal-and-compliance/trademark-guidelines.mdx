## 2. Understanding Trademarks

A trademark is generally a word, name, symbol, design, slogan, logo, or combination of identifying elements used to distinguish the goods or services of one party from those of others.

Trademark rights may arise through registration, qualifying commercial use, or other circumstances recognized under applicable law.

Trademark protection varies by jurisdiction, the goods or services involved, the nature of the mark, and the relevant facts.

A trademark owner may have legal rights against unauthorized uses that create a likelihood of confusion regarding the source, sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement of goods or services.

However, **not every reference to another party's trademark constitutes infringement**.

Lawful uses may include certain descriptive references, identification of genuine products or services, commentary, criticism, parody, comparative references, and other uses permitted under applicable law.

The existence of a registered trademark does not automatically prohibit every use of the same word, name, or symbol in every context.

## 3. Creator Responsibility

Creators are responsible for ensuring that all content they submit through RugFreePump complies with applicable trademark laws and other intellectual property requirements.

Before creating a coin, Creators should verify that they have a lawful basis for using the proposed:

- Coin name.
- Ticker symbol.
- Logo or image.
- Brand name or visual identity.
- Project description.
- Website and social-media links.

Creators must not use another party's trademark in a manner that infringes applicable rights, falsely implies authorization, or creates unlawful confusion.

Where permission or a license is legally necessary, the Creator is responsible for obtaining it before submitting the content.

RugFreePump does not routinely require Creators to submit evidence of trademark ownership, authorization, or licensing during coin creation.

**The absence of an ownership-verification requirement does not constitute permission to use another party's protected marks unlawfully.**

By submitting content, a Creator represents that the submission and its intended use through the Platform are lawful, including with respect to applicable intellectual property rights.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that every submitted coin name, ticker, image, or description has been independently reviewed for trademark compliance.

## 4. Prohibited Trademark and Impersonation Practices

Users must not engage in trademark infringement, misleading impersonation, or deceptive representations of affiliation through the Platform.

Prohibited conduct may include:

- Using another party's trademark in a manner likely to cause unlawful confusion about the source or sponsorship of a token.
- Creating a token that falsely claims to be officially issued by an existing business, organization, cryptocurrency project, exchange, or other rights holder.
- Using another party's logo or branding to misleadingly suggest that a token is authorized or endorsed by that party.
- Impersonating a company, project, organization, or individual through misleading token branding.
- Falsely claiming an official partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation.
- Using confusingly similar branding in circumstances that infringe applicable trademark rights.
- Submitting misleading website or social-media links to reinforce a false claim of affiliation.
- Misrepresenting the identity of a trademark owner or an authorized representative.
- Using the Platform to promote fraudulent activity involving unauthorized or misleading brand identities.

A token's name, ticker, image, description, and associated links may be considered together when evaluating whether its presentation is misleading.

A disclaimer does not automatically cure otherwise unlawful trademark infringement or deceptive conduct.

RugFreePump may also address impersonation, fraud, or misleading representations under its Terms of Service or other applicable policies, even where a trademark infringement claim has not been legally established.

## 5. Permitted References, Commentary, Parody, and Fan Content

RugFreePump recognizes that some references to third-party trademarks may be lawful without express permission from the trademark owner.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, potentially lawful uses may include:

- Referring to a trademark to identify its owner or associated products or services.
- Expressing opinions about a business, brand, product, or service.
- Commentary, criticism, or discussion involving a trademark.
- Parody or satire.
- Fan-created content that does not unlawfully misrepresent its origin or affiliation.
- Other descriptive, nominative, or legally permitted uses.

For example, a Creator may wish to create content discussing or commenting on an existing brand.

Such use is not automatically prohibited solely because the brand's name is mentioned.

However, Creators must not present independent content as officially authorized, sponsored, or endorsed when that representation is false or misleading.

Where appropriate, Creators should clearly identify independent fan, parody, or commentary projects.

Examples of potentially helpful descriptions include:

- "Unofficial Fan Project"
- "Independent Commentary"
- "Parody"
- "Not Affiliated With [Brand]"

These examples are illustrative and do not guarantee that a particular use is lawful.

**A label such as "fan," "parody," or "unofficial" does not automatically establish a legal defense or eliminate a likelihood of confusion.**

Whether a particular use is permissible depends on applicable law and the complete factual context.

## 6. No Implied Endorsement or Affiliation

The creation or availability of a token through RugFreePump does not establish that the token is officially associated with any person, business, organization, or trademark owner.

RugFreePump does not independently endorse every token or Creator using the Platform.

Similarly, a token that references an existing brand, business, cryptocurrency project, public figure, or organization is not necessarily authorized or endorsed by the referenced party.

Creators must not falsely represent that:

- A trademark owner created or approved their token.
- A company or organization sponsors their project.
- An existing cryptocurrency project officially supports their token.
- A public figure endorses or participates in their project.
- RugFreePump has verified or approved their intellectual property rights.
- A token has an official relationship with a third party when no such relationship exists.

Users should independently verify claims of sponsorship, authorization, or affiliation before relying on them.

RugFreePump is not responsible for independently confirming every affiliation claim made through Creator-submitted content.

Nothing in this section limits RugFreePump's obligations under applicable law.

## 8. Reporting Trademark Infringement

If you believe that content associated with RugFreePump infringes a trademark you own or are authorized to represent, you may submit a trademark infringement complaint.

Complaints should be sent to:

**Trademark Complaints:** [legal@rugfreepump.com](mailto:legal@rugfreepump.com)

**Official Website:** [https://rugfreepump.com](https://rugfreepump.com)

To assist with reviewing the complaint, please provide the information described below.

### 8.1 Complainant Information

Provide:

- Your full legal name.
- Your organization or business name, if applicable.
- Your email address.
- Your mailing address.
- Your telephone number, where reasonably necessary.
- Your relationship to the trademark owner.

If you are submitting the complaint on behalf of another person or organization, identify the rights holder and explain your authority to act on their behalf.

### 8.2 Trademark Identification

Clearly identify the trademark involved.

Where applicable, provide:

- The trademark name or description.
- A representation of the trademark or logo.
- The trademark owner.
- The country or jurisdiction in which rights are claimed.
- Trademark registration or application numbers, if available.
- The relevant trademark registry, where applicable.
- The goods or services associated with the mark.
- Relevant evidence of trademark use or ownership.

Trademark registration is not required for every legally recognized trademark right.

Where rights are based on use rather than registration, provide sufficient information to explain the claimed rights.

### 8.3 Identification of the Disputed Content

Provide information reasonably sufficient for RugFreePump to locate the allegedly infringing content.

This may include:

- The relevant RugFreePump token-sale page URL.
- The Solana token mint address.
- The coin name and ticker symbol.
- The disputed image or logo.
- The relevant metadata URL.
- The Creator's public wallet address, if known.
- Screenshots showing the disputed content.
- Other identifying information.

Where multiple tokens or items are involved, identify each disputed item as clearly as reasonably possible.

### 8.4 Explanation of the Alleged Violation

Explain why you believe the disputed content infringes your trademark rights.

Where relevant, describe:

- How the disputed mark is being used.
- Why the use may create confusion.
- Whether the content falsely suggests affiliation or endorsement.
- Whether the Creator appears to be impersonating the trademark owner.
- Any relevant differences or similarities between the marks.
- Any other circumstances supporting the complaint.

### 8.5 Accuracy and Authority

Include a statement substantially equivalent to:

"I confirm that the information provided in this complaint is accurate to the best of my knowledge and that I am the trademark owner or am authorized to act on behalf of the trademark owner."

You may also provide supporting documents or other relevant evidence.

Do not submit wallet private keys, recovery phrases, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information.

RugFreePump may request clarification or additional information where reasonably necessary.

## 9. Review of Trademark Complaints

Upon receiving a trademark complaint, RugFreePump may review the information provided and evaluate whether action is appropriate.

The review may include:

- Identifying the relevant token or content.
- Determining whether the disputed content appears through Platform-controlled interfaces.
- Reviewing evidence of trademark ownership or authorization.
- Evaluating the alleged likelihood of confusion or misleading affiliation.
- Considering whether the disputed use may be lawful commentary, parody, criticism, or another permitted use.
- Reviewing the content against these Guidelines, the Terms of Service, and other applicable policies.
- Requesting additional information from the complainant.
- Contacting the affected Creator where reasonably possible and appropriate.
- Determining which moderation actions are technically available.

RugFreePump may consider whether a complaint is incomplete, unsupported, abusive, misleading, or unrelated to trademark rights.

**Submission of a complaint does not automatically establish that trademark infringement has occurred.**

RugFreePump is not a court and does not issue binding legal determinations of trademark ownership or infringement.

However, RugFreePump may make reasonable Platform-moderation decisions based on available information, applicable law, and its policies.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that every complaint will be resolved within a particular timeframe.

Where applicable law imposes specific obligations or deadlines, those requirements govern.

## 10. Removal or Restriction of Trademark-Infringing Content

RugFreePump reserves the right, subject to applicable law, to remove, restrict, or disable access to content through Platform-controlled systems when it reasonably determines that the content:

- Infringes applicable trademark rights.
- Impersonates another party.
- Creates misleading impressions of authorization, sponsorship, or affiliation.
- Violates applicable law.
- Breaches the Terms of Service or other enforceable Platform policies.

Where appropriate and legally permitted, RugFreePump may take action immediately and without prior notice.

### 10.1 Available Image-Removal Controls

RugFreePump maintains an administrative database tool that can remove coin image references from its Platform database.

This functionality may be used to address coin images associated with trademark infringement, impersonation, or other applicable policy violations.

Removing an image reference from RugFreePump's database does not necessarily:

- Delete the original image from IPFS or another storage provider.
- Remove copies held by independent gateways or caches.
- Modify the token's original metadata.
- Change the token's name or ticker.
- Delete the token mint.
- Reverse any blockchain transaction.
- Prevent third parties from displaying the original image.

The visibility of an affected image may also depend on caching, independent data sources, and other technical factors.

### 10.2 Other Platform-Controlled Measures

RugFreePump reserves the right to introduce or use additional content-moderation measures where legally appropriate and technically available.

Such measures may include restricting the display, hosting, promotion, or accessibility of disputed content through Platform-controlled services.

**These Guidelines do not represent that RugFreePump currently operates a complete token-delisting, token-blacklisting, project-removal, or trading-blocking system.**

The availability and effectiveness of any moderation action depend on the Platform's actual technical capabilities.

### 10.3 No Guaranteed Removal of Entire Projects

A trademark complaint does not guarantee that RugFreePump can remove an entire token project from its interfaces or prevent further participation in an existing sale.

In particular, removal of a coin image from RugFreePump's database does not necessarily prevent the coin from appearing in Platform listings, search results, sale pages, or other interfaces.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that every disputed token name, ticker, description, or other metadata field can be independently modified or removed after token creation.

### 10.4 On-Chain Rights and Transactions

A content-moderation decision does not, by itself, cancel a token sale, invalidate token ownership, reverse a purchase, terminate an on-chain token-claim entitlement, or create a refund right.

Existing on-chain rights and obligations remain subject to the applicable program logic, transaction conditions, and law.

RugFreePump will not represent a content-removal action as an on-chain cancellation unless the relevant on-chain action has actually occurred.

## 14. Copyright and Other Intellectual Property Rights

Trademark law and copyright law protect different types of intellectual property.

Trademark law generally concerns source-identifying names, logos, symbols, and other marks.

Copyright law generally protects qualifying original creative works.

A particular logo, image, illustration, or other item may implicate both trademark and copyright rights.

Complaints involving copyrighted artwork, photographs, illustrations, or other protected creative material may be addressed under RugFreePump's separate **DMCA Policy**.

Complaints involving impersonation, misleading affiliation, fraudulent representations, or other unlawful conduct may also be evaluated under the Terms of Service and applicable law.

RugFreePump may consider the substance of a complaint rather than solely the category selected by the reporting party.

Nothing in these Guidelines limits remedies available under applicable intellectual property or consumer protection laws.
