## 8. Token Sale Models and Claiming

RugFreePump supports two Token Sale models. The applicable model and its parameters are determined by the relevant Smart Contract state.

### 8.1 TU — Token Unlocked

Under the TU model, purchased Tokens become eligible for claiming according to the Smart Contract conditions following successful Liquidity Migration.

Purchasing Tokens does not necessarily deliver them immediately to the Buyer's Wallet.

### 8.2 TL — Token Locked

Under the TL model, purchased Tokens unlock according to a sale-specific schedule.

The current TL mechanism begins its unlock schedule when Liquidity Migration is successfully finalized, rather than when an individual Buyer purchases Tokens.

The schedule is determined by the relevant on-chain parameters, including the unlock interval, total unlock cycles, and schedule starting timestamp.

Claimable amounts depend on elapsed cycles, previously claimed allocations, and Smart Contract calculation rules.

**Users must not assume that every TL sale has the same unlock schedule.** Administrative defaults may change without necessarily changing parameters already recorded for an individual sale.

### 8.3 Claim Transactions

Claiming is not necessarily automatic.

Eligible Buyers may need to initiate separate blockchain transactions, pay applicable Network costs, and satisfy the relevant on-chain conditions.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that a claim will execute successfully or become available at a particular time.
