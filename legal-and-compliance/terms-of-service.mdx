## 8. Token Sale Models: TU and TL

RugFreePump supports two token sale models.

### 8.1 TU — Token Unlocked

Under the TU model, purchased tokens become eligible for claiming in accordance with the applicable smart-contract conditions after successful liquidity migration.

A successful purchase does not necessarily mean that tokens are immediately transferable to the Buyer's wallet.

Token claiming may require a separate blockchain transaction.

### 8.2 TL — Token Locked

Under the TL model, purchased tokens are subject to a scheduled unlocking mechanism.

The unlock schedule is associated with the relevant token sale and is governed by its recorded smart-contract parameters.

Depending on the sale configuration, these parameters include:

- The interval between unlock cycles.
- The total number of unlock cycles.
- The unlock schedule's starting timestamp.

For the current implemented TL model, the unlock schedule begins when liquidity migration is successfully finalized, not when the Buyer makes a purchase. Unlock intervals and cycle counts are sale-specific on-chain parameters.

Claimable amounts depend on elapsed unlock cycles, previously claimed allocations, and the smart contract's calculation rules.

### 8.3 Schedule Variations

**Users must not assume that all TL token sales have identical unlock schedules.**

Administrative defaults may change, while an individual sale may retain the parameters recorded for that sale.

Users are responsible for reviewing the applicable sale-specific unlock terms.

### 8.4 Claiming Is Not Automatic

Eligible token allocations may require Users to initiate a claim transaction.

Claim transactions may incur network costs and may be affected by maintenance, blockchain availability, or smart-contract conditions.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that every claim will execute at a particular time.
