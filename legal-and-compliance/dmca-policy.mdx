## 5. Information Required in a Copyright Notice

To assist with review, a copyright infringement notice should include the following information.

### 5.1 Identification of the Copyrighted Work

Identify the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.

Where appropriate, provide:

- The title or description of the work.
- The name of the copyright owner.
- A link to an authorized publication of the work.
- Relevant registration information, if available.
- Other information demonstrating the claimed rights.

Copyright registration is not required for every copyright complaint, although registration may be relevant to particular legal remedies or proceedings.

### 5.2 Identification of the Allegedly Infringing Material

Provide enough information for RugFreePump to locate the allegedly infringing material.

This may include:

- The RugFreePump token-sale page URL.
- The relevant Solana token mint address.
- The token name and symbol.
- A description of the disputed image or content.
- A direct URL to the material.
- The relevant metadata URL or content identifier.
- Other identifying details.

Where multiple items are involved, identify each disputed item as clearly as reasonably possible.

### 5.3 Contact Information

Provide contact information reasonably sufficient for RugFreePump to communicate with you.

This may include:

- Your full legal name.
- Your organization, if applicable.
- Your email address.
- Your mailing address.
- Your telephone number.

Only provide information necessary for the complaint process.

Do not submit wallet private keys, recovery phrases, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information.

### 5.4 Good-Faith Statement

Include a statement substantially equivalent to:

"I have a good-faith belief that the use of the copyrighted material described in this notice is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."

### 5.5 Accuracy and Authority Statement

Include a statement substantially equivalent to:

"I declare, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this notice is accurate and that I am the copyright owner or am authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right allegedly infringed."

### 5.6 Signature

Include your physical or electronic signature.

An authorized representative may submit a notice on behalf of the copyright owner.

A typed legal name may serve as an electronic signature where legally sufficient.

### 5.7 Additional Legal Requirements

A notice intended to satisfy Section 512(c)(3) of the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act must comply with the applicable statutory requirements.

Providing the information described above does not guarantee that a notice is legally sufficient in every jurisdiction.

RugFreePump may request clarification or additional information where reasonably necessary to evaluate a complaint.

RugFreePump does not guarantee that every complaint will be resolved within a particular timeframe.

## 9. Counter-Notification Procedure

A User whose content has been removed or disabled in response to a copyright complaint may submit a counter-notification if the User believes that the removal resulted from mistake or misidentification.

A counter-notification should include the following information.

### 9.1 Identification of Removed Material

Identify the material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before the restriction.

Where available, provide:

- The relevant RugFreePump page URL.
- The token mint address.
- The disputed content URL.
- A description of the removed material.
- Any notice or reference information provided by RugFreePump.

### 9.2 Contact Information

Provide your:

- Full legal name.
- Mailing address.
- Telephone number.
- Email address.

### 9.3 Statement Under Penalty of Perjury

Include a statement substantially equivalent to:

"I declare, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification."

### 9.4 Jurisdiction and Service Statement

For a counter-notification intended to satisfy Section 512(g)(3) of the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act, include the applicable statutory consent to jurisdiction and acceptance of service of process.

For a person whose address is within the United States, this generally concerns the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which the address is located.

For a person whose address is outside the United States, this generally concerns a Federal District Court for a judicial district in which the service provider may be found.

The counter-notification must also include agreement to accept service of process from the original complaining party or that party's agent.

Users should obtain legal advice if they are uncertain about the implications of making this statement.

### 9.5 Signature

Include a physical or electronic signature.

### 9.6 Submission

Counter-notifications may be submitted to:

**Email:** [legal@rugfreepump.com](mailto:legal@rugfreepump.com)

A counter-notification may have legal consequences.

Users should not submit a counter-notification containing false statements or without a good-faith basis.
