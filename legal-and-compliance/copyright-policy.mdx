## 1. Purpose and Scope

RugFreePump respects the rights of copyright owners and expects Users to submit only content they are legally entitled to use.

This Policy applies to copyright concerns involving content submitted, displayed, referenced, hosted, or otherwise made accessible through Platform-controlled services, including:

- Token images, illustrations, and artwork.
- Logos containing copyright-protected creative elements.
- Photographs and graphic designs.
- Token descriptions and other written materials.
- Creator-submitted metadata.
- Promotional content displayed through the Platform.
- Other qualifying copyrighted material associated with Platform functionality.

Not every token name, symbol, short phrase, or identifying element qualifies for copyright protection.

Complaints primarily involving brand identity, impersonation, misleading affiliation, or confusingly similar project names may be more appropriately addressed under the separate [Trademark Guidelines](https://rugfreepump.com/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).

Some disputes may involve both copyright and trademark rights.

This Policy concerns RugFreePump's handling of copyright complaints. It does not guarantee that the Platform can control content independently recorded, stored, or distributed through blockchain networks and third-party services.

## 2. User Responsibility for Submitted Content

Users are responsible for ensuring that content they submit through RugFreePump is lawful and appropriately authorized.

Creators must not submit, reproduce, publish, or distribute copyrighted material without the necessary rights or permissions unless the relevant use is otherwise permitted by applicable law.

This responsibility includes content obtained from:

- Websites and search engines.
- Social-media platforms.
- Online image libraries.
- Other token projects.
- Artists, photographers, or designers.
- Commercial branding materials.
- Other publicly accessible sources.

**Public availability does not automatically mean that material is free to copy or reuse.**

Likewise, modifying an image, adding text, changing colors, or using an automated generation tool does not necessarily eliminate another person's copyright rights.

By submitting content, Users represent that they have the necessary rights, licenses, permissions, or other lawful grounds for its intended use through the Platform.

Users must not knowingly submit content that infringes another person's rights.

RugFreePump does not independently verify the copyright ownership or licensing status of every item submitted by Creators.

## 5. Information to Include in a Copyright Complaint

To support an effective review, copyright complaints should contain the following information.

### 5.1 Identification of the Copyrighted Work

Identify the original work you believe has been infringed.

Useful information may include:

- The title or description of the work.
- The copyright owner's name.
- A link to an authorized publication.
- Relevant copyright registration details, if available.
- Information explaining your ownership or authority.
- Supporting documentation where reasonably necessary.

Copyright registration requirements vary by jurisdiction and by the remedy being sought. Registration is not universally required for copyright protection.

### 5.2 Identification of the Disputed Material

Provide sufficient information for RugFreePump to locate the allegedly infringing content.

This may include:

- The RugFreePump token-sale page URL.
- The Solana token mint address.
- The token name and symbol.
- The disputed image or content URL.
- The metadata URI or content identifier.
- A screenshot or description of the material.
- Other relevant identifying information.

Where multiple items are disputed, identify each item as clearly as reasonably possible.

### 5.3 Reporting Party Information

Provide contact information sufficient for RugFreePump to communicate with you.

This may include:

- Full legal name.
- Organization, if applicable.
- Email address.
- Mailing address or telephone number where relevant and reasonably necessary.
- Details of your authority to represent the copyright owner, if applicable.

Do not provide unnecessary sensitive information.

**Never submit Wallet private keys, recovery phrases, passwords, or authentication secrets.**

### 5.4 Explanation of the Alleged Infringement

Explain why you believe the disputed material infringes your copyright.

Where relevant, identify whether the complaint concerns unauthorized reproduction, publication, distribution, display, or another protected use.

Describe any relevant licensing restrictions or reasons why the disputed use is believed to be unauthorized.

### 5.5 Good-Faith and Accuracy Confirmation

Include a statement substantially equivalent to:

"I have a good-faith belief that the use of the identified material is not authorized by the copyright owner or otherwise permitted by applicable law. I confirm that the information provided is accurate to the best of my knowledge and that I am the copyright owner or am authorized to act on the owner's behalf."

An electronic signature or typed legal name may be included to identify and authenticate the reporting party.

RugFreePump may request additional declarations or formal information where required by applicable law.

**These general submission requirements do not replace any mandatory statutory notice requirements applicable to a particular complaint.**

## 6. Review of Copyright Complaints

RugFreePump will assess received complaints according to the available information, applicable law, the nature of the disputed content, and the Platform's technical capabilities.

Review may involve:

1. Identifying the disputed work and corresponding Platform content.
2. Checking whether the complaint contains sufficient information.
3. Evaluating the complainant's claimed rights and supporting evidence.
4. Considering relevant permissions, licenses, exceptions, or defenses.
5. Determining which affected systems are controlled by RugFreePump.
6. Requesting clarification or additional evidence where appropriate.
7. Considering the affected Creator's explanation where appropriate.
8. Determining whether an available restriction or removal measure is justified or legally required.

RugFreePump may decline to act on complaints that are materially incomplete, unsupported, fraudulent, or unrelated to copyright, except where applicable law requires a different response.

Where applicable law requires action within a particular period or without undue delay, RugFreePump must comply with the relevant requirement.

**RugFreePump does not guarantee a particular resolution timeframe except where one is required by applicable law.**

A complaint may be referred to a different Platform policy where its substance primarily concerns trademarks, impersonation, privacy, or another issue.

## 7. Content Removal and Platform-Controlled Measures

RugFreePump may take appropriate action concerning disputed content through infrastructure under its control, subject to applicable law and available technical measures.

### 7.1 Current Image-Reference Removal Capability

RugFreePump currently maintains an administrative tool capable of removing coin image references from its Platform database.

This functionality may be used where a token image is associated with copyright infringement or another applicable policy violation.

Removing a database image reference does not necessarily:

- Delete the original image from IPFS or another storage provider.
- Modify the original token metadata.
- Remove images cached by browsers or independent gateways.
- Prevent third-party applications from retrieving the image.
- Change the token name, symbol, or description.
- Delete the token mint or associated blockchain accounts.
- Remove the token project from every Platform interface.

The practical effect of removal depends on how the relevant interfaces retrieve and display image information.

### 7.2 Other Available or Future Measures

Depending on the Platform's actual implementation, additional measures may include restricting the display of disputed content, disabling access to Platform-controlled files, modifying presentation of relevant content, or restricting future submissions.

**These are possible measures, not representations that every listed capability currently exists.**

RugFreePump does not currently represent that it operates a complete token-delisting, wallet-blacklisting, project-removal, or trading-blocking system.

Where a legally required action cannot be performed using an existing administrative tool, additional technical or operational measures may be necessary.

### 7.3 No Automatic Cancellation of Token Sales

A copyright complaint or content-removal action does not, by itself:

- Cancel an existing token sale.
- Reverse a completed token purchase.
- Invalidate token ownership.
- Terminate an existing on-chain claim entitlement.
- Create a refund entitlement.
- Reverse liquidity migration.
- Prevent independent token transfers or trading.

Any separate on-chain action remains subject to the applicable Smart Contract conditions and legal requirements.

RugFreePump will not describe an off-chain content restriction as an on-chain cancellation unless the relevant blockchain action has actually occurred.

### 7.4 Legal Obligations

The limitations of existing administrative tools do not eliminate legal obligations that independently apply to RugFreePump.

Where applicable law requires additional action concerning content or services within RugFreePump's control, the operator must address those requirements through legally appropriate measures.

## 8. Blockchain, Token Metadata, and Decentralized Storage

RugFreePump operates on the Solana blockchain and interacts with decentralized or independently operated infrastructure.

### 8.1 Blockchain Records

Token mints, transactions, Smart Contract accounts, and associated records may remain publicly accessible through independent blockchain infrastructure.

RugFreePump generally cannot arbitrarily delete or rewrite finalized blockchain records.

A copyright complaint does not automatically enable RugFreePump to remove token mints, reverse transactions, or erase public blockchain history.

### 8.2 Token Metadata

Token names, symbols, descriptions, images, and related links may be established during token creation and referenced through publicly accessible metadata.

RugFreePump's ordinary token-creation workflow does not provide a general mechanism for Creators to change these fields after creation.

Removing an image reference from RugFreePump's database does not necessarily alter the original metadata file or its independent copies.

### 8.3 Decentralized Storage

Images and metadata may be uploaded to IPFS-related infrastructure or other storage services.

Such content may be replicated, cached, or retained by independent gateways, nodes, archives, or other providers.

**Removing content from RugFreePump-controlled systems does not guarantee its deletion from independent decentralized storage or third-party services.**

### 8.4 Independent Services

Wallet applications, blockchain explorers, decentralized exchanges, indexing services, and external websites may independently display or retain token information.

Copyright owners may need to contact those services directly regarding content outside RugFreePump's control.

These technical limitations do not excuse noncompliance with applicable obligations concerning systems and services under RugFreePump's control.

- Fair dealing.

- Public-domain material.
- Uses covered by statutory exceptions.

## 14. Copyright and Trademark Distinctions

Copyright and trademark rights protect different interests.

Copyright generally protects qualifying original creative works, such as illustrations, photographs, artwork, and written expression.

Trademark law generally protects qualifying identifiers used to distinguish the commercial source of goods or services, subject to applicable law.

A token image or logo may raise copyright concerns, trademark concerns, or both.

A token name or symbol may raise trademark or impersonation concerns without necessarily qualifying for copyright protection.

Complaints involving unauthorized brand use, misleading endorsement, confusingly similar branding, or project impersonation may be addressed under the separate [Trademark Guidelines](https://rugfreepump.com/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).

RugFreePump may assess a complaint according to its substance rather than solely the label used by the reporting party.
