## 2. Our Privacy Principles

RugFreePump is designed primarily around blockchain wallet addresses rather than conventional accounts requiring a User's legal name.

Our intended privacy practices include:

1. Collecting information reasonably necessary for Platform operations, security, authentication, transaction processing, consent verification, and legal compliance.
2. Using wallet-based authentication instead of requiring conventional username-and-password accounts for ordinary Platform access.
3. Not requesting wallet private keys or recovery phrases.
4. Not using personal information for behavioral advertising or cross-site advertising profiling.
5. Not selling personal information to advertisers or data brokers.
6. Limiting access to operational information according to legitimate administrative and security needs.
7. Recognizing that public blockchain records and decentralized storage may remain accessible independently of the Platform.
8. Maintaining records needed to verify User consent, investigate security incidents, and support legitimate legal obligations.

**Wallet-based access does not mean that all Platform activity is anonymous.**

A wallet address may be associated with transaction history, session information, public records, or other information that can identify or help identify a person.

## 7. Cookies and Browser Storage

RugFreePump uses cookies and browser storage for functional and security-related purposes.

### 7.1 Persistent Visitor Cookie

The Platform uses a cookie named:

**`rfp_visitor_id`**

Its purpose is to recognize a browser for operational announcement-view status.

The cookie contains a randomly generated identifier rather than a User's legal name.

The current implementation configures this cookie with:

- **HttpOnly:** Enabled.
- **SameSite:** Lax.
- **Path:** `/`.
- **Secure:** Enabled in production environments.
- **Expiration:** December 31, 2099.

**This is a long-lived persistent cookie.** Unless deleted, replaced, blocked, or otherwise invalidated, it may remain on the browser for an extended period.

The corresponding server-side announcement record may store the visitor identifier and the last-viewed announcement timestamp.

The cookie is not intended for advertising, cross-site profiling, or the sale of browsing information.

### 7.2 Authentication and Session Storage
