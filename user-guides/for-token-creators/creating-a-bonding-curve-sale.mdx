Creating a bonding curve sale on RugFreePump starts with creating your coin, configuring its metadata, and making the creator's initial purchase during creation.

Once the transaction succeeds, your bonding curve sale becomes **Live** and is available for buyers to discover and participate in.

# Before You Start

## Use Content You Have the Right to Use

When creating your coin, ensure you have the legal right to use all associated content, including **names, tickers, descriptions, images, and artwork**.

In particular, avoid using copyrighted or trademarked images, logos, characters, brand identities, or other protected content belonging to third parties unless you have explicit permission or a valid license.

RugFreePump does not require creators to provide proof of ownership or licensing during coin creation. However, if a rights holder reports content associated with your coin, RugFreePump will review the complaint.

If the reported content is found to infringe third-party rights, RugFreePump reserves the right to remove or disable access to the affected content from the RugFreePump platform immediately and without prior notice.

Please note that removing content from the RugFreePump platform cannot modify or delete data that already exists on the Solana blockchain or within independent decentralized storage systems. RugFreePump's actions are limited to halting the display, promotion, or hosting of the infringing content on its own interface.

Creators bear sole responsibility for ensuring their submissions do not infringe upon the rights of others.

## Prerequisites Checklist

Before creating a sale, ensure you have prepared the following requirements:

* **Supported Solana Wallet:** An active, funded wallet provider such as [Phantom](https://phantom.app) or [Solflare](https://solflare.com/).
* **Adequate SOL Balance:** Enough SOL to cover the creation fee, PDA storage rent, network fees, and any initial token buy-in.
* **Token Branding:** A designated coin name and matching ticker symbol.
* **Project Description:** A compelling summary or description explaining your coin’s concept (optional).
* **Digital Footprint:** Active website links and social media channels (e.g., X, Telegram) if applicable.
* **Coin Artwork:** A high-quality profile image that fully satisfies the platform's upload size and format constraints.

## Keep Enough SOL in Your Wallet

As a creator, initializing a new coin and bonding curve sale requires a higher SOL balance than just the amount you intend to deploy for your initial token purchase. 

To prevent transaction failures, your wallet must contain sufficient SOL to cover the initial buy order plus all protocol, storage, and network execution costs.

### Total Wallet Balance Breakdown

Before executing the creation transaction, ensure your wallet can comfortably cover the following distinct line items:

### 1. Protocol Creation Fee
* **Mechanism:** A mandatory protocol fee currently set at the **$5 USD equivalent in SOL**.
* **Calculation:** The smart contract reads the real-time SOL/USD oracle price at the exact moment of transaction execution to dynamically calculate and deduct the required SOL fraction.

### 2. On-Chain Storage Rent Exemption
* **Mechanism:** Solana requires a baseline allocation of SOL to make the **Program Derived Addresses (PDAs)** rent-exempt.
* **Purpose:** This deposit permanently secures the validator storage space required to maintain your coin's metadata, token mint configuration, and bonding curve state.

### 3. Initial Creator Buy-In
* **Mechanism:** The optional or mandatory amount of SOL you choose to contribute toward the immediate purchase of your own coin supply at launch.

### 4. Solana Network Transaction Fees
* **Mechanism:** The micro-amounts of SOL required by the network to pay for compute budget units, prioritization fees, and signature verification.

<Callout type="warning" title="Execution Warning">
The RugFreePump on-chain program validates your total balance before initiating the launch sequence. If your wallet possesses insufficient SOL to cover the combined sum of the creation fee, PDA rent, your initial purchase, and network fees, the transaction will **fail during the simulation phase or revert during mainnet execution**. Always maintain a small buffer of extra SOL in your deployment wallet to ensure seamless execution. 
</Callout>

# Open the Create Coin Page

You can initiate the creation process from any page on the platform:
* **Navigation:** Select **Create Coin** from the top header navigation or the sidebar.
* **Direct Access:** Navigate directly to the creation interface via [rugfreepump.com/create](https://rugfreepump.com/create).

# Sign In & Wallet Authentication

You can begin filling out your coin's details before authenticating. However, wallet connection and authentication are strictly required before submitting the final transaction.

* **Initiate Login:** Select **Sign In** from the top header, or click **Login to create coin** situated at the bottom of the creation form.
* **Supported Wallets:** RugFreePump natively supports [Phantom](https://phantom.app) and [Solflare](https://solflare.com).

# Connect Your Wallet
When connecting for the first time, your wallet extension or mobile app will prompt you with a connection request. Approve this request to grant the platform permission to view your public wallet address.

# Sign the Login Message
After connecting, your wallet will prompt you for a secure cryptographic message signature.
* **Purpose:** This proves you own the private keys to the connected wallet address.
* **Safety Note:** This is a zero-cost message signature. It is used strictly for authentication and **does not transfer SOL, tokens, or grant contract allowances**.

Once successfully signed, the **Login to create coin** button will transition to **Create coin**.

# Complete the Consent Process

RugFreePump permits read-only platform exploration without authentication. However, executing financial operations—such as initializing a token bonding curve—mandates explicit legal consent.

When required, a **Consent Card** will appear on your screen presenting three primary legal frameworks:
1. **Terms of Service**
2. **Privacy Policy**
3. **Risk Disclosure**

## Consent Steps
* **Review:** Click each legal link at the bottom of the card to read the respective documentation fully.
* **Acceptance:** Once reviewed, select **Continue**.
* **Cryptographic Agreement:** Your wallet will prompt you to sign a consent confirmation message. Approve the signature to write your compliance state locally. Like the login message, this signature **costs no network fees and transfers no assets**.

<Callout type="info" title="Policy Updates">
Your consent signature remains valid for the lifespan of the current document versions. If RugFreePump updates any of these three core legal frameworks, you will be prompted to re-verify and sign an updated consent message before executing your next financial transaction. For deeper insights, review the [RugFreePump Consent Documentation](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).
</Callout>

Carefully input your token parameters within the creation interface. Ensure your metadata adheres strictly to the protocol's hardcoded constraints:

### Ticker

Input the complete, absolute URLs for your project's digital ecosystem channels:
* **Website:** Your project's central landing page URL.

# Creator Incentive Program

When the Creator Incentive Program is active, the creation page will display the current rewards available for launching a new coin.

* **Current Rate:** Eligible creators receive a **1% reward**.
* **Program Changes:** The platform administration can enable, disable, or change this reward rate for future launches.
* **Rate Protection:** Your reward rate is permanently locked in the moment you create your coin. Even if the platform later turns off the program or changes the rate, your active sale will keep its original rate.
* **Payout Timing:** Your reward is paid out automatically as soon as your sale successfully reaches its hard cap and completes liquidity migration to the DEX.

# Sale Lifetime

Every bonding curve sale has a fixed time limit set by the platform at launch.

* **Time Window:** Sales are configured to last between **24 and 48 hours**. 
* **Precise Timing:** The system counts the time in seconds. This means a lifetime does not have to be a whole hour—it can be a precise duration like 24.5 hours or 35.2 hours.
* **No Retrospective Changes:** Your sale's lifetime is locked upon creation. If the platform changes the default time limit rules later, **your ongoing sale will not be affected**.
* **Sale Outcomes:**
  * **Completed:** If your sale hits its hard cap before time runs out, it automatically finishes and moves to liquidity migration.
  * **Expired:** If the timer reaches zero before hitting the hard cap, the sale closes unsuccessfully and does not move forward.

# Choose Your Initial Purchase

After filling out your coin details and logging in, click **Create coin**. A purchase panel will appear where you can buy the very first supply of your new token.

In this panel, you will see your available wallet balance alongside these options and metrics:
* The amount of SOL you wish to spend
* **Reset** button
* **Max** button
* The estimated number of tokens you will receive

## Initial Purchase Rules

Your initial purchase is the first buy transaction on your new bonding curve.

* **Mandatory Step:** Making an initial purchase is **required** to launch your coin. 
* **Minimum Requirement:** You must spend at least **$1 USD worth of SOL** (calculated using real-time market prices at launch).
* **Automatic Correction:** The interface and the smart contract enforce this minimum. If you type an amount lower than $1 USD, the system automatically corrects it to the minimum allowed amount.
* **Total Cost vs. Net Buy:** The amount you enter is your total spending amount, not the final amount that goes into the bonding curve.
* **Platform Fee:** RugFreePump charges a **1% buy fee**. The system protocol deducts this fee automatically and sends the rest to the bonding curve pricing model. For example, if you spend `10 SOL`, a 0.1 SOL fee is deducted, and **9.9 SOL** goes toward purchasing your tokens.
* **Token Pricing:** The final number of tokens you get is calculated automatically by the bonding curve pricing model based on the current oracle SOL/USD price when the transaction is processed.

## Reset

Click **Reset** to clear the numbers in the purchase field and start over.

## Max

Click **Max** to automatically fill in the highest amount you can spend.

* **Smart Cap:** If your wallet balance is larger than what the sale needs to hit its hard cap, the **Max** button will only fill in the exact amount needed to fill the sale, rather than using your entire balance.
* **Gas Buffer:** Make sure to leave a little extra SOL in your wallet to cover the platform creation costs and Solana network transaction fees.

# Launching and Completing the Sale

Once you enter a valid initial purchase amount, the final **Create coin** button becomes active. Click it to start the launch process.

If you have not completed the required legal consent step, the platform will prompt you to do so before continuing.

### Step 1: Preparing Metadata
The platform will first upload your coin details and prepare the blockchain transaction.
> **Status Message:** *Uploading Metadata & Preparing The Transaction...*
* **Rule:** Do not close or refresh your browser window while this process is running.

### Step 2: Approve the Transaction
Your connected wallet (Phantom or Solflare) will open a pop-up window asking you to confirm the transaction.
> **Status Message:** *Waiting For Transaction Approval...*
* **Rule:** Review the transaction details in your wallet and click Approve. **Do not close or reload the website window.**

### Step 3: Wait for Blockchain Confirmation
The system will now wait for the Solana network to confirm and finalize your transaction.
> **Status Message:** *Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...*

### Step 4: Finalizing the Page
Once confirmed, RugFreePump securely processes the blockchain data to build your new sale webpage.
> **Status Message:** *Transaction Succeeded. Waiting For Transaction data To Map The Sale Page...*
* **Outcome:** You do not need to do anything. The platform will automatically redirect you directly to your new, live sale page.

# Handling Cancelled Transactions

If you reject the wallet request or if the Solana network times out, the launch will stop and no sale will be created. RugFreePump will automatically clean up the temporary files prepared for the launch.

<Callout type="warning" title="Cleanup in Progress">
During this cleanup process, the webpage will lock and display:

**"IPFS Metadata Cleanup Is In Progress..."**

Please keep your browser window open until this automated cleanup finishes.
</Callout>

Once the cleanup completes, the interface unlocks, and you can safely:
* Try launching the coin again
* Edit your coin's name, ticker, or description
* Change your initial purchase amount
* Safely navigate away or leave the page

# Your Sale Is Live!

If the transaction is successful, you will be redirected to the new sale page. Your bonding curve sale starts in the **Live** state, making it immediately visible to the platform users.

## Bonding Curve Pool Information

Once your sale is active, the main dashboard displays the core structural details of your bonding curve. Here is what each metric means:

* **Pool Address:** The secure on-chain account where all raised SOL is stored safely during the sale.
* **Total Supply:** The absolute total number of tokens created for your coin.
* **Tokens For Sale:** The specific portion of the total supply that buyers can purchase through the bonding curve.
* **Tokens For Liquidity:** The portion of the supply locked and saved to fund the liquidity pool after a successful launch.
* **Hard Cap:** The maximum amount of SOL the sale is allowed to raise before successfully closing.
* **Start Time:** The exact date and time your coin went live on the platform.
* **End Time:** The exact moment the sale's time limit expires.
* **Listing On:** The decentralized exchange (DEX) where your coin's official trading pool will be created after a successful migration.
* **LP Tokens:** Information detailing how the liquidity-provider tokens are handled or permanently locked after migration to guarantee safety.

## Tracking Sale Progress

While your sale is active, the dashboard provides real-time data tracking its market performance. Your initial creator purchase is automatically included in these statistics:

* **Countdown Timer:** A ticking clock showing exactly how much time is left before the sale expires.
* **Sale Status:** The current state of your bonding curve (e.g., Live).
* **Raised Funds:** The total amount of SOL collected so far, along with its current USD market value.
* **Progress Bar:** The percentage of the hard cap that has been filled.
* **Sold Tokens:** The total number of tokens purchased by participants so far.
* **Unique Buyers:** The total number of individual, unique wallets that have joined the sale.
* **Contributions Feed:** A live ledger showing every individual purchase, including the wallet address, SOL spent, USD value, tokens received, and the transaction time.
* **Top Holders:** A Leaderboard showing the wallets holding the largest percentages of the token supply.

## Understanding Sale Statuses

Your sale moves through a simple lifecycle based on time limits and funding targets. 

* **Live:** The sale is fully active, open to the public, and actively accepting buy orders.
* **Completed:** The sale successfully hit its hard cap before time ran out and is now ready to transition to the exchange.
* **Expired:** The time limit ran out before the hard cap could be reached. The sale is closed, and no liquidity migration will occur.
