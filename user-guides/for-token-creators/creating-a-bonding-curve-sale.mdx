# Creating a Bonding Curve Sale

Creating a bonding curve sale on RugFreePump starts with creating your coin, configuring its metadata, and making an initial purchase during creation.

Once the transaction succeeds, your bonding curve sale becomes **Live** and is available for buyers to discover and participate in.

## Before You Start

Before creating a sale, make sure you have:

- A supported wallet — **Phantom** or **Solflare**
- Enough SOL for the creation process
- Your coin name and ticker
- A description if you want to provide one
- Your website and social links if applicable
- An image that meets the upload requirements

### Keep Enough SOL in Your Wallet

Creating a coin requires more SOL than the amount you enter for the initial purchase.

Your wallet may need SOL for:

- The **escrow creation fee**, which is currently **$5 USD worth of SOL**
- Required Solana account creation and rent costs
- Your initial creator purchase
- The Solana network transaction fee

The $5 escrow creation fee is converted from USD to SOL using the SOL/USD price available when the coin is created.

Make sure your wallet retains enough SOL to cover all required costs. If your wallet does not have enough SOL available for the transaction and network fee, the transaction may fail during simulation or execution.

## Open the Create Coin Page

You can start from any RugFreePump page.

Select **Create Coin** from the main header or from the sidebar.

You can also open the creation page directly:

[Create a Coin](https://rugfreepump.com/create)

## Sign In

You can begin entering your coin information before signing in.

However, you must sign in before you can create the coin.

If you are not signed in, you can select **Sign In** from the header.

You can also select **Login to create coin** at the bottom of the creation form when you are ready to continue.

### Connect Your Wallet

RugFreePump currently supports:

- Phantom
- Solflare

When signing in for the first time, your wallet will ask you to approve a connection request.

Approve the connection request in your wallet to continue.

### Sign the Login Message

After connecting, your wallet will request a message signature.

Approve the signature to prove that you control the connected wallet.

This signature is used for authentication and **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

Once authentication is complete, the **Login to create coin** button changes to **Create coin**.

## Complete the Consent Process

Before creating a bonding curve sale, you must complete the RugFreePump consent process.

RugFreePump allows users to explore the platform without completing consent. However, consent is required before performing financial operations such as creating a coin.

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays the required documents in a consent card.

You must **carefully read all three documents** before continuing:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Risk Disclosure

Use the document links provided at the bottom of the consent card to read the documents. These links take you directly to the documents you are being asked to agree to.

After carefully reviewing all three documents, select **Continue**.

Your wallet will then ask you to sign the consent message. Approve the signature to confirm your agreement and continue with the creation process.

This signature **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

You only need to complete the consent process once for the current versions of the required documents. If a new version of any of the three documents is published, you will be asked to review and sign the updated consent again before performing financial operations.

For a detailed explanation of the consent process, see [**RugFreePump Consent**](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).

## Enter Coin Details

Enter the information for your new coin.

### Coin Name

Enter the name of your coin.

Make sure the name is correct before continuing.

### Ticker

Enter the coin ticker, such as `DOGE`.

### Description

Add a short description of your coin.

This field is optional.

### Social Links

You can optionally add links for:

- Website
- Telegram

Enter the appropriate URL for each link you want to associate with your coin.

### Upload an Image

Select an image for your coin or drag and drop it into the upload area.

The current image requirements are:

- **Maximum file size:** 20 MB
- **Supported formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF
- **Minimum resolution:** 1024 × 1024 pixels
- **Recommended aspect ratio:** Square (1:1)

### Metadata Is Permanent

Review your information carefully before creating the coin.

The coin's:

- Name
- Ticker
- Description
- Image
- Social links

are set when the coin is created and cannot be changed afterward.

## Creator Incentive

When the Creator Incentive Program is active, the creation page displays information about the incentive available to eligible creators.

The current incentive rate is **1%**.

The program can be enabled or disabled by the protocol, and the configured rate can be changed for future creations.

Your incentive eligibility and rate are determined when your coin is created. If the program is active when you create your coin, that incentive remains associated with your sale even if the global program is later disabled or its rate is changed.

The incentive is paid when the sale successfully reaches its hard cap and liquidity migration is successfully completed.

## Sale Lifetime

Every bonding curve sale has a fixed lifetime determined by the protocol when the sale is created.

The protocol currently allows a sale lifetime between **24 and 48 hours**. The lifetime is configured in seconds, so the protocol can use any duration within this range rather than being limited to whole-hour values.

For example, the protocol could use a lifetime of 24 hours, 25.1 hours, 36 hours, or another duration within the allowed range.

The lifetime is determined when the sale is created and is fixed for that individual sale.

If the protocol lifetime setting is changed later, the change applies only to newly created sales. **Existing and currently running sales are not affected.**

If a sale reaches its hard cap before its lifetime expires, it becomes **Completed** and can proceed to liquidity migration.

If the lifetime expires before the hard cap is reached, the sale becomes **Expired** and does not proceed to liquidity migration.

## Choose Your Initial Purchase

After entering your coin information and signing in, select **Create coin**.

A creator purchase panel will open.

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use for your initial purchase.

Your wallet balance is displayed in the panel, along with:

- The SOL amount
- **Reset**
- **Max**
- The estimated number of tokens you will receive

### Initial Purchase

Your initial purchase is the first purchase made in your newly created bonding curve sale.

The amount you enter is the **purchase amount**, not the net amount that is ultimately counted toward the bonding curve.

RugFreePump currently applies a **1% buy fee** to purchases. This fee is deducted by the on-chain program, and the remaining amount is used for the bonding curve purchase.

For example, if you enter `10 SOL`, the 1% buy fee is deducted from that purchase and the remaining `9.9 SOL` is used as the net bonding-curve contribution.

The number of tokens you receive is calculated from the bonding curve using the applicable SOL/USD price at the time of the transaction.

### Reset

Select **Reset** to clear the entered purchase amount.

### Max

Select **Max** to automatically use the maximum available purchase amount.

If your wallet balance is greater than the sale's remaining hard-cap capacity, **Max** uses the maximum amount permitted by the sale rather than your entire wallet balance.

You should still keep enough SOL in your wallet to cover the creation costs and Solana network fee.

## Create the Coin

Once you enter a valid initial purchase amount, the **Create coin** button becomes available.

Select **Create coin** to begin the creation process.

If you have not completed the required consent process, RugFreePump will ask you to complete it before continuing.

After all requirements are complete, RugFreePump will first upload and prepare the coin metadata and transaction.

You will see a status such as:

> Uploading Metadata & Preparing The Transaction...

Keep the creation window open while this process is running.

## Approve the Transaction

After the metadata and transaction are prepared, your wallet will request transaction approval.

The creation window will show:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if everything is correct.

Do not close or reload the creation window while the process is running.

## Wait for Confirmation

After approving the transaction, RugFreePump waits for the transaction to be confirmed on Solana.

The creation window will show:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Once the transaction succeeds, RugFreePump begins processing the resulting transaction data so the new sale can be displayed.

You may briefly see:

> Transaction Succeeded. Waiting For Transaction data To Map The Sale Page...

You do not need to take any additional action during this step.

RugFreePump will automatically redirect you to your newly created sale.

## If You Cancel the Transaction

If you reject the wallet request or the transaction times out, the creation does not complete.

RugFreePump cleans up the metadata that was prepared for the attempted creation.

During this cleanup process, the creation window remains locked and displays:

> IPFS Metadata Cleanup Is In Progress...

Keep the window open until the cleanup finishes.

After cleanup is complete, you can:

- Try creating the coin again
- Change your coin information
- Change your initial purchase amount
- Leave the creation page

No sale is created when the creation transaction is canceled or times out.

## Your Sale Is Live

After a successful creation transaction, you are automatically redirected to the new sale page.

The newly created bonding curve sale starts in the **Live** state and is immediately available for buyers to discover and participate in.

### Bonding Curve Pool Information

The sale page shows the main information about your bonding curve sale, including:

- **Total Supply** — the total supply of the coin
- **Tokens For Sale** — the portion of the supply available through the bonding curve
- **Tokens For Liquidity** — the portion reserved for liquidity after a successful migration
- **Hard Cap** — the maximum amount the sale can raise
- **Started Time** — when the sale became Live
- **End Time** — when the sale lifetime expires
- **Listing On** — the DEX where liquidity will be created after a successful migration
- **LP Tokens** — the treatment of liquidity-provider tokens after migration

### Sale Progress

While the sale is Live, the sale page displays its current progress, including:

- **Raised SOL**
- **Raised USD**
- **Sale Progress**
- **Tokens Sold**
- **Total Buyers**
- Remaining time until the sale lifetime expires

Your initial purchase is included in these figures after the sale is created.

The contributions section also records purchases made during the sale, including the contributor, SOL amount, USD value, tokens received, time, and transaction.

### Sale Status

The sale progresses according to its conditions:

- **Live** — the sale is active and accepting purchases.
- **Completed** — the sale has reached its hard cap.
- **Expired** — the sale lifetime has ended before the hard cap was reached.

A sale that becomes **Completed** can proceed to liquidity migration.

A sale that becomes **Expired** does **not** proceed to liquidity migration. Eligible contributors can instead use the withdrawal process described in the buyer guide.

The normal lifecycle before and after the sale is therefore:

**Live → Completed → Migrated**

or:

**Live → Expired**

Only a **Completed** sale proceeds to the migration process.
