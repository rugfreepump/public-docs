RugFreePump allows anyone with a compatible wallet to participate in an active bonding curve sale by purchasing its tokens.

Purchases are made directly from the sale page using the **Buy** button.

When you purchase tokens, the applicable **Buy Fee** is applied to the purchase, and the remaining amount is used for the bonding curve contribution.

# Before You Buy

Before purchasing tokens, make sure:

- Your compatible Solana wallet is connected to RugFreePump.
- You have enough SOL in your wallet for the purchase.
- You have enough additional SOL to cover the Solana network transaction fee and selected priority fee.
- The bonding curve sale is still active and has not reached its hard cap.

The amount of SOL required depends on the purchase amount, the applicable **Buy Fee**, the selected priority fee, and the current SOL/USD price used by the sale.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee** and its authorized update range.

# Anyone Can Participate

Buying is not restricted to the wallet that created the sale.

Any user with a compatible wallet can participate in an active bonding curve sale, subject to the sale's purchase rules and available remaining capacity.

The creator does not receive special purchasing access through the normal buy process.

# Transaction Settings

Before submitting a purchase, you can configure the transaction settings used for your purchase.

## Set Max Slippage

Slippage defines the maximum price movement you are willing to accept when your transaction is executed.

The available slippage setting can be configured from **0% to 50%**.

A **0% slippage** setting means your purchase will only execute at the exact expected price and is generally not recommended.

If the price moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance before the transaction executes, the transaction will fail rather than allowing execution beyond your selected tolerance.

A higher slippage tolerance allows a greater difference between the expected and actual execution price, while a lower tolerance provides stricter protection against unfavorable execution.

## Set Transaction Priority Fee

The priority fee controls the fixed additional transaction fee paid to the Solana network to prioritize your transaction.

RugFreePump provides three fixed priority fee options:

- **Fast — 0.000020 SOL**
- **Turbo — 0.000100 SOL**
- **Ultra — 0.000200 SOL**

These priority fee settings are fixed tiers and are **not dynamically adjusted based on network conditions or network health**.

Higher priority fee settings are intended to give your transaction higher processing priority relative to transactions using lower priority fees.

The priority fee is a Solana network transaction fee and is separate from the RugFreePump **Buy Fee**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Priority Fee Notice">
If a transaction is processed by the Solana network but ultimately fails, the selected priority fee may still be paid to the network.

For example, this can occur when the transaction has insufficient funds or encounters an execution error after processing.
</Callout>

# Buy a Bonding Curve Sale Token

Once you have found an active bonding curve sale, you can purchase its tokens directly from the sale page.

## Step 1: Enter Your Purchase Amount

On the sale page, enter the amount of SOL you want to use for the purchase.

The purchase amount represents the total amount of SOL you are submitting for the transaction.

RugFreePump applies the applicable **Buy Fee** to each purchase. The Buy Fee is deducted from the purchase amount, and the remaining amount is used as the bonding curve contribution.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current Buy Fee.

The amount of tokens you receive is calculated from the amount that remains after the applicable Buy Fee.

## Step 2: Configure Transaction Settings

Before submitting your purchase, review your transaction settings.

You can configure:

- **Max Slippage** — the maximum price movement you are willing to accept during execution.
- **Transaction Priority Fee** — the fixed Solana network priority fee tier used for the transaction.

Make sure the selected settings match your intended transaction behavior.

## Step 3: Select Buy

After entering your purchase amount and reviewing your transaction settings, select the **Buy** button.

The purchase process begins, and the transaction window displays:

> Preparing The Transaction...

RugFreePump prepares the transaction required to complete your purchase.

Keep the transaction window open while the transaction is being prepared.

## Step 4: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet will request approval.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it to continue.

Make sure the transaction details shown by your wallet are consistent with the purchase you intended to make.

## Step 5: Wait for Transaction Confirmation

After approving the transaction, RugFreePump waits for confirmation from the Solana network.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Keep the transaction window open while the transaction is being confirmed.

Once the transaction succeeds, your purchase is complete.

# How Your Purchase Works

Each purchase consists of the amount you enter and the applicable Buy Fee.

The **Buy Fee** is deducted from the purchase amount before the remaining SOL is applied to the bonding curve.

The bonding curve then determines the number of tokens received based on the applicable contribution amount and the current state of the sale.

Your selected slippage setting determines how much price movement is acceptable for the transaction to execute.

You do not need to calculate the bonding curve manually.

RugFreePump calculates the applicable token amount as part of the purchase transaction.

# Purchase Limits

Each bonding curve sale has limits on how much can be purchased based on the remaining capacity of the sale.

Your purchase must also satisfy the minimum purchase requirement displayed by the sale.

If the amount you enter exceeds the available remaining capacity, the purchase amount must be reduced before the transaction can proceed.

The available purchase amount can change as other users participate in the sale.

# SOL Balance Requirements

Make sure your wallet contains enough SOL to cover:

- Your purchase amount.
- The applicable **Buy Fee**.
- The Solana network transaction fee.
- Your selected transaction priority fee.

You should keep some additional SOL available in your wallet rather than using your entire balance for a purchase.

The Solana network transaction fee and priority fee are separate from the RugFreePump **Buy Fee**.

# What Happens After a Successful Purchase?

After the purchase transaction is successfully confirmed:

- The bonding curve sale records your contribution.
- The corresponding token amount is transferred to your wallet.
- The sale's progress is updated.
- Your contribution becomes part of the funds raised by the sale.

The purchase is recorded on-chain as part of the bonding curve sale.

# When the Sale Reaches Its Hard Cap

A bonding curve sale can reach its hard cap as users continue purchasing tokens.

When the hard cap is reached:

- The sale status changes to **Completed**.
- The bonding curve sale is no longer available for additional purchases.
- The creator can proceed with liquidity migration to **Raydium CPMM**.

The creator must then complete the liquidity migration process before the token becomes available for public trading through the migrated Raydium pool.

# Buying During an Active Sale

While the sale remains active, users can continue participating through the **Buy** button, provided their purchases satisfy the sale's current limits.

The sale's available capacity can change quickly when multiple users are purchasing.

For this reason, the amount available when you begin preparing a transaction may differ from the amount available when the transaction is processed.

# Transaction States

During a purchase, RugFreePump displays the current transaction state.

### Preparing The Transaction...

RugFreePump is preparing the transaction required to complete your purchase.

### Waiting For Transaction Approval...

The transaction has been prepared and your wallet is requesting approval.

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if the details are correct.

### Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Your transaction has been submitted and RugFreePump is waiting for confirmation from the Solana network.

Keep the transaction window open until confirmation is complete.

Once the transaction succeeds, the purchase is complete.

# If Your Purchase Fails

A purchase can fail for several reasons, including:

- Insufficient SOL balance.
- Insufficient SOL remaining for the network transaction fee or selected priority fee.
- The sale reaching its hard cap before your transaction is processed.
- The requested purchase amount no longer being available.
- The price moving beyond your selected slippage tolerance.
- A wallet connection problem.
- A transaction preparation or execution problem.
- A temporary Solana network or transaction issue.
- Another protocol validation failure.

If a purchase fails, review the transaction message shown by RugFreePump and your wallet.

If the problem persists, make sure your wallet is connected correctly, that you have enough SOL available, and that your selected transaction settings are appropriate before trying again.

# Purchase and Sale Risks

Purchasing tokens through a bonding curve sale involves financial and market risk.

The token price can change as other users participate in the sale, and the token may have little or no secondary-market liquidity before migration.

A successful purchase does not guarantee that the token will increase in value or that you will be able to sell it later at a profit.

Review the project's information carefully before contributing SOL.

Do not rely solely on a project's name, logo, description, social media presence, or creator claims when deciding whether to participate.

For general transaction and project safety guidance, see [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

# After the Sale Is Completed

Once a sale reaches its hard cap, the sale status changes to **Completed**.

At this point, purchasing through the bonding curve is no longer available.

The creator is then responsible for migrating the raised liquidity to **Raydium CPMM**.

After successful migration, the sale status changes to **Migrated**, and the migrated liquidity becomes available for public trading on Raydium.

# Buying Summary

The normal buying process is:

1. Open an active bonding curve sale.
2. Connect your compatible Solana wallet.
3. Enter the amount of SOL you want to use for the purchase.
4. Configure your maximum slippage and transaction priority fee.
5. Review the applicable purchase amount and available sale capacity.
6. Select **Buy**.
7. Wait while RugFreePump prepares the transaction.
8. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.
9. Wait for Solana transaction confirmation.
10. Receive the purchased tokens in your wallet after the transaction succeeds.
11. Continue participating while the sale remains active and has available capacity.

Every purchase is subject to the applicable **Buy Fee**, selected slippage tolerance, and selected transaction priority fee.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current Buy Fee and other RugFreePump protocol fees.

Once the sale reaches its hard cap, the bonding curve sale becomes **Completed** and no further purchases can be made through the bonding curve.
