RugFreePump allows anyone with a compatible Solana wallet to participate in an active bonding curve sale.

You can purchase tokens directly from the sale page using the **Buy** panel.

The panel displays your wallet balance, purchase amount, **Max Slippage**, **Reset**, **Max**, and the estimated number of tokens you will receive.

# Before You Buy

Before purchasing, make sure:

- Your compatible Solana wallet is connected.
- You have enough SOL available for the purchase and applicable transaction costs.
- The sale is still **Live** and has available purchase capacity.
- You have completed the required RugFreePump consent process.

RugFreePump currently supports:

- [Phantom](https://phantom.com)
- [Solflare](https://solflare.com/)

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee** and its authorized update range.

# Complete the Consent Process

RugFreePump allows users to explore the platform without completing consent. However, consent is required before performing financial operations such as buying tokens.

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays a consent card containing:

1. **Terms of Service**
2. **Privacy Policy**
3. **Risk Disclosure**

## Consent Steps

To complete the consent process:

1. **Review the Documents:** Use the links provided at the bottom of the consent card to read all three documents.
2. **Continue:** After reviewing the documents, select **Continue**.
3. **Sign the Consent Message:** Your wallet will ask you to sign a consent confirmation message.

The consent signature **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

<Callout type="info" title="Policy Updates">
Your consent remains valid for the current versions of the required documents.

If RugFreePump publishes a new version of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or Risk Disclosure, you may be asked to review and sign the updated documents before performing another financial operation.

For more information, see the [RugFreePump Consent Documentation](/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).
</Callout>

# Understanding a Bonding Curve Sale

A bonding curve sale starts in the **Live** state and accepts purchases from participants.

Each sale has a fixed lifetime determined when the sale is created. The protocol currently allows a sale lifetime between **24 and 48 hours**.

The sale page displays information that helps you understand the current state of the sale, including:

- **Countdown Timer:** The remaining time until the sale lifetime expires.
- **Sale Status:** The current state of the sale.
- **Raised SOL:** The amount of SOL raised by the sale.
- **Raised USD:** The USD value associated with the raised amount.
- **Sale Progress:** The percentage of the hard cap reached.
- **Tokens Sold:** The number of tokens purchased through the sale.
- **Total Buyers:** The number of unique buyers who have participated in the sale.
- **Contributions:** Purchase activity associated with the sale.
- **Top Holders:** Wallets holding the largest portions of the token supply.

The sale's available purchase capacity can change as other users participate.

# Sale States and Outcomes

The sale progresses according to its funding, lifetime, and liquidity migration status.

## Live

**Live** means the bonding curve sale is active and accepting purchases.

You can purchase tokens while the sale remains Live and has available capacity.

The sale remains Live until it reaches its hard cap or its lifetime expires.

## Completed

**Completed** means the bonding curve has reached its hard cap.

Once the sale becomes Completed:

- No further bonding curve purchases can be made.
- The creator can proceed with liquidity migration to **Raydium CPMM**.
- The sale page displays that the pool has completed.
- A migration countdown is shown for the creator's migration window.

### Liquidity Migration Pending

After a sale becomes Completed, the creator is given a **60-minute window** to migrate the raised liquidity.

During this period, it is normal for the liquidity to remain unmigrated.

The sale page may display:

> ⚠️ Liquidity Migration Pending

The page also displays the remaining migration time.

The 60-minute window gives the creator time to complete the migration and prepare the project for public trading on Raydium.

The countdown itself does **not** automatically migrate or cancel the sale when it reaches zero.

If the timer expires and the liquidity has not been migrated, you should not assume that the sale has been canceled or that your contribution has been lost.

If you experience delays or have concerns about an unmigrated completed sale, contact RugFreePump support so the situation can be reviewed.

## Migrated

**Migrated** means the completed bonding curve sale has successfully migrated its liquidity to **Raydium CPMM**.

After successful migration:

- The bonding curve sale no longer accepts purchases.
- The migrated liquidity becomes available for public trading.
- The sale page provides access to view the project's market information.

A Migrated sale is not eligible for bonding-curve contribution withdrawal because the sale has successfully proceeded to liquidity migration.

## Expired

**Expired** means the sale lifetime ended before the bonding curve reached its hard cap.

An Expired sale does not proceed to liquidity migration.

Eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL through the applicable withdrawal process.

See [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) for the withdrawal process and requirements.

## Canceled

**Canceled** is an exceptional sale outcome that may occur when the platform authority determines that cancellation is necessary.

A canceled sale does not proceed to liquidity migration.

Eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL through the applicable withdrawal process.

See [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) for the withdrawal process and requirements.

# Buy Settings

Before submitting a purchase, you can configure the purchase amount, maximum slippage, and transaction priority.

## Purchase Amount

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use for the purchase.

The amount you enter is the total purchase amount submitted for the transaction.

The applicable **Buy Fee** is deducted from the purchase amount, and the remaining amount is used as the bonding curve contribution.

The number of tokens you receive is calculated from the applicable contribution and the current state of the bonding curve.

## Reset

Select **Reset** to clear the entered purchase amount.

## Max

Select **Max** to automatically set the highest purchase amount currently available to you.

The maximum is determined by both your wallet balance and the current maximum amount permitted by the sale:

- If the current maximum purchase amount is greater than your wallet balance, **Max** sets the purchase amount to your available wallet balance.
- If your wallet balance is greater than the current maximum purchase amount, **Max** sets the purchase amount to the exact current maximum permitted by the sale.

The maximum amount can change as the sale progresses or your wallet balance changes.

You should still keep enough SOL available for applicable Solana network fees and your selected priority fee.

## Max Slippage

**Max Slippage** defines the maximum price movement you are willing to accept when the purchase is executed.

The available setting can be configured from **0% to 50%**.

A **0%** slippage setting requires execution at the exact expected price and is generally not recommended.

If the price moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance before execution, the transaction fails instead of allowing execution beyond your selected limit.

A higher slippage setting allows more price movement, while a lower setting provides stricter protection against unfavorable execution.

## Transaction Priority

The transaction priority setting controls the fixed additional priority fee paid to the Solana network.

RugFreePump provides three fixed options:

- **Fast — 0.000020 SOL**
- **Turbo — 0.000100 SOL**
- **Ultra — 0.000200 SOL**

These are fixed priority tiers and are **not dynamically adjusted based on network conditions or network health**.

Higher tiers are intended to give the transaction higher processing priority relative to transactions using lower priority fees.

The priority fee is a **Solana network transaction fee** and is separate from the RugFreePump **Buy Fee**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Priority Fee Notice">
If a transaction is processed by the Solana network but ultimately fails, the selected priority fee may still be paid to the network.
</Callout>

# Buy Tokens

Once the sale is **Live** and has available capacity, you can purchase its tokens.

## Step 1: Enter Your Purchase Amount

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use.

The Buy panel displays:

- Your wallet balance
- Purchase amount
- **Max Slippage**
- **Reset**
- **Max**
- Estimated tokens you will receive

Review the displayed purchase amount and estimated token quantity before continuing.

## Step 2: Review Your Settings

Review your:

- Purchase amount
- **Max Slippage**
- **Transaction Priority**

Make sure your wallet contains enough SOL for the purchase and applicable Solana network fees.

## Step 3: Select Buy

Select **Buy** to begin the purchase.

The transaction window displays:

> Preparing The Transaction...

RugFreePump prepares the transaction required to complete your purchase.

Keep the transaction window open while the transaction is being prepared.

## Step 4: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet requests approval.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if the details are correct.

## Step 5: Wait for Confirmation

After approval, RugFreePump waits for confirmation from the Solana network.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Keep the transaction window open until confirmation is complete.

Once the transaction succeeds, your purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet.

# How Your Purchase Works

The amount you enter is the purchase amount.

The applicable **Buy Fee** is deducted from the purchase amount, and the remaining SOL becomes the bonding curve contribution.

The bonding curve determines the token amount based on the applicable contribution and the current sale state.

You do not need to calculate the bonding curve manually.

Your selected slippage tolerance is checked when the purchase is executed.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee**.

# Purchase Limits

Each sale has a maximum remaining capacity.

Your purchase must satisfy the sale's current purchase limits and minimum purchase requirement.

The available maximum can change while you are preparing a transaction because other users may purchase tokens at the same time.

If the available capacity changes before your transaction is processed, your transaction may fail rather than execute beyond the permitted sale capacity.

# SOL Balance Requirements

Your wallet should contain enough SOL to cover:

- The purchase amount.
- The applicable **Buy Fee**.
- The Solana network transaction fee.
- The selected transaction priority fee.

The **Buy Fee** is a RugFreePump protocol fee.

The standard Solana transaction fee and selected priority fee are network fees and are separate from the Buy Fee.

# If Your Purchase Fails

A purchase may fail if, for example:

- Your wallet does not have enough SOL.
- The sale reaches its hard cap before your transaction is processed.
- The requested purchase amount is no longer available.
- The price moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance.
- Your wallet connection fails.
- The transaction cannot be prepared or executed.
- Another protocol validation fails.
- A Solana network or transaction issue occurs.

If a purchase fails, review the message shown by RugFreePump and your wallet.

If you try again, check your wallet balance, available sale capacity, purchase amount, and transaction settings.

# Purchase Risks

Purchasing tokens through a bonding curve sale involves financial and market risk.

Token prices can change as other users participate in the sale, and a token may have little or no secondary-market liquidity before migration.

A successful purchase does not guarantee that the token will increase in value or that you will later be able to sell it at a profit.

Review project information carefully before contributing SOL.

Do not rely solely on a project's name, ticker, image, description, social links, or creator claims when deciding whether to participate.

For general safety guidance, see [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

# Buying Summary

The normal buying process is:

1. Open an active **Live** bonding curve sale.
2. Connect your compatible Solana wallet.
3. Complete the required consent process if prompted.
4. Review the sale's status, progress, remaining capacity, and lifetime.
5. Enter the amount of SOL you want to use.
6. Use **Reset** or **Max** as needed.
7. Select your **Max Slippage**.
8. Select your **Transaction Priority**.
9. Review the estimated tokens you will receive.
10. Select **Buy**.
11. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.
12. Wait for Solana transaction confirmation.
13. Receive the purchased tokens in your wallet after the transaction succeeds.

If the sale later becomes **Completed**, the creator receives a 60-minute migration window. If the sale becomes **Migrated**, the liquidity is available for public trading on Raydium. If the sale becomes **Expired** or **Canceled**, eligible contributors can use the withdrawal process.

See [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) for information about withdrawing contributed SOL.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee** and other RugFreePump protocol fees.
