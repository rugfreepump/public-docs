Buying tokens on RugFreePump allows you to participate in an active bonding curve sale using SOL.

Before buying, review the sale information, understand how the purchase works, and make sure your wallet has enough SOL to cover the purchase and associated transaction costs.

Once your purchase transaction succeeds, the purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet and your contribution becomes part of the sale's raised funds.

# Before You Start

Before buying tokens, make sure you understand the sale you are participating in and have enough SOL in your wallet to complete the purchase transaction.

## Review the Sale Carefully

Before purchasing, review the information displayed on the sale page.

Pay particular attention to:

- **Coin Name and Ticker:** Confirm that you are buying the coin you intended to purchase.
- **Pool Address:** Verify the on-chain bonding curve pool associated with the sale.
- **Sale Progress:** Check how much of the hard cap has already been reached.
- **Raised Amount:** Review the current SOL and USD amounts raised.
- **Hard Cap:** Check the maximum amount the sale can raise.
- **Countdown Timer:** Check how much time remains before the sale lifetime expires.
- **Sale Status:** Make sure the sale is currently **Live** before attempting to purchase.
- **Tokens Sold:** Review how much of the bonding curve token allocation has already been purchased.
- **Total Buyers:** Review the number of wallets that have participated in the sale.

The sale page also provides contribution and holder information to help you evaluate the activity surrounding the sale.

## Prerequisites Checklist

Before buying tokens, make sure you have the following:

- **Supported Solana Wallet:** An active, funded wallet using [Phantom](https://phantom.com) or [Solflare](https://solflare.com/).
- **Adequate SOL Balance:** Enough SOL to cover your purchase amount, the applicable Buy Fee, Solana network fees, and any selected transaction priority fee.
- **Active Sale:** The bonding curve sale must still be in the **Live** state.
- **Completed Consent:** You must complete the required consent process before performing the purchase.

# Keep Enough SOL in Your Wallet

Buying tokens requires more SOL than the amount that ultimately becomes your net bonding-curve contribution.

Your wallet must have enough SOL to cover the purchase amount and the fees required to process the transaction.

## Total Wallet Balance Breakdown

Before buying, make sure your wallet can cover the following:

### 1. Purchase Amount

The amount you enter in the purchase panel is the amount of SOL you are attempting to spend on the purchase.

The purchase amount is subject to the current limits of the sale and your available wallet balance.

### 2. Buy Fee

A **Buy Fee** is deducted from each bonding curve purchase.

The remaining amount is used as the net bonding-curve contribution for calculating the tokens you receive.

For the current **Buy Fee** and its authorized update range, see [Protocol Fees](/protocol-fees).

### 3. Solana Network Transaction Fees

Your wallet also needs enough SOL to pay the Solana network fees required to process the transaction.

These are separate from RugFreePump protocol fees.

### 4. Transaction Priority Fee

If you select a transaction priority level, an additional Solana priority fee is included with your transaction.

The priority fee is a network fee and is separate from the RugFreePump Buy Fee.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep a SOL Buffer">
Make sure your wallet contains enough SOL to cover your purchase amount, the applicable Buy Fee, Solana network fees, and your selected priority fee.

Keeping a small additional SOL buffer can help avoid transaction failures caused by the total transaction cost exceeding your available balance.
</Callout>

# Open the Sale Page

You can discover bonding curve sales through RugFreePump and open the sale page for the coin you want to purchase.

Each sale has its own dedicated sale page.

The sale page displays the current state of the bonding curve and provides the interface for purchasing tokens.

Before purchasing, confirm that the sale is **Live**.

# Sign In and Connect Your Wallet

You can explore sale pages without connecting your wallet.

However, you must connect and authenticate your wallet before buying tokens.

RugFreePump currently supports:

- [Phantom](https://phantom.com)
- [Solflare](https://solflare.com/)

## Connect Your Wallet

When connecting for the first time, your wallet extension or mobile wallet will request permission to connect.

Approve the connection request to continue.

Connecting allows RugFreePump to identify your public wallet address. It does not give RugFreePump access to your private keys.

## Sign the Login Message

After connecting your wallet, you may be asked to sign a login message.

- **Purpose:** The signature proves that you control the connected wallet.
- **Safety:** The login signature is used for authentication and **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

Once authentication is complete, the purchase controls become available when the sale is eligible for purchases.

# Complete the Consent Process

RugFreePump allows users to explore the platform without completing consent. However, consent is required before performing financial operations such as buying tokens.

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays a consent card containing the required documents:

1. **Terms of Service**
2. **Privacy Policy**
3. **Risk Disclosure**

## Consent Steps

To complete the consent process:

1. **Review the Documents:** Use the links provided at the bottom of the consent card to read all three documents.
2. **Continue:** After reviewing the documents, select **Continue**.
3. **Sign the Consent Message:** Your wallet will ask you to sign a consent confirmation message.

The consent signature **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

<Callout type="info" title="Policy Updates">

If RugFreePump publishes a new version of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or Risk Disclosure, you may be asked to review and sign the updated documents before performing another financial operation.

For more information, see the [RugFreePump Consent Documentation](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).
</Callout>

# Understand the Bonding Curve Sale

RugFreePump bonding curve sales use a pricing curve where the token price changes as the sale progresses.

As more tokens are purchased and the sale raises more funds, the purchase price generally increases.

This means the number of tokens you receive for a given amount of SOL can change as other users purchase tokens.

The purchase calculation uses the applicable SOL/USD price when the transaction is processed.

## Fixed Token Supply

Every RugFreePump bonding curve sale has a total supply of **1 billion tokens**.

The supply is divided between:

- **800 million tokens:** Available through the bonding curve sale.
- **200 million tokens:** Reserved for liquidity after a successful migration.

These allocations are fixed by the protocol and are not configured separately for each sale.

# Review Sale Information

The sale page provides information that helps you understand the current state of the sale before purchasing.

## Bonding Curve Pool Information

The sale page displays information including:

- **Pool Address:** The on-chain account associated with the bonding curve sale and its raised funds.
- **Total Supply:** The total supply of the coin.
- **Tokens For Sale:** The portion of the supply available through the bonding curve.
- **Tokens For Liquidity:** The portion of the supply reserved for liquidity after a successful migration.
- **Hard Cap:** The maximum amount the sale can raise.
- **Start Time:** When the sale became Live.
- **End Time:** When the sale lifetime expires.
- **Listing On:** The DEX where liquidity will be created after a successful migration.
- **LP Tokens:** Information about how liquidity-provider tokens are handled after migration.

## Track Sale Progress

While the sale is Live, the sale page displays its current progress.

The sale information includes:

- **Countdown Timer:** The remaining time until the sale lifetime expires.
- **Sale Status:** The current state of the bonding curve sale.
- **Raised SOL:** The amount of SOL raised by the sale.
- **Raised USD:** The USD value associated with the raised amount.
- **Sale Progress:** The percentage of the hard cap reached.
- **Tokens Sold:** The number of tokens purchased through the sale.
- **Total Buyers:** The number of unique buyers who participated in the sale.
- **Contributions:** Purchases made during the sale, including the contributor, SOL amount, USD value, tokens received, time, and transaction.
- **Top Holders:** Wallets holding the largest portions of the coin's token supply.

# Understanding Sale Statuses

The state of the sale determines whether you can purchase tokens and what happens to your contribution afterward.

## Live

A **Live** sale is active and accepting purchases.

While a sale is Live:

- You can purchase tokens if you meet the purchase requirements.
- The bonding curve continues to determine the token price.
- The sale progress can increase as users purchase tokens.
- The remaining sale lifetime continues to count down.
- The sale can eventually become **Completed** or **Expired**.

## Completed

A **Completed** sale has reached its hard cap before its lifetime expired.

A Completed sale no longer accepts bonding curve purchases.

The sale then enters the liquidity migration stage.

### Liquidity Migration Pending

After the sale becomes Completed, the pool creator has a **60-minute migration window** to initiate liquidity migration.

During this period, it is normal for the liquidity to remain unmigrated.

The timer does **not** automatically migrate the liquidity, expire the sale, cancel the sale, or make your contribution withdrawable.

If the creator completes migration, the sale becomes **Migrated**.

If the timer expires without migration and you experience delays or concerns, contact support so the situation can be reviewed.

## Migrated

A **Migrated** sale has successfully completed liquidity migration to **Raydium CPMM**.

The bonding curve sale is no longer active.

The liquidity pool has been created using the sale's raised funds and the tokens reserved for liquidity.

The liquidity-provider tokens are automatically burned as part of the migration process.

After migration, the tokens are available for public trading through the created Raydium liquidity pool.

Your purchased tokens remain in your wallet.

A Migrated sale does **not** become withdrawable through the bonding curve withdrawal process.

## Expired

An **Expired** sale is a sale whose lifetime ended before reaching its hard cap.

An Expired sale does not proceed to liquidity migration.

If you contributed SOL to an eligible Expired sale, you can use the withdrawal process to recover your eligible contributed SOL.

For more information, see [**Withdrawing Contributed SOL**](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

## Canceled

A **Canceled** sale is an exceptional state used when a sale is canceled through an authorized cancellation mechanism.

Canceled sales do not proceed to liquidity migration.

If you contributed SOL to an eligible Canceled sale, you can use the withdrawal process to recover your eligible contributed SOL.

For more information, see [**Withdrawing Contributed SOL**](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

<Callout type="info" title="Normal Sale Lifecycle">
The normal lifecycle is:

**Live → Completed → Migrated**

A sale can instead become:

**Live → Expired**

Cancellation is an exceptional outcome and is not part of the normal sale lifecycle.
</Callout>

# Configure Your Purchase

Once your wallet is connected and the sale is Live, the purchase panel allows you to configure your transaction.

The panel displays information such as:

- Your current wallet balance
- The SOL amount you want to spend
- **Reset**
- **Max**
- **Max Slippage**
- **Transaction Priority**
- The estimated number of tokens you will receive

# Purchase Amount

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use for the purchase.

The amount you enter represents the purchase amount before the Buy Fee is deducted.

The estimated token amount shown in the purchase panel is based on the current sale price and applicable SOL/USD price.

Because the bonding curve price can change as the sale progresses, the final token amount is determined when the transaction is processed.

## Reset

Select **Reset** to clear the entered purchase amount.

You can then enter a different amount.

## Max

Select **Max** to automatically set the purchase amount to the maximum amount currently permitted.

Max considers both your wallet balance and the current purchase limit imposed by the sale.

- If your wallet balance is lower than the current maximum permitted purchase amount, **Max** uses your available wallet balance.
- If your wallet balance is greater than the current maximum permitted purchase amount, **Max** uses the exact maximum amount currently permitted by the sale.

The maximum permitted amount can change as the sale progresses or your wallet balance changes.

You should still keep enough SOL available for the Buy Fee and Solana network fees.

# Set Max Slippage

**Max Slippage** controls how much the final purchase conditions are allowed to differ from the expected purchase conditions before the transaction is rejected.

RugFreePump allows a slippage setting between **0% and 50%**.

## 0% Slippage

A setting of **0%** requires the transaction to execute at the exact expected purchase conditions.

This provides the strictest protection against price movement but can cause the transaction to fail if the sale changes before the transaction is processed.

## Higher Slippage

A higher slippage setting allows a greater difference between the expected and final purchase conditions.

This can reduce failures caused by price movement, but it also means you accept a wider range of execution conditions.

If the sale moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance, the transaction fails instead of executing outside the limit you specified.

<Callout type="warning" title="Choose Your Slippage Carefully">
Higher slippage does not guarantee a successful transaction.

It only increases the amount of price movement your transaction is willing to accept.
</Callout>

# Select Transaction Priority

RugFreePump provides three fixed transaction priority options.

| Priority | Priority Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| **Fast** | **0.000020 SOL** |
| **Turbo** | **0.000100 SOL** |
| **Ultra** | **0.000200 SOL** |

Higher priority levels are intended to give the transaction greater processing priority over transactions using lower priority levels.

These priority tiers are fixed and are not dynamically adjusted based on network conditions.

The priority fee is a **Solana network fee**, not a RugFreePump protocol fee.

<Callout type="warning" title="Failed Transactions">
If a transaction is processed by the network but ultimately fails, the selected priority fee may still be charged by the network.
</Callout>

# Buy Tokens

After configuring your purchase amount, slippage, and transaction priority, select **Buy** to begin the purchase.

The purchase process proceeds through several stages.

## Step 1: Prepare the Transaction

After selecting **Buy**, RugFreePump prepares the purchase transaction using your selected purchase settings.

You will see a status such as:

> Preparing The Transaction...

Keep the sale page open while the transaction is being prepared.

## Step 2: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet will request transaction approval.

The sale page will show:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if everything is correct.

Do not approve a transaction you do not understand or did not intentionally initiate.

## Step 3: Wait for Transaction Confirmation

After approving the transaction, RugFreePump waits for the transaction to be confirmed on Solana.

The sale page will show:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Keep the page open while confirmation is in progress.

## Step 4: Purchase Completed

Once the transaction is successfully confirmed, your purchase is complete.

The purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet.

Your contribution is reflected in the sale's raised amount and contribution records.

# If You Cancel the Transaction

If you reject the wallet request, the transaction does not complete.

Your intended purchase is not executed.

If the transaction fails during simulation or execution, the purchase may also not complete.

Depending on when the transaction was processed, Solana network fees or selected priority fees may still be charged even when the purchase itself does not succeed.

If the transaction fails, review the displayed error and make sure:

- Your wallet has enough SOL.
- The sale is still **Live**.
- Your purchase amount is within the current permitted limits.
- Your selected slippage is appropriate for the current sale conditions.
- Your transaction settings are correct.

You can then try again if the sale is still eligible for purchases.

# After Your Purchase

After a successful purchase, the purchased tokens belong to your connected wallet.

The sale page updates to reflect your contribution and the new sale progress.

Your purchase may affect:

- The current bonding curve price
- The remaining amount available before the hard cap
- The sale progress
- The total tokens sold
- The total number of buyers
- The contribution history

The token amount you receive is determined by the bonding curve and the applicable purchase conditions when the transaction is processed.

# What Happens When the Sale Completes

A sale becomes **Completed** when it reaches its hard cap.

At this point:

- New bonding curve purchases are no longer accepted.
- The sale enters the liquidity migration stage.
- The creator has a 60-minute migration window.
- Your purchased tokens remain in your wallet.
- Your contribution is not automatically withdrawn.

During the migration window, it is normal for the sale page to display:

> This pool has completed

along with:

> Liquidity Migration Pending

The migration timer is only a reminder of the creator's migration window. It does not automatically change the sale's state when it expires.

## After Successful Migration

When liquidity migration succeeds:

- The sale becomes **Migrated**.
- Liquidity is created on **Raydium CPMM**.
- The tokens reserved for liquidity are added to the liquidity pool.
- Liquidity-provider tokens are automatically burned.
- The purchased tokens in your wallet remain yours.
- The token becomes available for public trading through the migrated liquidity pool.

# What Happens If the Sale Expires

If the sale lifetime expires before the hard cap is reached:

- The sale becomes **Expired**.
- New bonding curve purchases are no longer accepted.
- The sale does not proceed to liquidity migration.
- Eligible contributors can withdraw their eligible contributed SOL.

Your purchased tokens and withdrawal eligibility are handled according to the protocol's on-chain rules.

For the withdrawal process, see [**Withdrawing Contributed SOL**](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

# What Happens If a Sale Is Canceled

A sale can be canceled only through an authorized exceptional cancellation mechanism.

If a sale becomes **Canceled**:

- New bonding curve purchases are no longer accepted.
- The sale does not proceed to liquidity migration.
- Eligible contributors can withdraw their eligible contributed SOL.

For the withdrawal process, see [**Withdrawing Contributed SOL**](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

# Buying Summary

The complete buying process is:

1. **Review the Sale:** Confirm the coin, pool, sale progress, hard cap, countdown, and current status.
2. **Connect Your Wallet:** Connect Phantom or Solflare and authenticate your wallet.
3. **Complete Consent:** Review and sign the required consent documents.
4. **Check Your SOL:** Make sure you have enough SOL for the purchase and associated fees.
5. **Set Your Purchase:** Enter the SOL amount you want to spend.
6. **Set Max Slippage:** Choose the amount of price movement you are willing to accept.
7. **Select Priority:** Choose Fast, Turbo, or Ultra transaction priority if desired.
8. **Buy:** Select **Buy** and approve the transaction in your wallet.
9. **Confirm:** Wait for the transaction to be confirmed on Solana.
10. **Receive Tokens:** After successful confirmation, the purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet.
11. **Follow the Sale Outcome:** If the sale becomes Completed, wait for liquidity migration. If it becomes Migrated, the liquidity is available on Raydium. If it becomes Expired or Canceled, eligible contributors can use the withdrawal process.

# What's Next?

After purchasing, the outcome depends on how the sale progresses.

If the sale reaches its hard cap, it becomes **Completed** and proceeds to the liquidity migration stage.

If liquidity migration succeeds, the sale becomes **Migrated** and the liquidity becomes available for public trading on Raydium.

If the sale expires before reaching its hard cap, or is canceled through an exceptional mechanism, eligible contributors can use the withdrawal process.
