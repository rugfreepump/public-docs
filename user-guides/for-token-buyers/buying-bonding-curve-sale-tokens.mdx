RugFreePump allows anyone with a compatible Solana wallet to participate in an active bonding curve sale.

You can purchase tokens directly from the sale page using the **Buy** panel.

The panel shows your wallet balance, purchase amount, slippage setting, **Reset**, **Max**, and the estimated number of tokens you will receive.

# Before You Buy

Before purchasing, make sure:

- Your compatible Solana wallet is connected.
- You have enough SOL available for the purchase and applicable transaction costs.
- The sale is still **Live** and has available purchase capacity.
- You have completed the required RugFreePump consent process.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee** and its authorized update range.

# Required Consent

RugFreePump allows users to explore the platform without completing consent. Consent is required before performing financial operations such as buying tokens.

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays a consent card containing:

1. **Terms of Service**
2. **Privacy Policy**
3. **Risk Disclosure**

## Complete Consent

To complete the consent process:

1. Review all three documents using the links provided in the consent card.
2. Select **Continue**.
3. Sign the consent confirmation message in your wallet.

The consent signature **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

<Callout type="info" title="Policy Updates">
Your consent remains valid for the current versions of the required documents.

If RugFreePump publishes a new version of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or Risk Disclosure, you may be asked to review and sign the updated documents before performing another financial operation.

For more information, see the [RugFreePump Consent Documentation](/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).
</Callout>

# Buy Settings

Before submitting a purchase, you can configure the purchase amount, maximum slippage, and transaction priority.

## Purchase Amount

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use for the purchase.

The amount you enter is the total purchase amount submitted for the transaction. The applicable **Buy Fee** is deducted from this amount, and the remaining amount is used as the bonding curve contribution.

The number of tokens you receive is calculated from the applicable contribution amount and the current state of the bonding curve.

## Reset

Select **Reset** to clear the entered purchase amount.

## Max

Select **Max** to automatically set the highest purchase amount currently available to you.

The Max button considers both your wallet balance and the maximum amount currently permitted by the sale:

- If the current maximum purchase amount is greater than your available wallet balance, **Max** sets the purchase amount to your available wallet balance.
- If your wallet balance is greater than the current maximum purchase amount, **Max** sets the purchase amount to the exact current maximum permitted amount.

The maximum purchase amount can change as the sale progresses or as your wallet balance changes.

## Max Slippage

**Max Slippage** defines the maximum price movement you are willing to accept when the purchase is executed.

The available setting can be configured from **0% to 50%**.

A **0%** slippage setting requires execution at the exact expected price and is generally not recommended.

If the price moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance before execution, the transaction fails instead of allowing execution beyond your selected limit.

A higher slippage setting allows more price movement, while a lower setting provides stricter protection against unfavorable execution.

## Transaction Priority

The transaction priority setting controls the fixed additional priority fee paid to the Solana network.

RugFreePump provides three fixed options:

- **Fast — 0.000020 SOL**
- **Turbo — 0.000100 SOL**
- **Ultra — 0.000200 SOL**

These are fixed priority tiers and are **not dynamically adjusted based on network conditions or network health**.

Higher tiers are intended to give the transaction higher processing priority relative to transactions using lower priority fees.

The priority fee is a **Solana network transaction fee** and is separate from the RugFreePump **Buy Fee**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Priority Fee Notice">
If a transaction is processed by the Solana network but ultimately fails, the selected priority fee may still be paid to the network.
</Callout>

# Buy Tokens

Once the sale is **Live** and has available capacity, you can purchase its tokens.

## Step 1: Enter Your Purchase Amount

Enter the amount of SOL you want to use.

The Buy panel shows:

- Your current SOL balance.
- The purchase amount.
- **Max Slippage**.
- **Reset**.
- **Max**.
- The estimated number of tokens you will receive.

Review the displayed amount and estimated token quantity before continuing.

## Step 2: Review Your Settings

Review:

- Your purchase amount.
- **Max Slippage**.
- **Transaction Priority**.

Make sure your wallet contains enough SOL for the purchase and applicable Solana network fees.

## Step 3: Select Buy

Select the **Buy** button to begin the purchase.

The transaction window displays:

> Preparing The Transaction...

RugFreePump prepares the transaction required to complete your purchase.

Keep the transaction window open while the transaction is being prepared.

## Step 4: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet requests approval.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if the details are correct.

## Step 5: Wait for Confirmation

After approval, RugFreePump waits for confirmation from the Solana network.

The transaction window displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Keep the transaction window open until confirmation is complete.

Once the transaction succeeds, your purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet.

# How Your Purchase Works

The amount you enter is the purchase amount.

The applicable **Buy Fee** is deducted from the purchase amount, and the remaining SOL becomes the bonding curve contribution.

The bonding curve determines the token amount based on the applicable contribution and the current sale state.

You do not need to calculate the bonding curve manually.

The purchase transaction also checks your selected slippage tolerance before allowing the purchase to execute.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee**.

# Purchase Limits

Each sale has a maximum remaining capacity.

Your purchase must satisfy the sale's current purchase limits and minimum purchase requirement.

The available maximum can change while you are preparing a transaction because other users may purchase tokens at the same time.

If the available capacity changes before your transaction is processed, your transaction may fail rather than execute beyond the permitted sale capacity.

# SOL Balance Requirements

Your wallet should contain enough SOL to cover:

- The purchase amount.
- The applicable **Buy Fee**.
- The Solana network transaction fee.
- The selected transaction priority fee.

The **Buy Fee** is a RugFreePump protocol fee.

The standard Solana transaction fee and selected priority fee are network fees and are separate from the Buy Fee.

# When the Sale Reaches Its Hard Cap

A bonding curve sale remains available for purchases while it is **Live** and has remaining capacity.

When the sale reaches its hard cap:

- The sale status changes to **Completed**.
- No further bonding curve purchases can be made.
- The creator can proceed with liquidity migration to **Raydium CPMM**.

After successful migration, the sale becomes **Migrated** and the migrated liquidity becomes available for public trading.

# Transaction States

During a purchase, RugFreePump displays the current transaction state.

### Preparing The Transaction...

RugFreePump is preparing the transaction required to complete your purchase.

### Waiting For Transaction Approval...

The transaction has been prepared and your wallet is requesting approval.

Review the transaction and approve it if the details are correct.

### Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

The transaction has been submitted and RugFreePump is waiting for confirmation from Solana.

Keep the transaction window open until confirmation is complete.

# If Your Purchase Fails

A purchase may fail if, for example:

- Your wallet does not have enough SOL.
- The sale reaches its hard cap before your transaction is processed.
- The requested purchase amount is no longer available.
- The price moves beyond your selected slippage tolerance.
- Your wallet connection fails.
- The transaction cannot be prepared or executed.
- Another protocol validation fails.
- A Solana network or transaction issue occurs.

If a purchase fails, review the message shown by RugFreePump and your wallet.

If you try again, check your wallet balance, available sale capacity, purchase amount, and transaction settings.

# Purchase Risks

Purchasing tokens through a bonding curve sale involves financial and market risk.

Token prices can change as other users participate in the sale, and a token may have little or no secondary-market liquidity before migration.

A successful purchase does not guarantee that the token will increase in value or that you will later be able to sell it at a profit.

Review project information carefully before contributing SOL.

Do not rely solely on a project's name, ticker, image, description, social links, or creator claims when deciding whether to participate.

For general safety guidance, see [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

# Buying Summary

The normal buying process is:

1. Open an active **Live** bonding curve sale.
2. Connect your compatible Solana wallet.
3. Complete the required consent process if prompted.
4. Enter the amount of SOL you want to use.
5. Use **Reset** or **Max** as needed.
6. Select your **Max Slippage**.
7. Select your **Transaction Priority**.
8. Review the estimated tokens you will receive and the available sale capacity.
9. Select **Buy**.
10. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.
11. Wait for Solana transaction confirmation.
12. Receive the purchased tokens in your wallet after the transaction succeeds.

Every purchase is subject to the applicable **Buy Fee**, selected slippage tolerance, sale limits, and selected transaction priority fee.

See [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the current **Buy Fee** and other RugFreePump protocol fees.
