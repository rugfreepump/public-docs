title: "Withdrawing Contributed SOL"
description: "Learn how to withdraw contributed SOL from an Expired or Canceled RugFreePump bonding curve sale."

Withdrawing contributed SOL allows eligible participants to recover their contributed SOL when a RugFreePump bonding curve sale does not proceed to a successful liquidity migration.

Withdrawal is available only when the sale reaches an eligible withdrawal state.

You can withdraw directly from the sale page using the **Withdraw** button.

# Before You Start

Before withdrawing, make sure you understand the current state of the sale and whether your wallet has an eligible contribution available for withdrawal.

## When Can You Withdraw?

Withdrawal is not available while any sale is active.

The availability of withdrawal depends on the final state of the bonding curve sale.

### Live

A **Live** sale is still active and accepting purchases.

You cannot withdraw contributed SOL while the sale is Live.

The sale can eventually become **Completed** or **Expired**.

### Completed

A **Completed** sale has reached its hard cap.

Completed sales do not allow contributors to withdraw their contributed SOL through the bonding curve withdrawal process.

The sale proceeds to the liquidity migration stage.

If liquidity migration succeeds, the sale becomes **Migrated**.

### Migrated

A **Migrated** sale has successfully completed liquidity migration to **Raydium CPMM**.

A Migrated sale does not allow contributed SOL to be withdrawn through the bonding curve withdrawal process.

The sale's raised liquidity has been used for the completed liquidity migration.

### Expired

An **Expired** sale reached the end of its lifetime before reaching its hard cap.

Expired sales do not proceed to liquidity migration.

Eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL.

### Canceled

A **Canceled** sale is an exceptional state resulting from an authorized cancellation mechanism.

Canceled sales do not proceed to liquidity migration.

Eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL.

<Callout type="info" title="Withdrawal Availability">
The normal bonding curve sale lifecycle is:

**Live → Completed → Migrated**

A sale can instead become:

**Live → Expired**

Cancellation is an exceptional outcome and is not part of the normal sale lifecycle.

Withdrawal is available only when the sale reaches an eligible withdrawal state.
</Callout>

# Review the Sale

Before withdrawing, review the information displayed on the sale page.

Pay particular attention to:

- **Coin Name and Ticker:** Confirm that you are viewing the intended coin.
- **Mint Key:** Verify the unique token mint address associated with the coin.
- **Pool Address:** Verify the on-chain bonding curve pool associated with the sale.
- **Sale Status:** Confirm that the sale is in an eligible withdrawal state.
- **Your Contributed Amount:** Review the contribution amount displayed for your connected wallet.
- **Raised SOL:** Review the total SOL raised by the sale.
- **Raised USD:** Review the USD value associated with the raised amount.
- **Sale Progress:** Review the percentage of the hard cap reached.
- **Tokens Sold:** Review the number of tokens purchased through the sale.
- **Contributions:** Review purchases and contributions made during the sale.

The **Mint Key** uniquely identifies the token and can be used to distinguish it from other tokens with similar or identical names and tickers.

# Connect Your Wallet

You can explore sale pages without connecting your wallet.

However, you must connect and authenticate the wallet that made the contribution before withdrawing.

RugFreePump currently supports:

- [Phantom](https://phantom.com)
- [Solflare](https://solflare.com)

## Connect the Contributing Wallet

Connect the same wallet that participated in the bonding curve sale.

RugFreePump uses your connected wallet address to identify your contribution and determine whether you have an eligible amount available for withdrawal.

Connecting allows RugFreePump to identify your public wallet address.

It does not give RugFreePump access to your private keys.

## Sign the Login Message

After connecting your wallet, you may be asked to sign a login message.

- **Purpose:** The signature proves that you control the connected wallet.
- **Safety:** The login signature is used for authentication and **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

# Complete the Consent Process

RugFreePump allows users to explore the platform without completing consent.

However, consent is required before performing financial operations such as withdrawing contributed SOL.

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays a consent card containing:

1. **Terms of Service**
2. **Privacy Policy**
3. **Risk Disclosure**

## Consent Steps

To complete the consent process:

1. Review all three documents using the links provided in the consent card.
2. Select **Continue**.
3. Sign the consent confirmation message in your wallet.

The consent signature **does not transfer SOL or tokens**.

<Callout type="info" title="Policy Updates">
Your consent remains valid for the current versions of the required documents.

If RugFreePump publishes a new version of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or Risk Disclosure, you may be asked to review and sign the updated documents before performing another financial operation.

For more information, see the [RugFreePump Consent Documentation](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).
</Callout>

# Check Your Contributed Amount

When a withdrawal is available, the sale page displays your contribution information.

Your **contributed amount** is shown on the left side of the withdrawal area, above the **Withdraw** button.

Review the displayed amount before starting the withdrawal.

The available withdrawal amount is determined by the protocol's on-chain rules and the contribution associated with your connected wallet.

<Callout type="info" title="Use the Correct Wallet">
You must connect the wallet that contributed SOL.

Connecting a different wallet will not give that wallet access to another contributor's withdrawal.
</Callout>

# Withdraw Contributed SOL

Once the sale is in an eligible withdrawal state and your connected wallet has a contribution, the **Withdraw** button becomes available.

Select **Withdraw** to begin the withdrawal process.

The withdrawal process proceeds through several stages.

## Step 1: Prepare the Transaction

After selecting **Withdraw**, RugFreePump prepares the withdrawal transaction.

Keep the sale page open while the transaction is being prepared.

## Step 2: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet requests transaction approval.

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it if the details are correct.

The withdrawal transaction is then submitted to Solana.

## Step 3: Wait for Transaction Confirmation

After approval, RugFreePump waits for the transaction to be confirmed on Solana.

Keep the page open while confirmation is in progress.

## Step 4: Withdrawal Completed

Once the transaction succeeds, the contributed SOL is returned to your connected wallet according to the protocol's on-chain withdrawal rules.

The sale page updates to reflect the completed withdrawal.

# SOL Balance Requirements

Your wallet should contain enough SOL to pay the Solana network fee required to process the withdrawal transaction.

The network transaction fee is separate from your contributed amount.

Keeping a small SOL balance available can help ensure that the withdrawal transaction can be submitted successfully.

# If Your Withdrawal Fails

A withdrawal may fail if, for example:

- The sale is not in an eligible withdrawal state.
- Your connected wallet did not make a contribution.
- The withdrawal has already been completed.
- Your wallet does not have enough SOL for the network transaction fee.
- Your wallet connection failed.
- The transaction cannot be prepared or executed.
- Another protocol validation failed.
- A Solana network or transaction issue occurs.

If the withdrawal fails, review the message shown by RugFreePump and your wallet.

Make sure you are connected to the correct wallet and confirm that the sale is in an eligible withdrawal state before trying again.

# What Happens to Your Purchased Tokens?

A withdrawal of eligible contributed SOL and ownership of tokens are separate matters.

Your token balance remains associated with your wallet and is governed by the protocol's on-chain rules.

Before participating in or withdrawing from a bonding curve sale, review the applicable **Security & Risk** documentation.

# Withdrawal Summary

The normal withdrawal process is:

1. Open the bonding curve sale page.
2. Review the coin, **Mint Key**, pool, sale status, and your contribution.
3. Confirm that the sale is in an eligible withdrawal state.
4. Connect the wallet that made the contribution.
5. Complete the required consent process if prompted.
6. Review your contributed amount displayed above the **Withdraw** button.
7. Select **Withdraw**.
8. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.
9. Wait for Solana transaction confirmation.
10. Receive the eligible contributed SOL according to the protocol's on-chain withdrawal rules.
