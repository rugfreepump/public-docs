---
title: "Withdrawing Contributed SOL"
description: "Learn how to withdraw eligible contributed SOL from an Expired or Canceled RugFreePump bonding curve sale."
---

When a bonding curve sale does not proceed to liquidity migration, eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL.

Withdrawals are available when a sale becomes **Expired** or **Canceled** and are processed according to the protocol's on-chain rules.

# When the Withdraw Button Appears

When a sale becomes **Expired** or **Canceled**, the sale page transitions to the corresponding sale state.

If you contributed to that sale and are logged in using the wallet that made the contribution, the sale page displays your contributed amount and the **Withdraw** button.

The contributed amount is displayed above the **Withdraw** button.

The **Withdraw** button and contributed amount are only displayed when:

- You are logged in with the wallet that made the contribution.
- The sale is in an eligible withdrawal state.
- Your wallet has a contribution associated with that sale.

Users who did not contribute to the sale do not see the contributed amount or the **Withdraw** button.

Users who are not logged in also do not see the contributed amount or the **Withdraw** button.

# Your Displayed Contributed Amount

The contributed amount displayed on the sale page comes from RugFreePump's indexed transaction data.

The RugFreePump indexer processes on-chain sale transactions and stores aggregated contribution data in the platform database so the sale page can display the contribution amount efficiently.

The displayed amount is intended to reflect your actual contribution, but it is not the canonical source of truth.

In rare cases, an indexed transaction may be delayed, missed, or not correctly stored in the database. The actual contribution and withdrawal eligibility are determined by the protocol's on-chain state.

<Callout type="info" title="On-Chain State Is Canonical">
If an indexing issue causes the displayed contribution amount to be temporarily incomplete or inaccurate, your on-chain contribution is not lost.

The withdrawal transaction uses the canonical on-chain state to determine the eligible withdrawal amount.
</Callout>

# Withdrawal Fees

RugFreePump does not charge a protocol fee for withdrawing contributed SOL.

You only need enough SOL in your wallet to pay the required **Solana network transaction fee** for the withdrawal transaction.

The Solana network transaction fee is separate from any RugFreePump protocol fee.

# Withdraw Your Contributed SOL

Once the sale is in an eligible withdrawal state and the **Withdraw** button is available, select **Withdraw** to begin.

## Step 1: Select Withdraw

On the **Expired** or **Canceled** sale page, select the **Withdraw** button.

RugFreePump prepares the withdrawal transaction.

You will see:

> Preparing The Transaction...

## Step 2: Approve the Transaction

After the transaction is prepared, your wallet will request approval.

The sale page displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Approval...

Review the transaction in your wallet and approve it to continue.

## Step 3: Wait for Transaction Confirmation

After approval, RugFreePump waits for confirmation from the Solana network.

The sale page displays:

> Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...

Once the transaction is successfully confirmed, the withdrawal is complete.

# After a Successful Withdrawal

After the withdrawal transaction succeeds, your eligible contributed SOL is returned to your wallet according to the protocol's on-chain rules.

The sale page updates to reflect the completed withdrawal.

# If You Reject the Transaction

If you reject the withdrawal transaction in your wallet, the withdrawal does not complete.

Your eligible on-chain contribution remains unchanged.

You can try again while the sale remains eligible for withdrawal.

# If the Withdrawal Fails

If the withdrawal transaction fails, your eligible contributed SOL is not automatically lost.

Make sure:

- Your wallet is connected.
- You are using the wallet that made the contribution.
- Your wallet has enough SOL to pay the Solana network transaction fee.
- The sale remains in an eligible withdrawal state.

If the problem continues, contact RugFreePump support.

# Withdrawal Summary

The withdrawal process is:

1. The sale becomes **Expired** or **Canceled**.
2. Log in using the wallet that contributed to the sale.
3. Your contributed amount and the **Withdraw** button appear when applicable.
4. Select **Withdraw**.
5. Approve the transaction in your wallet.
6. Wait for Solana transaction confirmation.
7. Your eligible contributed SOL is returned according to the protocol's on-chain rules.

RugFreePump does not charge a protocol fee for withdrawals. The required Solana network transaction fee still applies.
