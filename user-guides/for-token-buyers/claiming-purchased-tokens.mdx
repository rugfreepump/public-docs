---
title: "Claiming Purchased Tokens"
description: "Learn how to claim your purchased tokens from a migrated RugFreePump bonding curve sale."
---

After a RugFreePump bonding curve sale successfully completes liquidity migration to Raydium CPMM, buyers can claim their purchased tokens according to the sale's **Token Unlocked (TU)** or **Token Locked (TL)** model.

Claiming requires the buyer to initiate a transaction. Purchased tokens are not automatically transferred to your wallet after migration.

For information about the two sale models and their claiming conditions, see [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models).

## Eligibility & UI Visibility

The **Claim Token** panel appears on the specific sale page when the following conditions are met:

- The sale has successfully entered the **Migrated** state.
- You have connected the wallet used to purchase tokens from that sale.
- Your wallet has a recorded purchased token allocation for the sale.

The panel displays your claiming information and the amount currently available to claim.

*Note: If you did not participate in the sale, have not connected the participating wallet, or the sale has not completed liquidity migration, the claiming panel will not be available.*

## Understanding Your Claim Panel

The **Claim Token** panel displays your purchased token allocation and current claiming status.

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Available To Claim** | The amount of tokens you can claim immediately. |
| **Total Claimable** | Your total purchased token allocation for the sale. |
| **Claimable Balance** | For TU, the amount remaining to claim. For TL, the remaining unclaimed allocation, including tokens that have not yet unlocked. |
| **Total Claimed** | The total amount of tokens you have already claimed. |

For **TL sales**, the panel also displays the current **Unlock Progress**, completed unlocks, claimed unlocks, and **Next Unlock In** countdown.

The countdown changes to **Fully Unlocked** once the entire purchased allocation has become available.

**Important:** Fully Unlocked does not mean Fully Claimed. You must still submit a claiming transaction to receive any remaining tokens.

### Claim Balance Accuracy

The displayed balances are provided through RugFreePump's frontend data services and may occasionally experience temporary synchronization delays.

However, **the on-chain program determines the actual amount of tokens available to claim**. Frontend display discrepancies cannot override the claiming conditions recorded on-chain.

## Transaction Fees

RugFreePump charges **zero additional protocol fees** for claiming purchased tokens.

You only need sufficient SOL to cover:

- The applicable Solana network transaction fee.
- Any token-account creation costs, if a receiving token account must be created.

For TL sales, each separate claiming transaction may incur its own network fee.

Make sure your connected wallet has enough SOL available before initiating a claim.

## Step-by-Step Claiming Process

Once the sale status changes to **Migrated** and you have tokens available to claim, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Sale Page:** Navigate to the specific token sale and connect the wallet used for your original purchase.
2. **Review Your Tokens:** Locate the **Claim Token** panel and check **Available To Claim**. For TL sales, you can also review the unlock progress and countdown.
3. **Initiate the Claim:** Click the **Claim** button displaying your token symbol. The interface will display: `Preparing The Transaction...`
4. **Approve in Wallet:** Your connected wallet will request transaction approval while the interface displays: `Waiting For Transaction Approval...` Review the transaction and select **Approve**.
5. **Network Confirmation:** After approval, RugFreePump monitors the transaction while displaying: `Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...`
6. **Verify the Transaction:** Once confirmed, the interface displays `Transaction Succeeded` and provides a **Tx Link** to view the transaction on Solscan.

After successful confirmation, the claimed tokens are transferred to your wallet.

## After a Successful Claim

The **Claim Token** panel updates to reflect your completed transaction.

- **TU Sales:** Your entire purchased token allocation is transferred to your wallet through a successful claim.
- **TL Sales:** The currently available tokens are transferred to your wallet. You can return later to claim additional tokens when they become available.

Once you have claimed your entire purchased token allocation, the claim button displays **Fully Claimed** and becomes unavailable for further claims.

For TL sales, you do not need to claim tokens at every unlock interval. Any unlocked tokens that remain unclaimed can be claimed later.

## When Claiming Is Unavailable

The claim button may be disabled for several reasons:

- **No Tokens Available:** Your TL allocation has not yet reached its next unlock, or no additional tokens are currently available.
- **Fully Claimed:** You have already claimed your entire purchased token allocation.
- **Transaction Processing:** A claim transaction is already in progress.
- **Platform Maintenance:** Claiming is temporarily restricted, and the interface displays `Claims are temporarily unavailable`.

If your TL allocation has not fully unlocked, check **Next Unlock In** to see when additional tokens are scheduled to become available.

## Troubleshooting Failed Transactions

If your claim transaction fails or does not complete, verify the following before trying again:

- Your wallet is connected and matches the address used for the original purchase.
- The sale is in the **Migrated** state.
- You have tokens currently available to claim.
- Your wallet contains sufficient SOL for network fees and any required token-account creation costs.
- The platform is not temporarily restricting claims due to maintenance.
- Your wallet and Solana network connection are functioning correctly.

If you reject the wallet request or the approval process times out, the interface may display `Transaction Canceled Or Timeout`.

If the transaction fails after submission, the interface may display `Transaction Failed. Please Try Again!` with a **Tx Link**.

### Transaction Confirmation Timeout

If the interface displays `Transaction Confirmation Timeout`, the transaction may have been submitted successfully even though its final confirmation was not received by the frontend.

**Before attempting another claim, check the transaction status using the provided Tx Link.**

If the transaction succeeded on-chain but your claim balances have not updated, allow time for the platform's data indexer to synchronize and then refresh the sale page.

If you continue experiencing issues, please contact **RugFreePump Support** with your wallet address, the token's mint address, and the relevant transaction signature.
