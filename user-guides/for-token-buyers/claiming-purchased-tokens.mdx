title: "Claiming Purchased Tokens"
description: "Learn how to claim purchased tokens from a migrated RugFreePump bonding curve sale under the Token Unlocked (TU) or Token Locked (TL) sale model."
---

When a RugFreePump bonding curve sale successfully completes liquidity migration to Raydium CPMM, buyers become eligible to claim their purchased tokens according to the sale's selected token sale model.

RugFreePump supports two models:

- **Token Unlocked (TU):** Buyers can claim their entire purchased token allocation immediately after successful liquidity migration.
- **Token Locked (TL):** Buyers can claim purchased tokens gradually as they become available under the sale's predefined unlock schedule.

Purchased tokens are not transferred to buyers' wallets during the active fundraising phase. Instead, the protocol records each buyer's purchased token allocation on-chain and releases tokens through the claiming process after the applicable conditions are satisfied.

For a detailed explanation of both models, see [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models).

## Eligibility & UI Visibility

The **Claim Token** panel automatically appears on the specific sale page when the following conditions are met:

- The sale has successfully completed liquidity migration and entered the **Migrated** state.
- You have connected the wallet used to purchase tokens from that sale.
- Your wallet has a valid purchased token allocation recorded for the sale.

The panel remains visible after you have claimed your entire allocation, allowing you to review your completed claim.

*Note: If you did not purchase tokens from the sale, have not connected the participating wallet, or the sale has not completed migration, the claiming panel will not appear.*

## Understanding Your Claimable Tokens

The **Claim Token** panel displays your purchased token allocation and claiming status.

The following information is available under both TU and TL:

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Available To Claim** | The amount of purchased tokens you can claim immediately. |
| **Total Claimable** | Your total purchased token allocation recorded for the sale. |
| **Claimable Balance** | Under TU, the amount currently available to claim. Under TL, the portion of your purchased allocation that has not yet been claimed, including tokens still locked. |
| **Total Claimed** | The total amount of purchased tokens you have already claimed. |

The displayed amounts are calculated using your recorded purchase and claim information.

While the frontend may occasionally experience temporary indexing delays or synchronization issues, **the on-chain program determines the actual amount you are eligible to claim**. Frontend balances do not override the protocol's claiming rules.

## Claiming Under Token Unlocked (TU)

For a **TU sale**, your entire purchased token allocation becomes available as soon as liquidity migration succeeds.

The Claim Token panel displays your full allocation under **Available To Claim** and **Total Claimable**, provided you have not already claimed it.

TU uses a single full-allocation claim rather than gradual token unlocking.

After a successful claim:

- Your purchased tokens are transferred to your wallet.
- **Available To Claim** and **Claimable Balance** become zero.
- **Total Claimed** reflects your full purchased allocation.
- The claim button displays **Fully Claimed** and becomes unavailable for further claims.

There is no additional unlock waiting period after successful migration.

## Claiming Under Token Locked (TL)

For a **TL sale**, purchased tokens become available gradually according to the unlock schedule established when the sale was created.

The schedule begins after successful liquidity migration. You can claim only the portion that has unlocked, but you do not need to submit a transaction every time more tokens become available.

### Understanding Unlock Progress

In addition to the standard token balances, the TL Claim Token panel displays:

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Unlock Progress** | The percentage of your purchased allocation that has unlocked. |
| **Unlocks** | The number of completed unlock intervals compared with the total scheduled intervals. |
| **Claimed** | The number of unlock intervals already covered by your successful claims. |
| **Next Unlock In** | A live countdown until the next scheduled token unlock. |

Once the entire allocation has unlocked, the countdown displays **Fully Unlocked**.

**Fully Unlocked does not necessarily mean Fully Claimed.** Your remaining unlocked tokens must still be claimed through a wallet transaction.

### Claiming Unlocked Tokens

As each scheduled unlock occurs, the newly available tokens are added to your claimable amount.

For example, consider a buyer who purchased **100,000 tokens** in a TL sale with an unlock schedule of **10% every 30 minutes**:

- After 30 minutes, 10,000 tokens become available.
- After 1 hour, 20,000 tokens have unlocked in total.
- After 2 hours 30 minutes, 50,000 tokens have unlocked in total.
- After 5 hours, the entire 100,000-token allocation has unlocked.

If you have not claimed any tokens after 2 hours 30 minutes, you can claim all 50,000 unlocked tokens in one transaction.

If you previously claimed 20,000 tokens, only the additional 30,000 unlocked tokens will be available to claim at that time.

**Claiming does not accelerate the unlock schedule.** Tokens that remain locked cannot be claimed early.

The exact unlock percentage and interval depend on the settings permanently recorded for the specific sale.

For the current settings applicable to newly created TL sales, see [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models).

## Transaction Fees

RugFreePump charges **zero additional protocol fees** for claiming purchased tokens under either TU or TL.

You only need sufficient SOL to cover the applicable Solana network transaction fee and any token-account creation costs required for your wallet.

For TL sales, each separate claiming transaction may incur its own network fee. Buyers may therefore choose to wait and claim multiple unlocked portions together rather than submitting a transaction after every unlock.

## Step-by-Step Claiming Process

Once the sale reaches the **Migrated** state and you have tokens available to claim, follow these steps:

1. **Connect Your Wallet:** Open the specific token sale page and connect the wallet used to purchase the tokens.

2. **Review Your Claim:** Locate the **Claim Token** panel and check **Available To Claim**. For TL sales, review the unlock progress and next unlock countdown if additional tokens remain locked.

3. **Initiate the Claim:** Click **Claim** followed by the token symbol. The interface displays `Preparing The Transaction...` while RugFreePump prepares your claim transaction.

4. **Approve in Your Wallet:** Your connected wallet prompts you to approve the transaction while the interface displays `Waiting For Transaction Approval...`. Review the transaction and approve it.

5. **Wait for Confirmation:** After submission, the interface displays `Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...` while checking the Solana network.

6. **Verify Your Claim:** When the transaction succeeds, the interface displays `Transaction Succeeded` with a **Tx Link** to view the transaction on Solscan.

After a successful claim, the claimed tokens are transferred to your wallet, and the claiming panel updates to reflect the completed transaction.

For TU, the claim is complete after the full allocation has been transferred.

For TL, you may return later to claim additional tokens as they unlock. Once your entire purchased allocation has been claimed, the button displays **Fully Claimed**.

## When the Claim Button Is Unavailable

The **Claim** button may be disabled under the following conditions:

- **No tokens are currently available:** For TL sales, you may need to wait until the next unlock.
- **Your allocation has been fully claimed:** The button displays **Fully Claimed**.
- **A claim transaction is processing:** The button temporarily displays **Processing...**.
- **Platform maintenance is enabled:** The interface displays `Claims are temporarily unavailable`.

If a TL sale has reached **Fully Unlocked** but you have already claimed all available tokens, the claim button remains disabled because no additional tokens are available.

## Troubleshooting Failed Transactions

If your claim fails or does not complete, verify the following before trying again:

- Your connected wallet matches the address that originally purchased the tokens.
- The sale has successfully completed liquidity migration.
- You have a positive amount displayed under **Available To Claim**.
- Your wallet has sufficient SOL to cover the transaction fee and any applicable token-account creation costs.
- Your wallet connection and Solana network connection are functioning correctly.
- The platform is not temporarily restricting claims due to maintenance.

If you reject the wallet approval request or the transaction times out before submission, the interface may display `Transaction Canceled Or Timeout`.

If a submitted transaction fails, the interface displays `Transaction Failed. Please Try Again!` with a **Tx Link**.

If the interface displays `Transaction Confirmation Timeout`, the transaction may have been submitted without its final status being retrieved successfully.

**Before retrying a transaction with an uncertain confirmation status, open the Tx Link and verify its status on Solscan.** Avoid submitting another claim until you have checked whether the previous transaction succeeded.

If your claim was confirmed on-chain but the displayed balances have not updated, refresh the sale page after allowing time for the platform's indexer to synchronize.

If the issue persists, please contact **RugFreePump Support** and provide the sale address, your wallet address, and the relevant transaction signature.
