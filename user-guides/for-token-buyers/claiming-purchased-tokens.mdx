---
title: "Claiming Purchased Tokens"
description: "Learn how to claim your purchased tokens from a migrated RugFreePump bonding curve sale."
---

After a bonding curve sale successfully completes liquidity migration to Raydium CPMM, buyers can claim their purchased tokens according to the claiming conditions of the selected **Token Unlocked (TU)** or **Token Locked (TL)** sale model.

Purchased tokens are not automatically transferred to buyers' wallets after liquidity migration. Buyers must initiate a token claim transaction to receive them.

For more information about the two models and their claiming conditions, see [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models).

## Eligibility & UI Visibility

The **Claim Token** panel appears on the specific sale page when the following conditions are met:

- The sale has successfully entered the **Migrated** state.
- You have connected the wallet used to purchase tokens from that sale.
- Your wallet has a valid purchased token allocation recorded for the sale.

The panel displays your claiming information according to the sale's selected model and remains visible after your entire allocation has been claimed.

*Note: If you did not participate in the sale, have not connected the participating wallet, or the sale has not completed liquidity migration, the claiming panel will not appear.*

## Token Unlocked (TU) Claim Panel

For TU sales, the **Claim Token** panel provides a straightforward, one-time claiming process.

The panel displays the following information:

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Available To Claim** | The amount of purchased tokens currently available to claim. |
| **Total Claimable** | Your total purchased token allocation for the sale. |
| **Claimable Balance** | Your remaining unclaimed token balance. |
| **Total Claimed** | The total amount of tokens you have already claimed. |

Once liquidity migration succeeds, your full purchased allocation is available to claim.

After a successful claim, **Available To Claim** and **Claimable Balance** become zero, **Total Claimed** reflects your full allocation, and the claim button displays **Fully Claimed**.

## Token Locked (TL) Claim Panel

For TL sales, the **Claim Token** panel provides additional information to help buyers monitor their token availability and claiming progress.

### Token Balances

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Available To Claim** | The amount of unlocked tokens you can claim immediately, excluding tokens already claimed. |
| **Total Claimable** | Your total purchased token allocation for the sale. |
| **Claimable Balance** | Your remaining unclaimed token allocation, including tokens that are still locked. |
| **Total Claimed** | The total amount of tokens you have already claimed. |

**Important:** For TL sales, **Claimable Balance** is not the amount you can withdraw immediately. Always check **Available To Claim** before initiating a transaction.

### Unlock Progress & Countdown

The TL panel also displays the following information:

| Display | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Unlock Progress** | A progress bar showing the percentage of your purchased allocation that has unlocked. |
| **Unlocks** | The number of completed unlock intervals compared with the total scheduled intervals. |
| **Claimed** | The number of unlock intervals already covered by your completed claims. |
| **Next Unlock In** | A live countdown showing when the next scheduled unlock becomes available. |

When the entire allocation has unlocked, **Next Unlock In** changes to **Fully Unlocked**.

**Fully Unlocked does not mean Fully Claimed.** If you still have unclaimed tokens, you must submit a claiming transaction to receive them.

You can claim available tokens whenever you choose. You are not required to claim at every unlock interval.

Once your entire purchased allocation has been claimed, the button displays **Fully Claimed** and becomes unavailable for further claims.

## Claim Balance Accuracy

The balances displayed in both claiming panels are calculated using RugFreePump's frontend data and the sale's recorded claiming information.

Temporary synchronization delays or indexing issues may occasionally cause displayed balances to differ from the latest on-chain state.

However, **the on-chain program determines the actual amount of tokens available to claim**. Frontend display discrepancies cannot override the protocol's claiming conditions.

## Transaction Fees

RugFreePump charges **zero additional protocol fees** for claiming purchased tokens under either sale model.

You only need sufficient SOL to cover:

- The applicable Solana network transaction fee.
- Any token-account creation costs, if a receiving token account must be created.

For TL sales, each separate claim may incur its own network fee.

Make sure your connected wallet has enough SOL before initiating a claim.

## Step-by-Step Claiming Process

Once the sale is in the **Migrated** state and you have tokens available to claim, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Sale Page:** Navigate to the specific token sale and connect the wallet used for your original purchase.
2. **Review Your Tokens:** Locate the **Claim Token** panel and check **Available To Claim**. For TL sales, you can also review the unlock progress and countdown.
3. **Initiate the Claim:** Click **Claim** followed by the token symbol. The interface displays: `Preparing The Transaction...`
4. **Approve in Wallet:** Your connected wallet requests transaction approval while the interface displays: `Waiting For Transaction Approval...` Review the transaction and select **Approve**.
5. **Network Confirmation:** After approval, RugFreePump monitors the transaction while displaying: `Waiting For Transaction Confirmation...`
6. **Verify the Transaction:** Once confirmed, the interface displays `Transaction Succeeded` with a **Tx Link** to view the transaction on Solscan.

After successful confirmation, the claimed tokens are transferred to your wallet, and the claiming panel updates to reflect the completed transaction.

For TU sales, the full purchased allocation is claimed in one successful transaction. For TL sales, you can return later to claim additional tokens as they become available.

## When Claiming Is Unavailable

The **Claim** button may be disabled under the following conditions:

- **No Tokens Available:** For TL sales, no additional tokens have unlocked since your previous claim, or the first unlock has not yet occurred.
- **Fully Claimed:** You have already claimed your entire purchased token allocation.
- **Transaction Processing:** A claiming transaction is in progress, and the button temporarily displays **Processing...**.
- **Platform Maintenance:** Claiming is temporarily restricted, and the interface displays `Claims are temporarily unavailable`.

If you are participating in a TL sale and no tokens are currently available, check **Next Unlock In** to see when additional tokens are scheduled to become available.

## Troubleshooting Failed Transactions

If your claiming transaction fails or does not complete, verify the following before trying again:

- Your connected wallet matches the address used for the original purchase.
- The sale has successfully entered the **Migrated** state.
- You have a positive amount under **Available To Claim**.
- Your wallet has sufficient SOL for network fees and any required token-account creation costs.
- The platform is not temporarily restricting claims due to maintenance.
- Your wallet and Solana network connection are functioning correctly.

If you reject the wallet approval request or the approval process times out, the interface may display `Transaction Canceled Or Timeout`.

If a submitted transaction fails, the interface displays `Transaction Failed. Please Try Again!` with a **Tx Link**.

### Transaction Confirmation Timeout

If the interface displays `Transaction Confirmation Timeout`, the transaction may have been submitted successfully even though its final confirmation was not received by the frontend.

**Before attempting another claim, check the transaction status using the provided Tx Link.**

If the transaction succeeded on-chain but your claim balances have not updated, allow time for the platform's data indexer to synchronize and then refresh the sale page.

If the issue persists, please contact **RugFreePump Support** with your wallet address, the token's mint address, and the relevant transaction signature.
