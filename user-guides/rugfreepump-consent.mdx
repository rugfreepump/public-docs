# RugFreePump Consent

RugFreePump uses a wallet-based consent process before users can access financial operations on the platform.

You can freely explore RugFreePump without completing consent. However, you must have a valid consent for operations such as creating a coin, buying bonding curve sale tokens, withdrawing contributed SOL, or migrating raised liquidity.

Consent is associated with your wallet address and the specific versions of the required legal documents that you agreed to.

---

## What Is RugFreePump Consent?

RugFreePump Consent is your confirmation that you have reviewed and agreed to the current required legal documents and meet the applicable eligibility requirements.

The consent process consists of:

1. Reviewing the required documents
2. Confirming that you understand and agree to them
3. Signing the consent agreement with your connected wallet

The wallet signature provides cryptographic confirmation that the wallet controlling the address approved the consent.

The signature is a **message signature**, not a blockchain transaction.

It does not transfer SOL or tokens, authorize a token transfer, or give RugFreePump custody of your assets.

---

## When Is Consent Required?

Consent is required before performing RugFreePump financial operations.

This includes:

- **Creating a coin**
- **Buying tokens from a bonding curve sale**
- **Withdrawing contributed SOL**
- **Migrating raised liquidity**

If your wallet does not have a current consent when you attempt one of these operations, RugFreePump will require you to complete the consent process before continuing.

### Exploring Without Consent

Consent is **not** required simply to explore the platform.

You can browse publicly available information such as:

- Tokens and sales
- Profiles
- Sale statistics
- Platform information
- Documentation

If you do not want to complete consent, you can select **Logout** when the consent prompt is displayed and continue exploring RugFreePump.

You can return later and complete the consent process when you decide to use a financial operation.

## How the Consent Process Works

When consent is required, RugFreePump guides you through the following process.

### 1. Open the Consent Card

If your wallet does not have a current consent and you attempt a financial operation, RugFreePump displays the consent card.

The card identifies the required documents and their applicable versions.

### 2. Review the Documents

Open the document links shown in the consent card and carefully read:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Risk Disclosure

Make sure you review the documents presented by the consent card before continuing.

### 3. Select Continue

After reviewing the documents, select **Continue**.

You will then be asked to approve a wallet signature.

### 4. Review the Wallet Request

Your wallet will display a signature request.

Review the request before approving it.

The consent request is a **message signature**, not a blockchain transaction.

### 5. Approve the Signature

If you agree to the applicable documents, approve the signature in your wallet.

The signature confirms that the wallet controlling the connected address completed the consent process.

### 6. Continue With Your Operation

After the consent has been successfully recorded, you can continue with the financial operation you originally attempted.

For example, if you were creating a coin, you can proceed with the coin creation transaction.

## Consent and Document Versions

RugFreePump legal documents are versioned.

Your consent is associated with the specific versions of the three required documents that you reviewed and agreed to.

For example, a consent may cover:

- Terms of Service v1.2
- Privacy Policy v1.3
- Risk Disclosure v1.2

The versions presented to you may change over time.

### When Any Document Changes

If a new version of **any one** of the three required documents is published, you must review and sign the updated consent before performing financial operations that require current consent.

For example:

Your existing consent covers:

- Terms of Service v1.2
- Privacy Policy v1.3
- Risk Disclosure v1.2

If the Risk Disclosure changes to v1.3, you must complete the consent process again.

You do not need to wait for all three documents to change.

**A new version of any required document triggers an updated consent requirement.**

### Why Versions Matter

Document versioning ensures that a consent can be associated with the exact versions that were presented to and accepted by the user.

A modified document is therefore not silently treated as the document previously accepted.

If a document needs to change, a new version is published.

Previously published versions are preserved rather than silently replaced.

## Consent Is Associated With Your Wallet

Consent is associated with the wallet address that completes the consent process.

This means consent is not simply a browser setting or temporary acknowledgement.

If you connect a different wallet, that wallet may need to complete its own consent process.

Changing your device or browser does not change which wallet address completed the consent.

You may still be asked to authenticate again when signing in from another device or browser.

---

## Wallet Signatures and Asset Security

The consent signature confirms agreement to the applicable documents. It does not authorize RugFreePump to spend your assets.

A consent signature:

- Does **not** transfer SOL
- Does **not** transfer tokens
- Does **not** approve a token transfer
- Does **not** create a blockchain transaction
- Does **not** give RugFreePump custody of your assets
- Does **not** provide RugFreePump with your private key

Actual blockchain operations require their own transaction approval through your wallet.

For example, creating a coin involves separate stages:

1. Complete consent if required
2. Prepare the coin creation transaction
3. Review the transaction in your wallet
4. Approve the blockchain transaction
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation

The consent signature does not replace transaction approval.

---

## Protect Your Wallet

Only approve wallet requests that you understand and intended to initiate.

Before signing:

- Confirm that you are using the intended RugFreePump website
- Review what your wallet is asking you to sign
- Confirm that the request matches the action you are performing
- Reject unexpected requests
- Never share your seed phrase or private key

RugFreePump does not require your wallet's seed phrase, private key, or wallet password.

If a wallet request does not match what you expect, reject it and investigate before continuing.

## If You Do Not Agree

You are not required to accept the RugFreePump consent agreement.

If you do not agree with the required documents:

1. Do not approve the wallet signature.
2. Select **Logout** if the consent card is displayed.
3. Continue exploring the publicly available parts of RugFreePump.

Without a current consent, financial operations requiring consent will remain unavailable.

You can return later and complete the process if you decide to use those features.

---

## Consent Does Not Guarantee Transaction Success

Completing consent only satisfies the consent requirement.

It does **not** guarantee that a financial transaction will succeed.

A subsequent transaction can still fail because of conditions such as:

- Insufficient SOL
- Wallet rejection
- Network conditions
- Transaction fees
- Slippage conditions
- Sale state changes
- Oracle conditions
- Smart contract validation
- Other blockchain or platform conditions

Consent should therefore be understood separately from transaction authorization and transaction execution.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need consent to use RugFreePump?

You do not need consent to explore the platform or view publicly available information.

You do need a current consent before performing supported financial operations.

### Do I need to sign consent every time I use RugFreePump?

No.

You normally complete consent once for the current versions of the three required documents.

You will need to complete it again when a new version of any required document is published.

### What happens if only one document changes?

The consent must be updated.

For example, if only the Risk Disclosure receives a new version, you must review and sign the updated consent before performing financial operations.

### Does consent expire after a fixed number of days?

Consent is based on the current versions of the required documents rather than simply expiring after a fixed number of days.

If the required document versions remain current, the consent remains valid for those versions.

### Does signing consent transfer SOL?

No.

The consent is completed through a message signature and does not transfer SOL or tokens.

### Does signing consent allow RugFreePump to spend my SOL?

No.

The consent signature does not authorize RugFreePump to spend your assets or take custody of your wallet.

Blockchain transactions require separate wallet approval.

### What if I connect another wallet?

Consent is associated with the wallet address that completed it.

A different wallet may therefore need to complete its own consent process.

### Why am I being asked for consent again?

The most common reason is that a new version of one of the required documents has been published.

You may also need to authenticate again if you are not currently signed in.

### Can I use RugFreePump without agreeing to the documents?

Yes, for non-financial exploration.

You can browse the platform without completing consent, but financial operations requiring consent will remain unavailable.

### Does consent mean I accept the risks of buying tokens?

The consent process requires you to review and agree to the applicable legal documents, including the Risk Disclosure.

You should carefully read the Risk Disclosure and understand the risks before participating in any token sale.

## Platform Use and External Program Access

RugFreePump operates a permissionless on-chain program on Solana. Because the program is permissionless, it can be accessed through RugFreePump's platform or through independent third-party applications, services, bots, scripts, and other software.

RugFreePump's platform consent and service terms apply to users who use the RugFreePump platform and its applicable services.

If a user performs financial operations through the RugFreePump platform, those operations are subject to the RugFreePump consent process and the applicable RugFreePump terms, policies, and risk disclosures.

If a user interacts directly with the RugFreePump on-chain program through an external application or service, that interaction occurs outside the RugFreePump platform.

Users accessing the program through an external service are responsible for complying with the terms, consent requirements, disclosures, and applicable legal obligations of the service or application they choose to use.

RugFreePump does not require or control the consent process of independent third-party services that interact with the public on-chain program.

### On-Chain Activity and Platform Use

RugFreePump's indexer monitors transactions involving the RugFreePump on-chain program regardless of where those transactions originate.

Therefore, a wallet may appear in RugFreePump's indexed on-chain records even if it has never completed RugFreePump Consent.

An indexed transaction only establishes that an interaction with the RugFreePump on-chain program occurred. It does not by itself establish that the transaction was initiated through the RugFreePump platform.

Platform consent and on-chain program activity are recorded separately.

This distinction allows RugFreePump to identify whether a wallet had completed the RugFreePump platform consent process when using applicable platform services, while still maintaining visibility of public on-chain activity involving the RugFreePump program.

### External Services

When using a third-party application, bot, service, or other software to interact with the RugFreePump on-chain program, users are responsible for understanding and complying with the requirements applicable to that external service.

Users should review the external service's terms, consent requirements, risk disclosures, and other applicable policies before performing financial operations through that service.

Interaction with the RugFreePump on-chain program through an external service does not constitute acceptance of RugFreePump's platform consent process merely because the transaction interacts with the RugFreePump program.

RugFreePump's platform consent applies to the use of RugFreePump's own platform services.
