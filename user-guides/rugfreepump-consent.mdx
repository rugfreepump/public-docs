# RugFreePump Consent

RugFreePump uses a wallet-based consent process to ensure that users have reviewed and agreed to the current legal documents before using the platform's financial services.

You can explore RugFreePump without completing consent. However, a valid consent is required before performing financial operations such as creating a coin, buying sale tokens, withdrawing contributed SOL, or migrating raised liquidity.

The consent process is tied to your connected wallet address and must be completed again whenever a new version of any required legal document is published.

---

## What Is RugFreePump Consent?

RugFreePump Consent is your confirmation that you have:

1. Reviewed the required legal documents
2. Agreed to their current versions
3. Confirmed that you meet the applicable eligibility requirements
4. Authorized your connected wallet to be associated with that consent

The consent is completed through your wallet by signing a message.

The wallet signature provides cryptographic proof that the wallet controlling the account completed the consent process.

A consent signature is **not a blockchain transaction** and does not transfer SOL, tokens, or other assets.

---

## Why Is Consent Required?

RugFreePump is a permissionless token launch platform where users can create tokens and participate in bonding curve sales.

These activities involve financial transactions and risks, including the possibility of losing funds.

Before using RugFreePump's financial functionality, users must therefore have an opportunity to review the documents governing the service and the risks associated with using it.

The consent process provides a clear point at which the user confirms that they have reviewed and agreed to the currently applicable documents.

---

## Consent Is Not Required to Explore RugFreePump

You do **not** need to complete consent simply to browse the platform.

You can explore RugFreePump and view publicly available information without signing the consent agreement.

For example, you can browse:

- Token and sale information
- Public profiles
- Sale statistics
- Platform information
- Documentation
- Other publicly available content

If you do not want to complete the consent process, you can choose **Logout** when the consent prompt is displayed and continue exploring RugFreePump.

However, financial operations require a current consent.

---

## When Is Consent Required?

A valid current consent is required before performing financial operations on RugFreePump.

This includes:

- **Creating a coin**
- **Buying tokens from a bonding curve sale**
- **Withdrawing contributed SOL**
- **Migrating raised liquidity**

If you attempt one of these operations without a current consent, RugFreePump will require you to complete the consent process before allowing the operation to continue.

This requirement applies even if you previously connected your wallet.

---

## The Three Required Documents

The consent process covers three required legal documents:

### 1. Terms of Service

The **Terms of Service** define the terms and conditions governing your use of RugFreePump.

They explain the rules applicable to users of the platform and the conditions under which the service is provided.

[Read the Terms of Service](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/legal-and-compliance/terms-of-service)

### 2. Privacy Policy

The **Privacy Policy** explains how information associated with the use of RugFreePump is handled.

It describes the platform's approach to information collection, use, storage, and related privacy matters.

[Read the Privacy Policy](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy)

### 3. Risk Disclosure

The **Risk Disclosure** explains important risks associated with using RugFreePump, including risks related to tokens, bonding curve sales, blockchain transactions, wallets, market conditions, and other aspects of the platform.

[Read the Risk Disclosure](https://rugfreepump.com/docs/legal-and-compliance/risk-disclosure)

> **Important:** The links shown in the RugFreePump consent card are the documents you are being asked to agree to. Always use those links to review the exact documents and versions presented during the consent process.

---

## Review the Documents Before Continuing

When consent is required, RugFreePump displays a consent card containing the documents applicable to your consent.

Before selecting **Continue**, carefully read all three documents:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Risk Disclosure

Do not treat the consent step as a formality.

The documents contain important information about using RugFreePump, including your responsibilities, platform terms, privacy practices, and risks associated with participating in token sales.

If you do not understand or agree with the applicable documents, do not continue with the consent process.

You can select **Logout** and continue using RugFreePump for non-financial exploration.

---

## How the Consent Process Works

The consent process consists of two main stages:

1. Review the required documents
2. Sign the consent agreement with your wallet

### Step 1: Open the Consent Card

When a financial operation requires consent and your wallet does not have a current consent, RugFreePump displays the consent card.

The card identifies the required legal documents and their applicable versions.

Review the information carefully.

### Step 2: Read All Three Documents

Open each document using the links provided in the consent card.

Read:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Risk Disclosure

Make sure you are reviewing the documents linked from the consent card before proceeding.

### Step 3: Select Continue

After reviewing the documents, select **Continue**.

This indicates that you are ready to proceed with the consent process.

### Step 4: Review the Wallet Signature Request

Your wallet will then request a signature.

Review the signature request shown by your wallet before approving it.

The request is a **message signature**, not a blockchain transaction.

### Step 5: Approve the Signature

Approve the signature in your wallet if you agree to the consent terms.

The signature confirms that the wallet controlling the connected address completed the consent process.

### Step 6: Continue With the Financial Operation

After the consent has been successfully recorded, RugFreePump can allow the requested financial operation to continue.

For example, if you were creating a coin, you can proceed with the coin creation transaction after completing consent.

---

## What Does the Wallet Signature Do?

The wallet signature serves as cryptographic confirmation that the connected wallet approved the consent agreement.

It allows RugFreePump to associate the consent with the wallet address that completed it.

The signature does **not**:

- Transfer SOL
- Transfer tokens
- Approve a token transfer
- Create a blockchain transaction
- Give RugFreePump custody of your wallet
- Give RugFreePump your private key
- Give RugFreePump permission to spend your assets

A message signature and a blockchain transaction are different things.

The consent signature itself does not move assets on Solana.

---

## Always Review Wallet Requests Carefully

Only approve a consent signature after reviewing the request and confirming that you intend to agree to the applicable documents.

You should never approve a wallet request simply because RugFreePump asks you to sign something.

For any wallet interaction:

- Confirm that you are using the intended RugFreePump website
- Review what your wallet is asking you to approve
- Do not share your seed phrase or private key
- Do not approve unexpected transaction requests
- Stop if the wallet request does not match what you expect

RugFreePump will never require you to provide your wallet's private key or recovery phrase.

---

## Consent Is Tied to Your Wallet Address

Consent is associated with the wallet address that completes the consent process.

This means consent is not simply a browser setting or a temporary acknowledgement.

If you connect a different wallet, that wallet may need to complete its own consent process before performing financial operations.

Similarly, clearing browser data, changing devices, or using another browser does not change which wallet address completed the consent.

---

## Consent Applies to Specific Document Versions

RugFreePump legal documents are versioned.

Your consent applies to the specific versions of the required documents that you reviewed and agreed to.

The three documents are:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Risk Disclosure

Each document has its own version.

For example, a consent could require:

- Terms of Service v1.2
- Privacy Policy v1.3
- Risk Disclosure v1.2

The exact versions presented to you may change over time.

---

## What Happens When a Document Is Updated?

If a new version of **any one** of the three required documents is published, your previous consent is no longer sufficient for financial operations that require the current documents.

You will be asked to complete the consent process again.

For example, suppose your previous consent covered:

- Terms of Service v1.2
- Privacy Policy v1.3
- Risk Disclosure v1.2

If the Risk Disclosure is updated to v1.3, you must review and sign the updated consent again.

You do not need to wait for all three documents to change.

**A new version of any one of the required documents triggers an updated consent requirement.**

---

## Why Are Document Versions Important?

Versioning ensures that a consent record can be associated with the exact legal documents that were presented to the user at the time of consent.

A change to a document means that its contents are no longer identical to the previous version.

Therefore, even a small change results in a new document version.

This allows RugFreePump to distinguish between:

- Consent to an older version
- Consent to the current version

Users are therefore not silently treated as having agreed to a document they have never reviewed.

---

## Versioned Documents Are Preserved

The documents used for consent are stored as versioned records.

Once a version has been published for consent, that version is preserved.

Published versions are not silently replaced with modified contents.

If a document needs to change, a new version is created and published instead.

This preserves the relationship between a user's consent and the document version that was available when the consent was completed.

---

## What Happens If You Do Not Agree?

You are not required to complete the consent process.

If you do not agree to the required documents, do not sign the consent agreement.

You can choose **Logout** and continue exploring the publicly available parts of RugFreePump.

However, without a current consent, you will not be able to perform financial operations that require consent.

This includes:

- Creating a coin
- Buying bonding curve sale tokens
- Withdrawing contributed SOL
- Migrating raised liquidity

You can return later and complete the consent process if you decide to use these features.

## Consent Does Not Mean Your Transaction Will Succeed

Completing consent only satisfies the platform's consent requirement.

It does not guarantee that a later blockchain transaction will succeed.

A financial operation can still fail because of factors such as:

- Insufficient SOL
- Wallet rejection
- Network conditions
- Transaction fees
- Slippage conditions
- Sale state changes
- Oracle conditions
- Smart contract validation
- Other blockchain or platform conditions

Consent should therefore not be confused with transaction authorization or transaction success.

---

## Consent Is Separate From Transaction Approval

RugFreePump may require multiple wallet interactions during a financial operation.

The consent signature confirms agreement to the required legal documents.

A later transaction approval is used to authorize the actual blockchain operation.

These are separate actions.

For example, when creating a coin:

1. You complete the consent process if required.
2. RugFreePump prepares the creation transaction.
3. Your wallet asks you to approve the blockchain transaction.
4. The blockchain transaction is submitted and confirmed.

The consent signature does not replace the transaction approval.

---

## What Information Is Associated With Consent?

A consent record needs to identify the wallet and the document versions to which the user consented.

The consent process therefore associates the consent with information such as:

- The wallet address
- The applicable Terms of Service version
- The applicable Privacy Policy version
- The applicable Risk Disclosure version
- The time at which consent was completed

The consent process also uses cryptographic signing to verify that the connected wallet approved the agreement.

---

## Consent and Your Wallet Security

Your wallet remains under your control.

RugFreePump does not need:

- Your seed phrase
- Your private key
- Your wallet password
- Custody of your assets

Only approve wallet requests that you understand and intended to initiate.

If a wallet request asks for something that does not match the action you are performing, reject it and investigate before continuing.
