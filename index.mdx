These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump**. Whether you are participating in a bonding curve sale, launching a project, developing an integration, reviewing the on-chain program, or researching the protocol, you can find the resources relevant to your role and objective.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized around the different participants, builders, and reviewers who interact with the ecosystem.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to get started, choose, create, and fund a wallet, participate in bonding curve sales, understand eligible withdrawals, and use the platform safely.

### Token Creators

Learn how to create a token, launch a bonding curve sale, manage the fundraising process, and migrate liquidity after a successful sale.

### Developers & Integrators

Build applications, bots, analytics tools, trading interfaces, and other integrations using RugFreePump's public on-chain program, APIs, generated types, and real-time infrastructure.

### Researchers & Analysts

Explore tokenomics, bonding curve pricing, oracle pricing, protocol fees, sale lifecycle, and the technical and economic principles behind RugFreePump.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Review the on-chain program, public instructions, account structures, Program Derived Address (PDA) derivation, transaction requirements, proof of execution, and security model.

### Ecosystem Participants

Explore the reward program, platform updates, security considerations, legal policies, and other resources covering the broader RugFreePump ecosystem.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose the path that best matches what you are trying to accomplish.

* **New to RugFreePump?** Start with [What is RugFreePump](/overview/what-is-rugfreepump) to understand the platform and its anti-rug launch model, then follow the lifecycle of a token launch in [How RugFreePump Works](/overview/how-rugfreepump-works).

* **Want to participate in a launch?** Begin with [Getting Started](/user-guides/first-steps/getting-started) to understand the basics. From there, learn how to handle your wallet ([Choosing a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/choosing-a-wallet), [Creating a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/creating-a-wallet), [Funding a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/funding-a-wallet)), execute [Your First Purchase](/user-guides/first-steps/your-first-purchase), and navigate safe contribution rules in [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

* **Want to launch a new token?** Learn how to initialize your market in [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale). If the graduation threshold is met, see how to execute the final automated LP generation process in [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity).

* **Want to manage your contributions?** Walk through the onboarding workflow in [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens). If a sale expires without meeting its graduation target, read [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) to safely reclaim your funds.

* **Want to understand the underlying tokenomics?** Master the math and rule engines behind the protocol by exploring [Tokenomics](/core-mechanics/tokenomics), [Token Metadata](/core-mechanics/token-metadata), [Slippage & Priority Fees](/core-mechanics/slippage-and-priority), [Oracle Price Verification](/core-mechanics/oracle-price), [Bonding Curve Mechanics](/core-mechanics/bonding-curve), [Protocol Fees](/core-mechanics/protocol-fees), and the [Sale Lifecycle](/core-mechanics/sale-lifecycle).

* **Want to audit or verify the protocol?** Review the core Solana program layout via the [On-Chain Program Overview](/on-chain-program/overview) and learn how state transitions are mathematically validated in [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution).

* **Want to build on-chain integrations or bots?** Read [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) for an integration blueprint. Then move step-by-step through our smart contract references: [Program ID & Network](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/program-id-and-network), [Public Instructions](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/public-instructions), [Account Structures](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/accounts-and-account-structures), [PDA Derivation](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/pda-derivation-and-seeds), [Anchor IDL](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/anchor-idl), and [Generated Types](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/generated-types).

* **Want to programmatically interact via API or WebSockets?** Dive into the [API Integration Overview](/developer-guide/api-integration/overview) to parse payload formats. Follow explicit steps for programmatic actions—including [Create Sale](/developer-guide/api-integration/create-sale), [Buy](/developer-guide/api-integration/buy), [Withdraw](/developer-guide/api-integration/withdraw), and [Migrate Liquidity](/developer-guide/api-integration/migrate-liquidity)—while handling [Rate Limits](/developer-guide/api-integration/rate-limits-and-restrictions) and streaming state changes via the [Real-Time WebSocket](/developer-guide/api-integration/realtime-websocket).

* **Want to analyze risk boundaries or disclose a flaw?** Review the complete [Security Model](/security-and-risk/security-model) alongside dedicated risk disclosure frameworks for [Smart Contracts](/security-and-risk/smart-contract-risks), [Oracles](/security-and-risk/oracle-risks), [Liquidity Migrations](/security-and-risk/liquidity-and-migration-risks), and [User Responsibilities](/security-and-risk/user-responsibilities). If you locate a technical vulnerability, please consult our [Security Disclosure](/security-and-risk/security-disclosure) policy.
