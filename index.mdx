# Welcome To RugFreePump

RugFreePump is a next-generation, high-conviction crowdfunded launchpad built natively on Solana. It moves away from the unrestricted buy-and-sell model commonly used by traditional bonding curve launchpads and introduces a structured, protocol-enforced framework for taking a token from creation through its funding phase and, when successful, into public liquidity.

Traditional token launches can expose participants to extreme early-stage speculation, automated sniping, rapid sell-offs, and significant reliance on the behavior of token creators. RugFreePump takes a different approach: important rules governing the launch are enforced by the on-chain program itself, creating a clearly defined environment where creators and buyers know how a sale can progress and what happens at each stage.

### Built Around Conviction

RugFreePump's launch model is designed to make the fundraising phase about building genuine participation rather than enabling an unrestricted race to buy and immediately sell. During an active sale, the protocol does not provide a normal sell operation, creating a purely additive funding phase in which contributions move the project toward its defined funding milestone.

### Defined Outcomes

Every sale follows a defined lifecycle. A successful sale reaches its funding target and becomes eligible for liquidity migration. A sale that expires without reaching its target enters an expired state, allowing eligible buyers to invoke the withdrawal mechanism and recover their contributed SOL according to the protocol's rules.

### On-Chain by Design

The rules that govern pricing, contributions, sale state transitions, fees, withdrawals, and migration are implemented through RugFreePump's Solana program. This reduces dependence on off-chain promises and gives participants a way to verify protocol execution directly on-chain.

### An Ecosystem, Not Just a Launch

RugFreePump also provides infrastructure for developers, real-time integrations, and a reward program that recognizes active creators and buyers. From the public on-chain program to APIs and real-time data feeds, the platform is designed to support an ecosystem that can grow beyond the launch interface itself.

These docs provide the complete guide to RugFreePump. Explore the sections below to understand how the platform works, how to create and participate in sales, how its mechanics are enforced on-chain, and how to build on top of the protocol.
