These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump**. Whether you are participating in a bonding curve sale, launching a project, developing an integration, reviewing the on-chain program, or researching the protocol, you can find the resources relevant to your role and objective.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized around the different participants, builders, and reviewers who interact with the ecosystem.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to get started, choose, create, and fund a wallet, participate in bonding curve sales, understand eligible withdrawals, and use the platform safely.

### Token Creators

Learn how to create a token, launch a bonding curve sale, manage the fundraising process, and migrate liquidity after a successful sale.

### Developers & Integrators

Build applications, bots, analytics tools, trading interfaces, and other integrations using RugFreePump's public on-chain program, APIs, generated types, and real-time infrastructure.

### Researchers & Analysts

Explore tokenomics, bonding curve pricing, oracle pricing, protocol fees, sale lifecycle, and the technical and economic principles behind RugFreePump.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Review the on-chain program, public instructions, account structures, Program Derived Address (PDA) derivation, transaction requirements, proof of execution, and security model.

### Ecosystem Participants

Explore the reward program, platform updates, security considerations, legal policies, and other resources covering the broader RugFreePump ecosystem.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose the path that best matches what you are trying to accomplish.

* **New to RugFreePump?** Start with **Overview** -> [What is RugFreePump](/overview/what-is-rugfreepump) to understand the platform and its anti-rug launch model, then follow a token's lifecycle in **Overview** -> [How RugFreePump Works](/overview/how-rugfreepump-works).

* **Want to participate in a launch?** Begin with **User Guides** -> [Getting Started](/user-guides/first-steps/getting-started) to understand the platform. From there, learn how to handle your wallet in **User Guides** -> [Choosing a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/choosing-a-wallet), [Creating a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/creating-a-wallet), and [Funding a Wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/funding-a-wallet), make your first purchase in **User Guides** -> [Your First Purchase](/user-guides/first-steps/your-first-purchase), and navigate platform safety rules in **User Guides** -> [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

* **Want to launch a new token?** Learn how to initialize your market in **User Guides** -> [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale). If the graduation threshold is met, see how to execute the automated LP process in **User Guides** -> [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity).

* **Want to manage your contributions?** Walk through the purchase workflow in **User Guides** -> [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens). If a sale expires without meeting its target, read **User Guides** -> [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) to safely reclaim your funds.

* **Want to understand the protocol mechanics?** Master the math and system rules by exploring **Core Mechanics** -> [Tokenomics](/core-mechanics/tokenomics), [Token Metadata](/core-mechanics/token-metadata), [Slippage & Priority](/core-mechanics/slippage-and-priority), [Oracle Price](/core-mechanics/oracle-price), [Bonding Curve](/core-mechanics/bonding-curve), [Protocol Fees](/core-mechanics/protocol-fees), and [Sale Lifecycle](/core-mechanics/sale-lifecycle).

* **Want to audit or verify the protocol?** Review the core Solana program layout via **On-Chain Program** -> [Overview](/on-chain-program/overview) and learn how state transitions are mathematically validated in **On-Chain Program** -> [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution).

* **Want to build on-chain integrations or bots?** Read **Developer Guide** -> [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) for a blueprint. Then move through the contract references in **Developer Guide** -> **On-Chain Program** -> [Program ID & Network](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/program-id-and-network), [Public Instructions](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/public-instructions), [Account Structures](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/accounts-and-account-structures), [PDA Derivation](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/pda-derivation-and-seeds), [Anchor IDL](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/anchor-idl), and [Generated Types](/developer-guide/on-chain-program/generated-types).

* **Want to programmatically interact via API or WebSockets?** Read **Developer Guide** -> **API Integration** -> [Overview](/developer-guide/api-integration/overview) to parse payload formats. Follow explicit steps for programmatic actions—including [Create Sale](/developer-guide/api-integration/create-sale), [Buy](/developer-guide/api-integration/buy), [Withdraw](/developer-guide/api-integration/withdraw), and [Migrate Liquidity](/developer-guide/api-integration/migrate-liquidity)—while managing [Rate Limits](/developer-guide/api-integration/rate-limits-and-restrictions) and streaming state changes via the [Real-Time WebSocket](/developer-guide/api-integration/realtime-websocket).

* **Want to analyze risk boundaries or disclose a flaw?** Review the complete **Security & Risk** -> [Security Model](/security-and-risk/security-model) alongside dedicated risk disclosure frameworks for [Smart Contract Risks](/security-and-risk/smart-contract-risks), [Oracle Risks](/security-and-risk/oracle-risks), [Liquidity & Migration Risks](/security-and-risk/liquidity-and-migration-risks), and [User Responsibilities](/security-and-risk/user-responsibilities). For reporting vulnerabilities, consult the [Security Disclosure](/security-and-risk/security-disclosure) framework.

* **Want to track platform incentives and rewards?** Check out the **Reward Program** -> [Overview](/reward-program/overview) to see how incentives are structures, then look into [How Rewards Are Earned](/reward-program/how-rewards-are-earned), [Reward Periods & Rankings](/reward-program/reward-periods-and-rankings), [Reward Calculation](/reward-program/reward-calculation), and [Reward Distribution](/reward-program/reward-distribution).

* **Want to review legal policies and compliance guidelines?** Read through **Legal & Compliance** -> [Terms of Service](/legal-and-compliance/terms-of-service), [Privacy Policy](/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy), [Risk Disclosure](/legal-and-compliance/risk-disclosure), [DMCA Policy](/legal-and-compliance/dmca-policy), and [Trademark Guidelines](/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).

* **Want to stay updated with platform updates?** Follow **Announcements** -> [Updates and Changelog](/announcements/updates-and-changelog) to monitor recent releases, protocol changes, and documentation modifications.
