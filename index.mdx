Welcome to the official **RugFreePump Documentation**.

These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump** — a Solana-native, high-conviction crowdfunded launchpad that uses structured bonding curve fundraising to help creators and contributors work toward a shared sale threshold.

Whether you are participating in a token sale, launching a project, exploring the protocol's economic model, developing an integration, or reviewing its on-chain execution, this documentation provides the resources relevant to your role and objectives.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized around the participants, creators, developers, researchers, and reviewers who interact with the Platform and its underlying Solana program.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to prepare and secure a supported Solana wallet, participate in bonding curve sales, understand TU and TL token claiming conditions, withdraw eligible SOL contributions, and evaluate the risks of participating in token launches.

### Token Creators

Learn how to create a coin, configure its metadata, select a token sale model, launch a bonding curve sale, manage the fundraising lifecycle, and migrate raised liquidity to Raydium CPMM after successful completion.

### Developers & Integrators

Explore the permissionless RugFreePump on-chain program, supported integration methods, Anchor IDL and generated types, and the technical limitations of building applications that interact with the protocol.

### Researchers & Analysts

Examine RugFreePump's high-conviction fundraising model, bonding curve mechanics, TU and TL token distribution, protocol fees, sale outcomes, liquidity migration, and economic incentives.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Review on-chain proof of execution, protocol security considerations, administrative authority, upgradeability, technical risks, and the responsible vulnerability disclosure process.

### Ecosystem Participants

Explore the Reward Program, official Platform announcements, wallet safety guidance, frequently asked questions, legal policies, and other resources covering the broader RugFreePump ecosystem.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose the documentation path that best matches what you want to accomplish, understand, or verify.

### New to RugFreePump?

Start with **Overview** to understand RugFreePump's high-conviction fundraising approach and how it differs from traditional buy-and-sell bonding curve launches.

Begin with [Overview](/overview/overview) to explore the fundraising lifecycle, successful liquidity migration, and expired sale outcomes.

Continue with [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models) to understand Token Unlocked (TU) and Token Locked (TL) claiming conditions, then review [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for current protocol charges and authorized update ranges.

### Want to Participate in a Launch?

Start with **User Guides** to prepare your wallet, understand participation requirements, and learn how token purchases work.

Follow [Getting Started](/user-guides/getting-started) to set up a Phantom or Solflare wallet, obtain SOL, and connect to RugFreePump.

Before participating, review [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety) for wallet protection and [RugFreePump Consent](/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent) for the wallet-based agreement required before Platform financial operations.

When ready, follow [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens). For common questions about participation, visit the [FAQ](/user-guides/faq).

### Want to Launch a Token?

Start with **User Guides → For Token Creators**.

Follow [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale) to understand coin creation, metadata requirements, creator purchases, and selecting a permanent TU or TL sale model.

After a sale reaches its fundraising threshold, continue with [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity) to understand migration to Raydium CPMM, liquidity provisioning, and LP token burning.

### Want to Manage Your Contributions?

Start with **User Guides → For Token Buyers**.

Read [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens) to understand how purchases are processed and token allocations are recorded during fundraising.

After successful liquidity migration, follow [Claiming Purchased Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/claiming-purchased-tokens) to understand immediate TU claiming and gradual TL unlocking. Under the current TL settings, 10% becomes available every 30 minutes, with full availability after 5 hours.

If a sale expires or is canceled through an authorized emergency procedure, follow [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) to understand eligibility and the withdrawal process.

### Want to Understand the Protocol?

Start with **Overview** to explore the fundamental mechanics and economic design of RugFreePump.

Read [Overview](/overview/overview) to understand structured fundraising, the absence of standard bonding-curve sell-back during active sales, sale progression, and liquidity migration.

Continue with [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models) for the differences between TU and TL, and [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for the applicable charges and allocations.

For information about independently examining protocol execution, visit [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution).

### Want to Build on RugFreePump?

Start with the **Developer Guide**.

Read [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) to understand integration with the public Solana program and the distinction between permissionless on-chain operations and Platform-operated services.

For supported on-chain interaction, review [Buy](/developer-guide/on-chain-integration/buy).

Continue with [Anchor IDL & Types](/developer-guide/anchor-idl-and-types) for program interface resources and [Developer Limitations](/developer-guide/developer-limitations) to understand integration boundaries and operational considerations.

### Want to Audit or Verify the Protocol?

Start with **On-Chain Program**.

Read [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution) to understand how transactions, protocol operations, and their execution outcomes can be independently verified using Solana's public blockchain.

For technical integration context, explore [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) and [Anchor IDL & Types](/developer-guide/anchor-idl-and-types).

Review [Security](/security-and-risk/security) and [Risks](/security-and-risk/risks) to understand the protocol's authority model, upgradeability, security boundaries, and remaining technical and operational risks.

### Want to Understand Security and Risk?

Start with **Security & Risks**.

Begin with [Security](/security-and-risk/security) to understand the Platform's security approach, protocol-enforced controls, administrative authority, and important limitations.

Continue with [Risks](/security-and-risk/risks) for potential risks involving smart contracts, token projects, liquidity, wallets, market activity, and supporting infrastructure.

If you discover a potential vulnerability, follow the [Security Disclosure](/security-and-risk/security-disclosure) process.

For practical guidance on avoiding scams, protecting wallet credentials, and verifying transactions, also review [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

### Want to Understand the Reward Program?

Start with **Reward Program**.

Read [Reward Program](/reward-program/reward-program) to understand the 28-day reward periods, separate buyer and creator rankings, eligibility requirements, reward calculations, and distribution procedures.

The program allocates defined portions of eligible Platform revenue to qualifying participants according to its published rules.

For official reward-related announcements, program developments, and published results, visit [Announcements](/announcements/announcements).

### Want to Review Legal and Compliance Information?

Start with **Legal & Compliance**.

Review the [Terms of Service](/legal-and-compliance/terms-of-service) for Platform usage conditions, [Privacy Policy](/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy) for information handling, and [Risk Disclosure](/legal-and-compliance/risk-disclosure) for important financial, technical, and participation risks.

For intellectual property matters, review the [Copyright Policy](/legal-and-compliance/copyright-policy) and [Trademark Guidelines](/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).

These documents explain the applicable Platform policies, user responsibilities, rights, restrictions, and important limitations.

### Want to Follow Platform Updates?

Start with **Announcements**.

Visit [Announcements](/announcements/announcements) for official Platform announcements, Reward Program updates and results, feature releases, maintenance notices, bug fixes, and other important developments.

Announcements are organized with the newest publications first, making it easier to follow recent updates while accessing earlier notices.
