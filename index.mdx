These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump**. Whether you are participating in a bonding curve sale, launching a project, developing an integration, reviewing the on-chain program, or researching the protocol, you can find the resources relevant to your role and objective.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized around the different participants, builders, and reviewers who interact with the ecosystem.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to get started, choose, create, and fund a wallet, participate in bonding curve sales, understand eligible withdrawals, and use the platform safely.

### Token Creators

Learn how to create a token, launch a bonding curve sale, manage the fundraising process, and migrate liquidity after a successful sale.

### Developers & Integrators

Build applications, bots, analytics tools, trading interfaces, and other integrations using RugFreePump's public on-chain program, APIs, generated types, and real-time infrastructure.

### Researchers & Analysts

Explore tokenomics, bonding curve pricing, oracle pricing, protocol fees, sale lifecycle, and the technical and economic principles behind RugFreePump.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Review the on-chain program, public instructions, account structures, Program Derived Address (PDA) derivation, transaction requirements, proof of execution, and security model.

### Ecosystem Participants

Explore the reward program, platform updates, security considerations, legal policies, and other resources covering the broader RugFreePump ecosystem.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose the path that best matches what you are trying to accomplish.

* **New to RugFreePump?** Start with [What is RugFreePump](/overview/what-is-rugfreepump) to understand the platform and its launch model, then continue with [How RugFreePump Works](/overview/how-rugfreepump-works) to follow the lifecycle of a token sale.

* **Want to participate in a sale?** Begin with [Getting Started](/user-guides/first-steps/getting-started), then learn [what you need](/user-guides/first-steps/what-you-need), [how to choose a wallet](/user-guides/first-steps/choosing-a-wallet), [how to fund it](/user-guides/first-steps/funding-a-wallet), and [how to make your first purchase](/user-guides/first-steps/your-first-purchase).

* **Want to launch a token?** Start with [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale) and [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity) for the successful-sale migration process.

* **Want to understand the protocol?** Explore [Tokenomics](/core-mechanics/tokenomics), [Bonding Curve](/core-mechanics/bonding-curve), [Oracle Price](/core-mechanics/oracle-price), [Protocol Fees](/core-mechanics/protocol-fees), and [Sale Lifecycle](/core-mechanics/sale-lifecycle).

* **Want to build an integration?** Start with [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump), then explore the [API Integration guide](/developer-guide/api-integration/overview).

* **Want to understand security and limitations?** Start with the [Security Model](/security-and-risk/security-model), then review the relevant [Smart Contract Risks](/security-and-risk/smart-contract-risks), [Oracle Risks](/security-and-risk/oracle-risks), and [User Responsibilities](/security-and-risk/user-responsibilities).
