These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump**. Whether you are participating in a bonding curve sale, launching a project, developing an integration, reviewing the on-chain program, or researching the protocol, you can find the resources relevant to your role and objective.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized around the different participants, builders, researchers, and reviewers who interact with the ecosystem.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to get started, choose and fund a wallet, participate in bonding curve sales, claim purchased tokens, understand eligible withdrawals, and use the Platform safely.

### Token Creators

Learn how to create a token, launch a bonding curve sale, understand the fundraising process, and migrate liquidity after a successful sale.

### Developers & Integrators

Learn about building on RugFreePump, interacting with supported on-chain instructions, using the Anchor IDL and generated types, and understanding integration limitations.

### Researchers & Analysts

Explore token sale models, bonding curve fundraising, protocol fees, sale outcomes, and the technical and economic principles behind RugFreePump.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Review the on-chain program's proof of execution, security considerations, risks, and disclosure procedures.

### Ecosystem Participants

Explore the Reward Program, Platform announcements, security considerations, legal policies, and other resources covering the broader RugFreePump ecosystem.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose the path that best matches what you are trying to accomplish.

### New to RugFreePump?

Start with the **Overview** section to understand the Platform and its bonding curve fundraising model.

Begin with [Overview](/overview/overview), then explore [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models) to understand the differences between Token Unlocked (TU) and Token Locked (TL) sales.

For information about applicable charges, continue with [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees).

### Want to Participate in a Launch?

Start with **User Guides**.

Begin with [Getting Started](/user-guides/getting-started) to understand the requirements for participating in RugFreePump.

Before interacting with the Platform, review [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety) and [RugFreePump Consent](/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent).

When you are ready to participate, follow [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens).

For answers to common questions, visit the [FAQ](/user-guides/faq).

### Want to Launch a Token?

Start with **User Guides → For Token Creators**.

Follow [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale) to understand how to create and launch a token sale.

After a sale successfully completes, continue with [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity) to understand the liquidity migration process.

### Want to Manage Your Contributions?

Start with **User Guides → For Token Buyers**.

Learn the purchase process in [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens).

After successful liquidity migration, follow [Claiming Purchased Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/claiming-purchased-tokens) to understand token claiming under the TU and TL sale models.

If a sale expires or is canceled and your contribution becomes eligible for withdrawal, follow [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

### Want to Understand the Protocol?

Start with **Overview**.

Read [Overview](/overview/overview) to understand RugFreePump's fundraising model and sale lifecycle.

Continue with [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models) and [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) to explore the available sale models and applicable charges.

For information about verifying protocol operations, visit [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution).

### Want to Build on RugFreePump?

Start with the **Developer Guide**.

Begin with [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) to understand the available development and integration approach.

For supported on-chain interaction, review [Buy](/developer-guide/on-chain-integration/buy).

Continue with [Anchor IDL & Types](/developer-guide/anchor-idl-and-types) for program interface information and [Developer Limitations](/developer-guide/developer-limitations) to understand the current integration boundaries.

### Want to Audit or Verify the Protocol?

Start with **On-Chain Program**.

Read [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution) to understand how important protocol operations and their outcomes can be verified through on-chain execution.

For additional technical context, explore [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) and [Anchor IDL & Types](/developer-guide/anchor-idl-and-types).

Review [Security](/security-and-risk/security) and [Risks](/security-and-risk/risks) for information about the Platform's security considerations and remaining risks.

### Want to Understand Security and Risk?

Start with **Security & Risks**.

Begin with [Security](/security-and-risk/security) to understand the Platform's security model and related considerations.

Continue with [Risks](/security-and-risk/risks) to review potential risks associated with using RugFreePump.

If you discover a potential vulnerability, follow the [Security Disclosure](/security-and-risk/security-disclosure) process.

For practical participant guidance, also review [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety).

### Want to Understand the Reward Program?

Start with **Reward Program**.

Read [Reward Program](/reward-program/reward-program) to understand participation requirements, reward periods, rankings, calculations, and distributions.

For official announcements about Reward Program activities and results, visit [Announcements](/announcements/announcements).

### Want to Review Legal and Compliance Information?

Start with **Legal & Compliance**.

Review the [Terms of Service](/legal-and-compliance/terms-of-service), [Privacy Policy](/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy), [Risk Disclosure](/legal-and-compliance/risk-disclosure), [Copyright Policy](/legal-and-compliance/copyright-policy), and [Trademark Guidelines](/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).

### Want to Follow Platform Updates?

Start with **Announcements**.

The [Announcements](/announcements/announcements) page contains official Platform announcements, Reward Program results, feature releases, maintenance notices, bug fixes, and other important updates
