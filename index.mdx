These docs are designed to help you **understand, use, research, verify, integrate with, and build on RugFreePump**, a Solana-native, high-conviction crowdfunded launchpad built around structured bonding curve fundraising and protocol-defined sale outcomes.

Whether you are discovering the Platform, participating in a token sale, launching a project, developing an integration, or independently reviewing the protocol, this documentation helps you find the information relevant to your objectives.

## Who Is This Documentation For?

RugFreePump documentation is organized for different participants and technical audiences, from first-time Solana users to developers and independent reviewers.

### Token Buyers

Learn how to prepare and protect a Solana wallet, participate in token sales, manage purchased token allocations, claim available tokens, withdraw eligible contributions, and understand participation risks.

### Token Creators

Explore the requirements for creating a coin, configuring a bonding curve sale, selecting a token sale model, and completing the liquidity migration process after successful fundraising.

### Developers & Integrators

Access resources for interacting with the RugFreePump Solana program, using its Anchor IDL and generated types, implementing supported instructions, and understanding integration boundaries.

### Researchers & Analysts

Study the high-conviction fundraising approach, bonding curve mechanics, token sale models, protocol fees, sale outcomes, liquidity migration, and the economic structure of the Platform.

### Auditors & Technical Reviewers

Examine on-chain execution, program authority and upgradeability, security considerations, technical risks, and procedures for reporting potential vulnerabilities.

### Ecosystem Participants

Discover the Reward Program, official announcements, frequently asked questions, participant safety resources, and legal policies governing use of the Platform.

## How to Use These Docs

Choose a path based on your objective. Each path points to the relevant documentation without requiring you to read unrelated sections.

### Understand RugFreePump

If you are new to RugFreePump or want to research its design, begin with the foundational documentation.

Start with [Overview](/overview/overview) to understand the Platform's purpose, fundraising approach, and sale lifecycle.

Continue with [Token Sale Models](/overview/token-sale-models) for the differences between Token Unlocked (TU) and Token Locked (TL) sales, and [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees) for applicable charges and configuration limits.

### Get Started and Participate

If you want to use RugFreePump for the first time, follow the participant onboarding resources.

Begin with [Getting Started](/user-guides/getting-started) to prepare a supported Solana wallet and connect to the Platform.

Before performing financial operations, review [Trust & Safety](/user-guides/trust-and-safety) for wallet protection and [RugFreePump Consent](/user-guides/rugfreepump-consent) for consent requirements.

For answers to common questions, explore the [FAQ](/user-guides/faq).

### Create and Launch a Token

If you are a token creator, follow the documentation covering the complete launch process.

Read [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale) for coin creation, sale configuration, model selection, and launch requirements.

Then explore [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity) to understand how successfully completed sales transition to Raydium CPMM liquidity.

### Buy, Claim, or Withdraw

If you are a token buyer, choose the guide corresponding to the action you want to perform.

- [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens) — Understand the purchasing process, contribution requirements, and recorded token allocations.
- [Claiming Purchased Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/claiming-purchased-tokens) — Learn when purchased tokens become claimable and how claiming works under TU and TL sales.
- [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol) — Understand withdrawal eligibility and procedures for expired or eligible canceled sales.

### Build and Integrate

If you are developing an application or integrating with the protocol, use the technical documentation.

Begin with [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) for the available integration approach and the relationship between the on-chain program and Platform services.

Explore [Buy](/developer-guide/on-chain-integration/buy) for the documented on-chain purchase integration and [Anchor IDL & Types](/developer-guide/anchor-idl-and-types) for program interface resources.

Review [Developer Limitations](/developer-guide/developer-limitations) to understand the current integration scope and technical boundaries.

### Verify Execution and Review Security

If you are researching the protocol's implementation, reviewing its security, or independently examining on-chain activity, use the verification and security resources.

Start with [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution) to understand how protocol operations and execution outcomes can be examined through Solana.

Read [Security](/security-and-risk/security) for the security model, administrative authority, and operational controls, followed by [Risks](/security-and-risk/risks) for remaining technical and financial risks.

If you discover a potential vulnerability, follow the [Security Disclosure](/security-and-risk/security-disclosure) process.

### Explore Rewards and Platform Updates

If you want to understand ecosystem incentives or follow official developments, explore the Reward Program and announcement resources.

Read [Reward Program](/reward-program/reward-program) for participation requirements, eligibility, rankings, reward calculations, and distributions.

Visit [Announcements](/announcements/announcements) for official Reward Program results, feature releases, maintenance notices, bug fixes, and other Platform updates.

### Review Legal and Compliance Information

If you need to understand the conditions and responsibilities associated with using RugFreePump, consult the applicable legal documentation.

Review the [Terms of Service](/legal-and-compliance/terms-of-service), [Privacy Policy](/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy), and [Risk Disclosure](/legal-and-compliance/risk-disclosure) for Platform conditions, information handling, and material risks.

For intellectual property matters, see the [Copyright Policy](/legal-and-compliance/copyright-policy) and [Trademark Guidelines](/legal-and-compliance/trademark-guidelines).
