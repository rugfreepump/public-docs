# How Rewards Are Earned

The RugFreePump Reward Program has two reward categories:

- **Buyer Rewards**
- **Creator Rewards**

Each category uses a different measure of qualifying activity.

## Buyer Rewards

Buyer rewards are based on **qualifying purchase volume** during the reward period.

Every qualifying token purchase contributes to a buyer's ranking for that period.

The more qualifying purchase volume a buyer accumulates during the period, the higher their potential ranking.

At the end of the 28-day reward period, the buyers are ranked according to their qualifying purchase volume.

Only the **top 100 buyers** are eligible for buyer rewards.

### Example

If a buyer makes several qualifying purchases during a reward period, the qualifying purchase volume from those purchases contributes toward that buyer's final ranking.

A buyer does not need to make one large purchase. Multiple qualifying purchases can contribute to the buyer's total qualifying volume.

## Creator Rewards

Creator rewards are based on **successful bonding-curve liquidity migrations** during the reward period.

Each successful migration contributes toward the creator's ranking.

Only creators who have completed at least one successful migration during the reward period can qualify for the creator ranking.

The **top 100 creators** by total successful migrations are eligible for creator rewards.

### Example

If a creator successfully migrates liquidity for multiple eligible sales during the reward period, those successful migrations contribute to the creator's ranking.

A creator with more successful migrations will generally rank higher than a creator with fewer successful migrations.

## Creator Ranking Tie-Breakers

If multiple creators have the same number of successful migrations, additional ranking criteria are used to determine their order.

The tie-breakers are applied in this order:

1. **Buy volume**
2. **Wallet key ordering**

This provides a deterministic ordering when creators have the same primary migration count.

## Activity Must Occur During the Reward Period

Reward activity is evaluated within the specific 28-day reward period.

Activity from a previous reward period does not carry over into a new period's rankings.

Likewise, activity that occurs after a period has ended does not change that completed period's final ranking.

## Active Rankings Are Estimates

During an active reward period, rankings and estimated rewards may change.

The displayed rankings are updated approximately every **60 seconds**.

A user's current position can therefore move up or down as other eligible participants generate additional qualifying activity.

The active ranking should be treated as an estimate rather than a final result.

## Final Eligibility

When the reward period ends, RugFreePump creates the final reward snapshot.

The final snapshot determines:

- Which buyers are eligible
- Which creators are eligible
- The final ranking of each participant
- The reward allocated to each participant

The final snapshot is permanent once finalized.

For the complete ranking process, see [Reward Periods & Rankings](/reward-program/reward-periods-and-rankings).
