# Reward Program

The RugFreePump Reward Program rewards users who contribute meaningful activity to the platform as **buyers** and **token creators**.

The program operates in recurring **28-day reward periods**. At the end of each period, eligible users are ranked based on qualifying activity, and the available reward pool is distributed between the highest-ranked buyers and creators.

## How the Program Works

Each reward period follows a simple process:

1. Platform revenue generated during the reward period contributes to the reward pool.
2. Buyer and creator activity is tracked throughout the period.
3. Rankings are updated periodically while the reward period is active.
4. The reward period ends after 28 days.
5. Final rankings are permanently recorded.
6. Rewards are calculated from the finalized rankings and reward pool.
7. Rewards are distributed to eligible participants.

The rankings shown while a reward period is active are estimates. The final reward amounts are determined from the permanent snapshot taken when the period ends.

## Reward Categories

There are two reward categories:

- **Buyer Rewards** — for users who make qualifying token purchases.
- **Creator Rewards** — for creators who successfully migrate bonding-curve liquidity.

Each category has its own ranking.

## Reward Pool Allocation

The current reward pool is divided as follows:

| Category | Allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Buyers | 60% |
| Creators | 40% |

This means that 60% of the available reward pool is reserved for eligible buyers, while 40% is reserved for eligible creators.

## Who Can Earn Rewards?

### Buyers

Buyer rewards are based on qualifying purchase volume during the reward period.

At the end of the period, the **top 100 buyers** by qualifying purchase volume are eligible for buyer rewards.

### Creators

Creator rewards are based on successful bonding-curve migrations during the reward period.

At the end of the period, the **top 100 creators** by total successful migrations are eligible for creator rewards.

A creator must have at least one successful migration during the reward period to qualify for the creator ranking.

## 28-Day Reward Periods

Each reward period lasts **28 days**.

Activity belongs to the reward period in which it occurs. When the period ends, the final rankings and qualifying activity are captured in a permanent snapshot.

After the snapshot is finalized, later activity does not change the completed period's rankings or rewards.

## Rankings During an Active Period

Rankings are updated periodically while a reward period is active.

The displayed rankings are intended to provide an estimate of the current standings. They can change as additional qualifying activity occurs.

The current estimated rankings and rewards are updated approximately every **60 seconds**.

The displayed values should therefore not be treated as final until the reward period has ended and its final snapshot has been established.

## Final Reward Snapshot

When a 28-day reward period ends, RugFreePump creates the final reward snapshot.

The snapshot records the qualifying activity, final rankings, and reward allocation for that period.

Once finalized, the snapshot is permanent.

This ensures that rewards for a completed period are based on a fixed set of results rather than continuously changing platform activity.

## Reward Calculation

The reward pool is based on qualifying RugFreePump platform revenue generated during the reward period.

The revenue included in the reward pool currently consists of:

- Bonding Curve Escrow Creation Fees
- Buy Fees
- Bonding Curve Escrow Completed Fees

Certain platform fees are excluded from the reward pool, including:

- Token Creator Incentive
- Raydium Liquidity Pool Creation Fee

The resulting reward pool is then divided between the buyer and creator categories according to the current allocation.
