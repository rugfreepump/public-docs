# Reward Periods & Rankings

The RugFreePump Reward Program operates in recurring **28-day reward periods**.

Each period independently measures qualifying buyer and creator activity. When the period ends, the final results are permanently recorded for reward calculation and distribution.

## 28-Day Reward Period

Each reward period lasts 28 days.

During the active period:

- Buyer activity contributes to buyer rankings.
- Successful creator migrations contribute to creator rankings.
- Rankings are periodically updated.
- Estimated rewards may change as activity changes.

When the 28-day period ends, the active ranking is closed and the final reward snapshot is established.

## Buyer Ranking

Buyers are ranked according to their **qualifying purchase volume** during the reward period.

The buyer ranking determines which participants qualify for the buyer reward pool.

Only the **top 100 buyers** at the end of the reward period are eligible for buyer rewards.

The final ranking is based on the qualifying activity captured in the period's final snapshot.

## Creator Ranking

Creators are ranked according to the **total number of successful bonding-curve migrations** completed during the reward period.

Only creators with at least one successful migration during the period can qualify.

The **top 100 creators** at the end of the reward period are eligible for creator rewards.

## Creator Tie-Breakers

When creators have the same number of successful migrations, RugFreePump applies additional criteria to determine the final order.

The tie-breakers are:

1. Buy volume
2. Wallet key ordering

These rules provide a deterministic ranking when the primary migration count is equal.

## Ranking Updates

Active rankings are updated approximately every **60 seconds**.

This means the ranking displayed during a reward period may not immediately reflect the most recent qualifying activity.

Rankings can change at any time before the period ends.

For example, a participant who is currently ranked within the top 100 may fall below the eligibility threshold if other participants generate more qualifying activity.

## Final Snapshot

At the end of the 28-day period, RugFreePump records a permanent snapshot of the reward period.

The final snapshot establishes:

- Qualifying buyer activity
- Qualifying creator activity
- Final buyer rankings
- Final creator rankings
- Eligible participants
- Reward allocations

The final snapshot is the authoritative result for that reward period.

## Final Results Cannot Be Changed by Later Activity

Once a reward period has been finalized, activity from a later period does not affect its results.

For example, if a buyer finishes a reward period in 25th place, additional purchases made during the following reward period do not change the completed period's 25th-place ranking.

Each reward period is therefore evaluated independently.

## Understanding the Ranking Display

While a reward period is active, the displayed ranking should be considered an estimate.

The final result is only established when the reward period ends and the final snapshot is created.

This distinction is important because:

- Rankings can change before the period ends.
- Estimated rewards can change.
- Being inside the top 100 during an active period does not guarantee a final top-100 position.
- The final snapshot determines the actual reward entitlement.

For information about how the reward amounts are calculated from the finalized results, see [Reward Calculation](/reward-program/reward-calculation).
