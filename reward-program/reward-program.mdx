# Reward Distribution

After a 28-day reward period ends, RugFreePump finalizes the reward snapshot and calculates the rewards owed to eligible participants.

Rewards are distributed based on the finalized results.

## When Rewards Are Distributed

Rewards are distributed **after the reward period has been finalized**.

The final snapshot must be established before distribution can begin because the snapshot determines:

- Eligible buyers
- Eligible creators
- Final rankings
- Final reward amounts

This prevents later activity from changing rewards that have already been finalized.

## Distribution Process

The general distribution process is:

1. The 28-day reward period ends.
2. The final reward snapshot is established.
3. The reward pool and participant rewards are finalized.
4. Eligible rewards are prepared for distribution.
5. Rewards are distributed to eligible participants.
6. Distribution transactions are recorded.

Distribution may be processed in multiple controlled batches.

This allows reward distributions to be handled reliably even when many participants are eligible.

## Buyer Rewards

Eligible buyer rewards are distributed to participants included in the finalized buyer reward results.

Eligibility is based on the final buyer ranking for the completed reward period.

Only the **top 100 buyers** are eligible for the buyer reward pool.

## Creator Rewards

Eligible creator rewards are distributed to participants included in the finalized creator reward results.

Eligibility is based on the final creator ranking for the completed reward period.

Only the **top 100 creators** are eligible for the creator reward pool.

A creator must have at least one successful migration during the reward period to qualify.

## Distribution Records

Reward distributions are recorded with the relevant transaction information.

This provides a verifiable record of the distribution activity and allows completed reward payments to be associated with their on-chain transactions.

Where applicable, users can use the recorded transaction information to verify the distribution on the Solana network.

## Finalized Rewards Are Independent of Later Activity

Once a reward period has been finalized, later activity does not change the rewards belonging to that completed period.

For example, if a participant receives a finalized reward for one 28-day period, activity performed during the following period does not change that previously finalized reward.

Each reward period has its own independent final result.

## Estimated vs. Final Rewards

The reward information displayed during an active period may be estimated.

Active rankings can change before the period ends, and the reward pool can continue to change as qualifying platform revenue is generated.

Only the finalized reward snapshot determines the actual reward entitlement.

## Important

Participation in the Reward Program does not guarantee that a user will receive a reward.

To receive a reward, a participant must satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements and be included in the final reward results for the relevant period.

The Reward Program's allocations, qualifying revenue sources, eligibility rules, and other parameters may be updated by the protocol according to its current configuration.

For the calculation methodology, see [Reward Calculation](/reward-program/reward-calculation).
