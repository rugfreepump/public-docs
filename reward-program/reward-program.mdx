# Reward Program

The RugFreePump Reward Program distributes a portion of eligible platform revenue to participants who contribute qualifying activity to the platform.

The program has two independent reward categories:

* **Buyers** who accumulate qualifying SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales
* **Creators** who successfully migrate raised liquidity to Raydium CPMM after reaching the bonding-curve hard cap

Buyer and creator rewards are calculated separately for each reward period.

<Note>
The number of qualifying participants for each reward category is determined before the corresponding reward period begins. The qualification count is based on the reward strategy established for that round and is not changed while the round is in progress.
</Note>

## You do not need to use the RugFreePump interface

Participation in the Reward Program is based on qualifying activity recorded from the **RugFreePump on-chain program**, not on whether the activity was performed through the RugFreePump website.

This means you do **not** need to use the RugFreePump interface to have qualifying activity.

For example, qualifying activity can originate from:

* The RugFreePump interface
* External trading interfaces
* Sniping bots
* Other third-party services
* Direct interaction with the RugFreePump on-chain program

As long as the relevant activity interacts with the RugFreePump on-chain program, the transaction data required for the Reward Program can be detected and recorded for reward calculations.

The Reward Program therefore focuses on **on-chain participation**, rather than requiring participants to use a particular interface.

<Note>
Using an external service does not automatically exclude your activity from the Reward Program. The important factor is whether the relevant activity interacts with the RugFreePump on-chain program and produces the qualifying on-chain data required by the program.
</Note>

## Reward categories

### Buyers

The **Buyers Reward Pool** rewards participants according to the SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the reward period.

The buyer ranking is based on:

1. Higher qualifying SOL purchase volume
2. Wallet address as a deterministic tie-breaker

Here, **qualifying SOL purchase volume** means the SOL volume used for qualifying purchases of tokens through RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the applicable reward period.

Buyer rewards are calculated proportionally from the Buyers Reward Pool based on each finalized buyer's qualifying SOL purchase volume.

<Note>
Creators are also eligible for the Buyers Reward Pool. Being a creator does not prevent a wallet from qualifying for buyer rewards when the wallet has qualifying SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales.
</Note>

### Creators

The **Creators Reward Pool** rewards creators who successfully migrate the raised liquidity from a completed bonding-curve sale to **Raydium CPMM**.

A creator becomes eligible for the creator category when their bonding-curve sale reaches its hard cap and the raised liquidity is successfully migrated to Raydium CPMM.

Creators are ranked according to:

1. Higher number of successful liquidity migrations
2. Higher qualifying SOL purchase volume
3. Wallet address as a deterministic final tie-breaker

A creator must complete at least one successful liquidity migration during the reward period to qualify.

<Note>
Qualifying SOL purchase volume is used only as a creator ranking tie-breaker when creators have the same number of successful liquidity migrations. It does not determine the creator reward amount.
</Note>

## How the qualification count is determined

The number of qualifying participants is established **before each reward period begins**.

RugFreePump reviews historical platform activity and other relevant information when determining an appropriate qualification count for the upcoming reward period.

The objective is to establish a qualification count that balances:

1. Allowing a meaningful number of participants to qualify
2. Maintaining meaningful reward amounts for qualified participants

The qualification count can therefore differ between reward periods.

Once the next reward period begins, its qualification count is already established and is not dynamically changed based on activity during that same round.

The qualification count for the upcoming or current reward period can be communicated through:

* The RugFreePump Reward Program page
* RugFreePump announcements
* Official RugFreePump community channels
* Official communications from RugFreePump admins or moderators

Participants should always refer to the information published for the specific reward period rather than assuming that a previous round's qualification count will remain the same.

<Note>
The qualification count is determined before the reward period starts. Activity during the round can change rankings and estimated rewards, but it does not change the predetermined number of qualifying participants for that round.
</Note>

## Reward period

Each reward period lasts:

**28 days**

Qualifying activity and eligible platform revenue are measured independently for each reward period.

When a reward period reaches its finalization point:

1. Final buyer rankings are determined
2. Final creator rankings are determined
3. Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
4. Reward pools are calculated
5. The predetermined qualification count is applied to the final rankings
6. Final reward amounts are calculated
7. A permanent reward snapshot is created
8. Distribution records are prepared
9. The next reward period begins

Activity from one reward period does not carry forward into the next.

For example:

* Qualifying SOL volume used to purchase tokens during one reward period belongs to that period
* Successful liquidity migrations completed during that period belong to that period
* Activity generated during the next reward period is measured independently

## Live leaderboard

During an active reward period, RugFreePump periodically updates the reward leaderboard.

The live leaderboard can show:

* Current buyer rankings
* Current creator rankings
* Qualifying SOL purchase volume
* Qualifying migration counts
* Estimated reward amounts

Temporary leaderboard snapshots are refreshed approximately every:

**60 seconds**

Because the reward period remains active, the displayed information can change.

The live leaderboard is intended to provide current visibility into the reward period. It does not represent the final reward result.

The predetermined qualification count remains unchanged throughout the round.

## Estimated rewards are not final

A wallet appearing in the live leaderboard does not guarantee that it will receive a reward.

During an active reward period:

* Rankings can change
* Participants can move higher or lower in the ranking
* Participants can move into or outside the predetermined qualifying range
* Estimated reward amounts can increase or decrease
* Eligible revenue can increase
* Reward pool sizes can change

The qualification count itself does not change during the round.

These changes occur because participant activity and eligible platform revenue continue to change throughout the reward period.

Only the permanent reward snapshot determines final qualification and reward amounts.

<Warning>
A temporary leaderboard position or estimated reward should not be treated as a guaranteed final reward.
</Warning>

## Permanent reward snapshot

At the end of each reward period, RugFreePump creates a permanent reward snapshot.

The permanent snapshot records the finalized information for that reward period, including:

* Reward period
* Snapshot date
* Reward period length
* Eligible platform revenue
* Buyers Reward Pool
* Creators Reward Pool
* Qualification count
* Qualified participants
* Final rankings
* Qualifying activity
* Final reward amounts
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

Once the permanent reward snapshot is created, the finalized reward amounts for that period are fixed.

The permanent reward snapshot is the source of truth for the completed reward period.

## Buyers leaderboard

The Buyers leaderboard ranks wallets according to their qualifying SOL purchase volume from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the current reward period.

The ranking order is:

1. Higher qualifying SOL purchase volume
2. Wallet address as a deterministic tie-breaker

Only wallets with qualifying token purchase activity during the reward period can qualify for the Buyers Reward Pool.

The predetermined Buyers qualification count determines how many of the highest-ranked eligible buyers qualify for that reward period.

## What counts toward buyer volume

Buyer qualification is based on the total SOL volume used for qualifying purchases of tokens through RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Qualifying SOL purchase volume = Total SOL volume used for qualifying bonding-curve token purchases during the current reward period**

Only qualifying purchase activity accumulated during the current reward period is used.

Activity from previous reward periods does not contribute to the current period's qualifying SOL purchase volume.

Because qualifying activity is derived from the RugFreePump on-chain program, qualifying token purchases can be made through the RugFreePump interface or through external services that interact with the program.

## Creators leaderboard

The Creators leaderboard ranks creators according to successful liquidity migrations completed during the current reward period.

A qualifying creator migration occurs when:

1. The bonding-curve sale reaches its hard cap
2. The raised liquidity is successfully migrated to **Raydium CPMM**

The ranking order is:

1. Higher number of successful liquidity migrations
2. Higher qualifying SOL purchase volume
3. Wallet address as a deterministic final tie-breaker

Only creators with at least one successful liquidity migration during the reward period can qualify for the Creators Reward Pool.

The predetermined Creators qualification count determines how many of the highest-ranked eligible creators qualify for that reward period.

## What counts toward creator migrations

Creator qualification is based on successful liquidity migrations completed during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Qualifying migrations = Successful liquidity migrations completed during the current reward period**

Only successful migrations completed during the current reward period count toward that period.

Successful migrations from previous reward periods do not contribute to the current period's qualifying migration count.

## Creator ranking tie-breaker

The number of successful liquidity migrations is the primary factor used to rank creators.

If two or more creators have the same migration count, their qualifying SOL purchase volume is compared.

For example:

| Creator   | Successful migrations | Qualifying SOL purchase volume | Result        |
| --------- | --------------------: | -----------------------------: | ------------- |
| Creator A |                     5 |                         Higher | Ranked higher |
| Creator B |                     5 |                          Lower | Ranked lower  |

If both migration count and qualifying SOL purchase volume are equal, the wallet address is used as the final deterministic tie-breaker.

This ensures that creators with identical qualifying activity still have a consistent ranking order.

## Platform revenue used for rewards

Reward pools are calculated from eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period.

The following revenue categories are included:

* Bonding Curve Escrow Creation Fee
* Buy Fee
* Bonding Curve Escrow Completed Fee

The following revenue categories are not included in Reward Program calculations:

* Token Creator Incentive
* Raydium Liquidity Pool Creation Fee

Eligible reward-period revenue is the amount of eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Eligible reward-period revenue = Eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period**

The resulting amount is used to calculate the Buyers and Creators Reward Pools.

<Note>
The Reward Program percentages apply only to eligible reward-period platform revenue. Revenue categories excluded above do not contribute to the reward pools.
</Note>

## Reward pool allocation

The Reward Program currently allocates **10% of eligible reward-period platform revenue** to rewards.

The allocation is:

| Reward category             | Allocation |
| --------------------------- | ---------: |
| Buyers Reward Pool          |         6% |
| Creators Reward Pool        |         4% |
| **Total Reward Allocation** |    **10%** |

Within the total 10% reward allocation:

* Buyers receive 60%
* Creators receive 40%

The two reward pools are calculated independently.

## Buyers reward calculation

The Buyers Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to each finalized buyer's qualifying SOL purchase volume.

The calculation is:

**Buyer Reward = Buyers Reward Pool × Buyer Qualifying SOL Purchase Volume ÷ Total Qualifying SOL Purchase Volume of All Finalized Buyers**

### Example

Assume:

* Buyers Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total qualifying SOL purchase volume of all finalized buyers: **1,000 SOL**
* Your qualifying SOL purchase volume: **100 SOL**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 100 SOL ÷ 1,000 SOL = 1 SOL**

Your reward depends on your qualifying SOL purchase volume relative to the combined qualifying SOL purchase volume of all finalized buyers.

Therefore, leaderboard position alone does not determine the exact reward amount.

## Creators reward calculation

The Creators Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to each finalized creator's qualifying successful liquidity migration count.

The calculation is:

**Creator Reward = Creators Reward Pool × Creator Qualifying Migration Count ÷ Total Qualifying Migration Count of All Finalized Creators**

### Example

Assume:

* Creators Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total qualifying migrations of all finalized creators: **100**
* Your qualifying migrations: **10**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 10 ÷ 100 = 1 SOL**

Your reward depends on your qualifying migration count relative to the combined qualifying migration count of all finalized creators.

## Purchase volume does not determine creator rewards

Qualifying SOL purchase volume can affect creator ranking when multiple creators have the same number of successful liquidity migrations.

However, creator reward amounts are calculated using:

**Qualifying migration count**

not qualifying SOL purchase volume.

For example, if two creators each complete five successful liquidity migrations, the creator with higher qualifying SOL purchase volume is ranked higher.

However, the reward amount for each creator is still calculated from their qualifying migration count.

## Reward size and platform growth

RugFreePump's Reward Program is designed with a focus on meaningful rewards for qualified participants as the platform grows.

The platform aims to balance:

* The number of participants who can qualify
* The reward amount available to each qualified participant
* Eligible platform revenue generated during the reward period
* Overall platform activity and growth

As eligible revenue grows, larger reward pools can become available.

The qualification count can also be adjusted between reward periods based on historical platform data and the reward strategy established for the upcoming period.

The long-term objective is to build a reward system where successful participants can receive increasingly substantial rewards as the platform grows, rather than simply distributing small amounts across a very large number of participants.

<Note>
RugFreePump aims to make rewards meaningful as the platform grows, but reward amounts are never guaranteed. Final rewards depend on actual eligible revenue, qualifying activity, final rankings, and the qualification count established for the applicable reward period.
</Note>

## Why estimated rewards change

Estimated reward amounts can change throughout an active reward period because participant activity and eligible platform revenue continue to change.

### Eligible platform revenue changes

As eligible platform revenue increases, the Buyers and Creators Reward Pools can increase.

### Buyer activity changes

Existing buyers can increase their qualifying SOL purchase volume and change their rankings.

New participants can also enter the ranking or move into the predetermined qualifying range.

### Creator activity changes

Creators can complete additional successful liquidity migrations and change their rankings.

New creators can enter the ranking after completing a successful liquidity migration.

### Qualification count remains fixed

The number of qualifying participants was established before the reward period began.

It does not increase or decrease during the active round.

As participant rankings change, however, different wallets may move into or outside the qualifying range.

Because of these factors, an estimated reward displayed during an active reward period is not guaranteed.

Only the permanent reward snapshot determines the final reward.

## End of the reward period

When a reward period reaches its scheduled finalization point, RugFreePump finalizes the completed period.

During finalization:

* Final buyer rankings are determined
* Final creator rankings are determined
* Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
* The Buyers Reward Pool is calculated
* The Creators Reward Pool is calculated
* The predetermined qualification count is applied to the final rankings
* Final reward amounts are calculated
* The permanent reward snapshot is created
* Distribution records are prepared
* The next reward period begins

The completed period is thereby separated from the new reward period.

## Snapshot consistency

The final reward calculation is based on a consistent state for the completed reward period.

The following information is finalized together:

* Buyer rankings
* Creator rankings
* Qualifying activity
* Eligible reward-period revenue
* Reward pools
* Qualification count
* Individual reward amounts

This ensures that the permanent snapshot represents the completed reward period rather than a mixture of activity from different periods.

After finalization, the new reward period begins and the live leaderboard resumes for the new period.

## Reward distribution

After a permanent reward snapshot has been created, the finalized rewards become available for distribution.

Reward distribution is performed manually through the RugFreePump distribution process.

Participants do not need to submit a separate reward claim transaction.

Distribution timing can vary depending on operational conditions.

Rewards may be distributed:

* On the same day as the permanent snapshot
* On the following day
* Within several days in rare cases

RugFreePump does not guarantee a specific payout time.

However, reward distribution is designed to remain transparent and trackable.

Once rewards are distributed, the relevant distribution information is recorded and made available for verification.

## Distribution batches

Reward distributions can be processed in controlled batches.

The current distribution process supports up to:

**17 reward transfers per batch**

Buyer rewards and creator rewards are processed through separate distribution batches.

Batching allows larger reward distributions to be processed in an organized manner while providing a clear record of each distribution.

## Reward distribution status

The reward history can show the distribution status of finalized rewards.

For an individual participant, this can include:

* Whether the reward has been distributed
* Distribution timestamp
* Distribution transaction signature

A reward that has not yet been distributed may show no distribution time or transaction signature.

Once a reward has been distributed, the associated transaction information can be used to verify the payment.

## Understanding "Fully Rewarded"

A completed reward period may display whether its rewards are **Fully Rewarded**.

**Fully Rewarded** means that all finalized rewards for that reward category have been distributed.

A category may therefore show that it is not yet fully rewarded even though:

* The reward period has already been finalized
* Participant rankings are final
* Reward amounts are final
* Some participants have already received their rewards
* Other participants are still awaiting distribution

Buyer and creator reward categories are tracked independently.

As a result, the Buyers category and Creators category for the same reward period can have different distribution statuses.

## Reward history

After a reward period has been finalized, RugFreePump provides historical information for the completed period.

The historical reward information can include:

* Reward period number
* Snapshot date
* Reward period length
* Eligible revenue
* Reward percentage
* Reward pool amount
* Number of qualified participants
* Whether the reward category is fully rewarded

The historical leaderboard can show each finalized participant's:

* Rank
* Wallet
* Qualifying activity
* Final reward
* Distribution status
* Distribution time
* Distribution transaction

This allows completed reward periods to remain transparent and independently verifiable.

## All Time Rewarded

The Reward Program interface can also display **All Time Rewarded**.

This represents the cumulative amount of Reward Program rewards that have been distributed across completed reward periods.

It provides a high-level view of the total rewards that have already been paid through the program.

## Separate reward categories

Buyer and creator rewards are independent.

A wallet can potentially qualify in both categories during the same reward period.

For example, the same wallet may:

* Accumulate qualifying SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales and qualify for the Buyers Reward Pool
* Create a token and successfully migrate its raised liquidity to Raydium CPMM after the bonding curve reaches its hard cap

If a wallet qualifies in both categories, its buyer and creator rewards are calculated independently from their respective reward pools.

The distribution status of the two rewards can also differ.

## Verifying a reward

After a reward has been distributed, its associated transaction signature can be used to verify the distribution on the Solana blockchain.

The permanent reward snapshot provides the finalized record of:

* The reward period
* The participant's final ranking
* Qualifying activity
* Final reward amount
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

This allows participants to independently verify completed reward distributions on-chain.

The RugFreePump Reward Program operates in repeating **28-day reward periods**.

### Before a reward period begins

* Historical platform data is reviewed
* The reward strategy for the upcoming period is established
* The number of qualifying Buyers is determined
* The number of qualifying Creators is determined
* The applicable qualification information is communicated through official RugFreePump channels

### During the active reward period

* Qualifying SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales is tracked
* Successful liquidity migrations are tracked
* Eligible platform revenue is tracked
* Temporary leaderboards are updated approximately every 60 seconds
* Estimated reward amounts are recalculated
* Participants can monitor the current state of the reward period
* Qualifying activity can come from the RugFreePump interface or external services interacting with the on-chain program
* Creators can independently qualify for both buyer and creator rewards
* The predetermined qualification counts remain unchanged

### At finalization

* Buyer rankings are finalized
* Creator rankings are finalized
* Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
* Reward pools are calculated
* The predetermined qualification counts are applied to the final rankings
* Final reward amounts are calculated
* A permanent reward snapshot is created
* Distribution records are prepared

### After finalization

* Finalized rewards become available for manual distribution
* Distribution is processed through controlled batches
* Distribution status is recorded
* Distribution timestamps are recorded
* Transaction information is recorded
* The completed reward period remains available as historical reward data
* The next reward period begins with its own predetermined qualification counts
