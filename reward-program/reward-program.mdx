# Reward Program

The RugFreePump Reward Program distributes a portion of eligible platform revenue to participants who contribute activity to the platform.

The program has two independent reward categories:

* **Buyers** who accumulate SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales
* **Creators** who successfully migrate raised liquidity to Raydium CPMM after reaching the bonding-curve sales hard cap

Buyer and creator rewards are calculated separately for each reward period.

## Participants and Qualified Participant Count

A **participant** is any wallet that performs the applicable activity during the current reward period.

For Buyers, a wallet becomes a participant when it purchases tokens through RugFreePump bonding-curve sales.

For Creators, a wallet becomes a participant when it completes at least one successful liquidity migration to Raydium CPMM during the reward period.

There can be many more participants than participants who ultimately receive rewards.

The **Qualified Participant Count** is the predetermined number of highest-ranked participants who can ultimately qualify for rewards in each category when the reward period is finalized.

The count is established **before each reward period begins** and remains fixed throughout that reward period.

For example, if a reward period has:

* 2,000 participating buyers
* A Qualified Participant Count of 100

then all 2,000 buyers are participants, but only the **100 highest-ranked buyers at finalization** qualify for the Buyers Reward Pool.

The same principle applies independently to creators.

RugFreePump reviews historical platform activity and other relevant information when establishing an appropriate **Qualified Participant Count** for the upcoming reward period.

The objective is to balance:

1. Allowing a meaningful number of participants to qualify
2. Maintaining meaningful reward amounts for qualified participants

The Qualified Participant Count can therefore differ between reward periods.

It is **not dynamically increased or decreased** based on activity during the active reward period.

What can change during the reward period is the ranking of participants:

* New participants can enter the leaderboard
* Existing participants can move higher or lower
* Participants can move into the current qualifying range
* Participants can move outside the current qualifying range

The number of qualifying positions remains unchanged.

<Note>
The Qualified Participant Count represents the number of participants who can ultimately qualify for rewards in that category. It does not represent the total number of participants in the reward period.
</Note>

## You do not need to use the RugFreePump interface

Participation in the Reward Program is based on activity recorded directly by the **RugFreePump on-chain program**, rather than whether the action was performed through the official RugFreePump web interface.

You do **not** need to use the RugFreePump web interface to participate in the Reward Program.

For example, activity can originate from:

* The official RugFreePump interface
* External web applications and user interfaces
* Automated bots and launch scripts
* Third-party ecosystem services
* Direct interaction with the RugFreePump on-chain program

As long as the relevant activity interacts with the RugFreePump on-chain program and generates the required on-chain data, that activity will be included in the Reward Program calculations.

The Reward Program focuses strictly on on-chain participation, rather than requiring participants to utilize a specific frontend application.

<Note>
Using an external service does not exclude your activity from the Reward Program. The determining factor is whether the transaction interacts with the RugFreePump on-chain program and produces the required on-chain data used by the system.
</Note>

## Reward categories

### Buyers

The **Buyers Reward Pool** rewards participants according to the SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the reward period.

For the Buyers category, **purchase volume** means the total SOL volume used to purchase tokens through RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the applicable reward period.

Buyers are ranked by higher purchase volume.

The predetermined Buyers Qualified Participant Count determines how many of the highest-ranked buyer participants ultimately qualify for the Buyers Reward Pool.

Buyer rewards are calculated proportionally from the Buyers Reward Pool based on each finalized buyer's purchase volume.

<Note>
Creators are also eligible to participate in the Buyers category. Being a creator does not prevent a wallet from qualifying for buyer rewards when the wallet has SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales.
</Note>

### Creators

The **Creators Reward Pool** rewards creators who successfully migrate the raised liquidity from a completed bonding-curve sale to **Raydium CPMM**.

A creator becomes a participant in the Creators category when their bonding-curve sale reaches its hard cap and the raised liquidity is successfully migrated to Raydium CPMM.

Creators are ranked by:

1. Higher number of successful liquidity migrations
2. Higher purchase volume when migration counts are equal

The predetermined Creators Qualified Participant Count determines how many of the highest-ranked creator participants ultimately qualify for the Creators Reward Pool.

Purchase volume is used only to distinguish creators who have the same number of successful migrations. It does not determine the creator reward amount.

## Reward period

Each reward period lasts:

**28 days**

Participant activity and eligible platform revenue are measured independently for each reward period.

Only activity generated during the current reward period contributes to that period.

For example:

* SOL volume used to purchase tokens during one reward period belongs to that period
* Successful liquidity migrations completed during that period belong to that period
* Activity generated during the next reward period is measured independently

Activity from one reward period does not carry forward into the next.

When a reward period reaches its finalization point:

1. Final buyer rankings are determined
2. Final creator rankings are determined
3. Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
4. Reward pools are calculated
5. The predetermined Qualified Participant Counts are applied to the final rankings
6. Final qualified participants are determined
7. Final reward amounts are calculated
8. A permanent reward snapshot is created
9. Distribution records are prepared
10. The next reward period begins

## Live leaderboard and estimated rewards

During an active reward period, RugFreePump periodically updates the reward leaderboard.

The live leaderboard can show:

* Current buyer rankings
* Current creator rankings
* SOL purchase volume
* Migration counts
* Estimated reward amounts

Temporary leaderboard snapshots are refreshed approximately every:

**60 seconds**

The live leaderboard can contain participants who are currently outside the qualifying range as well as participants who are currently inside it.

For example, if there are 2,000 buyer participants and the Buyers Qualified Participant Count is 100, the live leaderboard can show the current top 100 positions while the remaining participants continue to compete for those positions.

Because the reward period remains active:

* Rankings can change
* Participants can move higher or lower
* Participants can enter or leave the current qualifying range
* Estimated reward amounts can increase or decrease
* Eligible revenue can increase
* Reward pool sizes can change

The Qualified Participant Count itself does not change during the round.

The live leaderboard does not represent the final reward result.

<Warning>
A temporary leaderboard position or estimated reward should not be treated as a guaranteed final reward.
</Warning>

## Buyer activity and ranking

The Buyers leaderboard ranks participants according to their SOL purchase volume from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales during the current reward period.

Higher purchase volume results in a higher ranking.

Only wallets with token purchase activity during the reward period become buyer participants.

In simplified form:

**Purchase volume = Total SOL volume used for bonding-curve token purchases during the current reward period**

Only purchase activity accumulated during the current reward period is used.

Activity from previous reward periods does not contribute to the current period's purchase volume.

The Reward Program measures this activity in SOL volume. It does not convert purchase activity into USD-equivalent volume for ranking or reward calculations.

Because activity is derived from the RugFreePump on-chain program, token purchases can be made through the RugFreePump interface or through external services that interact with the program.

The predetermined Buyers Qualified Participant Count determines how many of the highest-ranked buyer participants ultimately qualify for the Buyers Reward Pool.

The live leaderboard therefore represents the current ranking of participants against a fixed number of qualifying positions.

## Creator activity and ranking

The Creators leaderboard ranks creator participants according to successful liquidity migrations completed during the current reward period.

A creator migration occurs when:

1. The bonding-curve sale reaches its hard cap
2. The raised liquidity is successfully migrated to **Raydium CPMM**

In simplified form:

**Migrations = Successful liquidity migrations completed during the current reward period**

Only successful migrations completed during the current reward period count toward that period.

Successful migrations from previous reward periods do not contribute to the current period's migration count.

The ranking order is:

1. Higher number of successful liquidity migrations
2. Higher purchase volume when migration counts are equal

For example:

| Creator   | Successful migrations | SOL purchase volume | Result        |
| --------- | --------------------: | ------------------: | ------------- |
| Creator A |                     5 |              Higher | Ranked higher |
| Creator B |                     5 |               Lower | Ranked lower  |

The secondary comparison is used only to establish the ranking between creators with the same migration count.

It does not affect how creator rewards are calculated.

Only creators with at least one successful liquidity migration during the reward period become creator participants.

The predetermined Creators Qualified Participant Count determines how many of the highest-ranked creator participants ultimately qualify for the Creators Reward Pool.

## Platform revenue and reward pool allocation

Reward pools are calculated from eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period.

The following revenue categories are included:

* Bonding Curve Escrow Creation Fee
* Buy Fee
* Bonding Curve Escrow Completed Fee

The following revenue categories are not included in Reward Program calculations:

* Token Creator Incentive
* Raydium Liquidity Pool Creation Fee

In simplified form:

**Eligible reward-period revenue = Eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period**

The resulting amount is used to calculate the Buyers and Creators Reward Pools.

<Note>
The Reward Program percentages apply only to eligible reward-period platform revenue. Revenue categories excluded above do not contribute to the reward pools.
</Note>

The Reward Program currently allocates **10% of eligible reward-period platform revenue** to rewards.

The allocation is:

| Reward category             | Allocation |
| --------------------------- | ---------: |
| Buyers Reward Pool          |         6% |
| Creators Reward Pool        |         4% |
| **Total Reward Allocation** |    **10%** |

Within the total 10% reward allocation:

* Buyers receive 60%
* Creators receive 40%

The two reward pools are calculated independently.

## Reward calculation

### Buyers

The Buyers Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to each finalized buyer's SOL purchase volume.

The calculation is:

**Buyer Reward = Buyers Reward Pool × Buyer Purchase Volume ÷ Total Purchase Volume of All Finalized Buyers**

#### Example

Assume:

* Buyers Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total purchase volume of all finalized buyers: **1,000 SOL**
* Your purchase volume: **100 SOL**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 100 SOL ÷ 1,000 SOL = 1 SOL**

Your reward depends on your purchase volume relative to the combined purchase volume of all finalized buyers.

Therefore, leaderboard position alone does not determine the exact reward amount.

### Creators

The Creators Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to each finalized creator's successful liquidity migration count.

The calculation is:

**Creator Reward = Creators Reward Pool × Creator Migration Count ÷ Total Migration Count of All Finalized Creators**

#### Example

Assume:

* Creators Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total migrations of all finalized creators: **100**
* Your migrations: **10**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 10 ÷ 100 = 1 SOL**

Your reward depends on your migration count relative to the combined migration count of all finalized creators.

Purchase volume can affect creator ranking when multiple creators have the same number of successful liquidity migrations.

However, creator reward amounts are calculated using:

**Migration count**

not purchase volume.

For example, if two creators each complete five successful liquidity migrations, the creator with higher purchase volume is ranked higher.

However, the reward amount for each creator is still calculated from their migration count.

## Reward size and changing estimates

RugFreePump's Reward Program is designed to balance the number of participants who can qualify with meaningful rewards for qualified participants as the platform grows.

The program considers:

* The number of participants who can qualify
* The reward amount available to qualified participants
* Eligible platform revenue generated during the reward period
* Overall platform growth

As eligible revenue grows, larger reward pools can become available.

The Qualified Participant Count can also differ between reward periods based on historical platform data and the reward strategy established for the upcoming period.

The long-term objective is to build a reward system where successful participants can receive increasingly substantial rewards as the platform grows, rather than simply distributing small amounts across an unnecessarily large number of participants.

Estimated reward amounts during an active reward period can change because:

* Eligible platform revenue continues to change
* Buyer purchase volume can change
* New buyer participants can enter
* Buyers can move into or outside the qualifying range
* Creators can complete additional successful migrations
* New creators can enter after completing a successful migration
* Participant rankings can change
* Reward pool sizes can change

The Qualified Participant Count remains fixed during the active reward period.

<Note>
RugFreePump aims to make rewards meaningful as the platform grows, but reward amounts are not guaranteed. Final rewards depend on actual eligible revenue, participant activity, final rankings, and the Qualified Participant Count established for the applicable reward period.
</Note>

## Permanent reward snapshot and finalization

At the end of each reward period, RugFreePump creates a permanent reward snapshot.

At finalization, the predetermined Qualified Participant Count is applied to the final rankings.

The participants occupying the qualifying positions at that moment become the finalized qualified participants for that category.

The permanent snapshot records the finalized information for that reward period, including:

* Reward period
* Snapshot date
* Reward period length
* Eligible platform revenue
* Buyers Reward Pool
* Creators Reward Pool
* Qualified Participant Count
* Qualified participants
* Final rankings
* Participant activity
* Final reward amounts
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

The final reward calculation is based on a consistent state for the completed reward period.

The following information is finalized together:

* Buyer rankings
* Creator rankings
* Participant activity
* Eligible reward-period revenue
* Reward pools
* Qualified Participant Count
* Individual reward amounts

This ensures that the permanent snapshot represents the completed reward period rather than a mixture of activity from different periods.

Once the permanent reward snapshot is created, the finalized reward amounts for that period are fixed.

The permanent reward snapshot is the source of truth for the completed reward period.

## Reward distribution

After a permanent reward snapshot has been created, the finalized rewards become available for distribution.

Reward distribution is performed manually through the RugFreePump distribution process.

Participants do not need to submit a separate reward claim transaction.

Distribution timing can vary depending on operational conditions.

Rewards may be distributed:

* On the same day as the permanent snapshot
* On the following day
* Within several days in rare cases

RugFreePump does not guarantee a specific payout time.

However, reward distribution is designed to remain transparent and trackable.

Reward distributions can be processed in controlled batches.

The current distribution process supports up to:

**17 reward transfers per batch**

Buyer rewards and creator rewards are processed through separate distribution batches.

Batching allows larger reward distributions to be processed in an organized manner while providing a clear record of each distribution.

## Reward distribution status

The reward history can show the distribution status of finalized rewards.

For an individual participant, this can include:

* Whether the reward has been distributed
* Distribution timestamp
* Distribution transaction signature

A reward that has not yet been distributed may show no distribution time or transaction signature.

Once a reward has been distributed, the associated transaction information can be used to verify the payment.

A completed reward period may display whether its rewards are **Fully Rewarded**.

**Fully Rewarded** means that all finalized rewards for that reward category have been distributed.

A category may therefore show that it is not yet fully rewarded even though:

* The reward period has already been finalized
* Participant rankings are final
* Reward amounts are final
* Some participants have already received their rewards
* Other participants are still awaiting distribution

Buyer and creator reward categories are tracked independently.

As a result, the Buyers category and Creators category for the same reward period can have different distribution statuses.

## Reward history and verification

After a reward period has been finalized, RugFreePump provides historical information for the completed period.

The historical reward information can include:

* Reward period number
* Snapshot date
* Reward period length
* Eligible revenue
* Reward percentage
* Reward pool amount
* Qualified Participant Count
* Whether the reward category is fully rewarded

The historical leaderboard can show each finalized participant's:

* Rank
* Wallet
* Participant activity
* Final reward
* Distribution status
* Distribution time
* Distribution transaction

This allows completed reward periods to remain transparent and independently verifiable.

After a reward has been distributed, its associated transaction signature can be used to verify the distribution on the Solana blockchain.

The permanent reward snapshot provides the finalized record of:

* The reward period
* The participant's final ranking
* Participant activity
* Final reward amount
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

This allows participants to independently verify completed reward distributions on-chain.

## Independent reward categories

Buyer and creator rewards are independent.

A wallet can potentially participate in both categories during the same reward period.

For example, the same wallet may:

* Accumulate SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales
* Create a token and successfully migrate its raised liquidity to Raydium CPMM after the bonding curve reaches its hard cap

If the wallet becomes a qualified participant in both categories, its buyer and creator rewards are calculated independently from their respective reward pools.

The distribution status of the two rewards can also differ.

The Reward Program is designed to provide visibility throughout the reward lifecycle.

Before a reward period begins, participants can find the applicable Qualified Participant Count through official RugFreePump sources, including:

* The RugFreePump Reward Program page
* Reward Program announcements
* Official RugFreePump community channels
* Official communications from RugFreePump admins and moderators

Participants should check the information published for the specific reward period rather than assuming that a previous period's Qualified Participant Count will remain the same.

During an active reward period, participants can monitor:

* Current leaderboard positions
* Participant activity
* Estimated rewards
* Reward period progress

After finalization, participants can review:

* Final rankings
* Final participant activity
* Eligible reward-period revenue
* Reward pool amounts
* Final Qualified Participant Count
* Final reward amounts
* Distribution status
* Distribution timestamps
* Distribution transaction signatures

This provides a transparent record from the start of a reward period through final reward distribution.

<Note>
The Qualified Participant Count is the number of participants who can ultimately qualify for rewards in that category. It is fixed before the corresponding reward period begins, while the identities and rankings of those qualifying participants can change throughout the active period.
</Note>

## Reward Program summary

The RugFreePump Reward Program operates in repeating **28-day reward periods**.

### Before a reward period begins

* Historical platform data is reviewed
* The reward strategy for the upcoming period is established
* The Buyers Qualified Participant Count is determined
* The Creators Qualified Participant Count is determined
* The applicable information is communicated through official RugFreePump channels

### During the active reward period

* Participants accumulate SOL volume used to purchase tokens from RugFreePump bonding-curve sales
* Participants can complete successful liquidity migrations
* Eligible platform revenue is tracked
* Temporary leaderboards are updated approximately every 60 seconds
* Estimated reward amounts are recalculated
* Participants can monitor the current ranking
* New participants can enter the active leaderboard
* Existing participants can move into or outside the current qualifying range
* Activity can come from the RugFreePump interface or external services interacting with the on-chain program
* The predetermined Qualified Participant Counts remain unchanged

### At finalization

* Buyer rankings are finalized
* Creator rankings are finalized
* Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
* Reward pools are calculated
* The predetermined Qualified Participant Counts are applied to the final rankings
* Final qualified participants are determined
* Final reward amounts are calculated
* A permanent reward snapshot is created
* Distribution records are prepared

### After finalization

* Finalized rewards become available for manual distribution
* Distribution is processed through controlled batches
* Distribution status is recorded
* Distribution timestamps are recorded
* Transaction information is recorded
* The completed reward period remains available as historical reward data
* The next reward period begins with its own predetermined Qualified Participant Counts
