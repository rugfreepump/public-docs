# Reward Program

The RugFreePump Reward Program distributes a portion of eligible platform revenue to participants who contribute meaningful activity to the platform.

The program has two independent reward categories:

* **Buyers** who generate qualifying buy volume
* **Creators** whose tokens successfully migrate liquidity

Rewards are calculated separately for each category and finalized at the end of each reward period.

<Note>
Temporary leaderboard positions and estimated rewards are not final. Only the permanent reward snapshot determines final qualification and reward amounts.
</Note>

## Reward categories

### Buyers

The **Buyers Reward Pool** rewards wallets with the highest qualifying buy volume during the current reward period.

Up to **100 buyers** can qualify for each reward period.

Buyer ranking is based on:

1. Higher qualifying buy volume
2. Wallet address as a deterministic tie-breaker

A higher qualifying buy volume generally results in a larger share of the Buyers Reward Pool.

### Creators

The **Creators Reward Pool** rewards creators who successfully migrate tokens during the current reward period.

Up to **100 creators** can qualify for each reward period.

Creator ranking is based on:

1. Higher number of successful token migrations
2. Higher qualifying buy volume
3. Wallet address as a deterministic final tie-breaker

A creator must complete at least one successful token migration during the reward period to qualify for the creator ranking.

<Note>
Buy volume is used only as a creator ranking tie-breaker when creators have the same number of successful migrations. It does not determine the creator's reward amount.
</Note>

## Reward period

Each reward period lasts:

**28 days**

All qualifying activity is measured independently for each reward period.

When a reward period ends:

1. Final buyer rankings are determined
2. Final creator rankings are determined
3. Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
4. Reward pools are calculated
5. Final rewards are calculated
6. A permanent reward snapshot is created
7. Reward distributions are prepared
8. The next reward period begins

Activity from one reward period does not carry forward into the next.

For example:

* Buy volume generated during Round 1 belongs to Round 1
* Successful migrations completed during Round 1 belong to Round 1
* Activity generated during Round 2 is measured independently

## Live leaderboard snapshots

During an active reward period, RugFreePump periodically updates temporary reward snapshots.

These snapshots provide an estimate of the current:

* Buyer rankings
* Creator rankings
* Qualifying buy volume
* Qualifying migration counts
* Reward pool sizes
* Estimated individual rewards

Temporary snapshots are refreshed approximately every:

**60 seconds**

Because the reward period remains active, the information shown in these snapshots can change.

## Estimated rewards are not final

A temporary leaderboard position does not guarantee a final reward.

During an active reward period, a wallet may:

* Move higher in the rankings
* Move lower in the rankings
* Enter the top 100
* Leave the top 100
* Receive a higher estimated reward
* Receive a lower estimated reward

This happens because additional platform activity can change rankings, qualifying activity, and reward pool sizes.

Estimated rewards should therefore be treated only as current estimates.

The final reward is determined by the permanent reward snapshot created when the reward period is finalized.

## Permanent reward snapshot

At the end of each reward period, RugFreePump creates a permanent reward snapshot.

The permanent snapshot records the finalized information for that reward period, including:

* Reward period
* Snapshot date
* Reward period length
* Eligible platform revenue
* Reward pool amounts
* Qualified participants
* Final rankings
* Qualifying activity
* Final reward amounts
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

Once the permanent snapshot is created, the finalized reward amounts for that reward period are fixed.

The permanent reward snapshot serves as the source of truth for the completed reward period.

## Buyers leaderboard

The Buyers leaderboard ranks wallets according to their qualifying buy volume during the current reward period.

The ranking order is:

1. Higher qualifying buy volume
2. Wallet address as a deterministic tie-breaker

Only wallets with qualifying buy activity during the reward period can appear in the buyer ranking.

The program selects up to:

**100 buyers**

for the Buyers Reward Pool.

If fewer than 100 wallets have qualifying activity, fewer than 100 buyers may be finalized.

## What counts toward buyer volume

Buyer qualification is based on the increase in a wallet's recorded buy volume during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Qualifying buy volume = Current total buy volume − Buy volume recorded at the start of the reward period**

Only buy activity accumulated during the current reward period is used for that reward period.

Previous reward periods do not contribute to the current period's qualifying buy volume.

## Creators leaderboard

The Creators leaderboard ranks wallets according to successful token migrations completed during the current reward period.

The ranking order is:

1. Higher number of successful migrations
2. Higher qualifying buy volume
3. Wallet address as a deterministic final tie-breaker

The program selects up to:

**100 creators**

for the Creators Reward Pool.

A creator must have at least one successful token migration during the reward period to appear in the creator ranking.

## What counts toward creator migrations

Creator qualification is based on the increase in a creator's successful migration count during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Qualifying migrations = Current total migrations − Migrations recorded at the start of the reward period**

Only successful migrations completed during the current reward period count toward that period.

## Creator ranking tie-breaker

Creators are primarily ranked by the number of successful migrations.

If two or more creators have the same migration count, qualifying buy volume is used as the next ranking factor.

For example:

| Creator   | Successful migrations | Qualifying buy volume | Result        |
| --------- | --------------------: | --------------------: | ------------- |
| Creator A |                     5 |                Higher | Ranked higher |
| Creator B |                     5 |                 Lower | Ranked lower  |

If both the migration count and qualifying buy volume are equal, the wallet address is used as the final deterministic tie-breaker.

This ensures that the leaderboard has a consistent ordering even when participants have identical qualifying activity.

## Platform revenue used for rewards

Reward pools are calculated from eligible platform revenue generated during the current reward period.

The eligible revenue categories are:

* Bonding Curve Escrow Creation Fee
* Buy Fee
* Bonding Curve Escrow Completed Fee

The following platform revenue categories are **not included** when calculating the Reward Program pools:

* Token Creator Incentive
* Raydium Liquidity Pool Creation Fee

The eligible reward-period revenue is calculated from the increase in eligible platform revenue during the current reward period.

In simplified form:

**Eligible reward-period revenue = Current accumulated eligible platform revenue − Eligible platform revenue recorded at the start of the reward period**

The resulting amount is used to calculate the Buyers and Creators Reward Pools.

## Reward pool allocation

The Reward Program currently allocates **10% of eligible reward-period platform revenue** to participants.

The allocation is:

| Reward category             | Allocation |
| --------------------------- | ---------: |
| Buyers Reward Pool          |         6% |
| Creators Reward Pool        |         4% |
| **Total Reward Allocation** |    **10%** |

This means that, within the total 10% reward allocation, Buyers receive 60% and Creators receive 40%.

The reward pools are calculated independently.

<Note>
The reward percentages are applied to eligible reward-period platform revenue, not to total platform revenue from categories excluded from the Reward Program.
</Note>

## Buyers reward calculation

The Buyers Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to each finalized buyer's qualifying buy volume.

The calculation is:

**Buyer Reward = Buyers Reward Pool × Buyer Qualifying Buy Volume ÷ Total Qualifying Buy Volume of All Finalized Buyers**

### Example

Assume:

* Buyers Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total qualifying buy volume of all finalized buyers: **1,000 SOL**
* Your qualifying buy volume: **100 SOL**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 100 SOL ÷ 1,000 SOL = 1 SOL**

Your reward therefore depends on your qualifying buy volume relative to the combined qualifying buy volume of all finalized buyers.

This means that leaderboard position alone does not determine the exact reward amount.

## Creators reward calculation

The Creators Reward Pool is distributed proportionally according to the number of qualifying successful migrations completed by each finalized creator.

The calculation is:

**Creator Reward = Creators Reward Pool × Creator Qualifying Migration Count ÷ Total Qualifying Migration Count of All Finalized Creators**

### Example

Assume:

* Creators Reward Pool: **10 SOL**
* Total qualifying migrations of all finalized creators: **100**
* Your qualifying migrations: **10**

Your calculated reward would be:

**10 SOL × 10 ÷ 100 = 1 SOL**

Your reward depends on your qualifying migration count relative to the combined qualifying migration count of all finalized creators.

## Buy volume does not determine creator reward amounts

Qualifying buy volume can affect creator ranking when multiple creators have the same migration count.

However, the creator reward itself is calculated using:

**Qualifying migration count**

not qualifying buy volume.

For example, if two creators both complete five successful migrations, the creator with higher qualifying buy volume is ranked higher.

However, if both creators remain finalized with five qualifying migrations, their reward calculation is based on their migration count, not their buy volume.

## Why estimated rewards change

Estimated reward amounts can change throughout an active reward period.

The main reasons include:

### More eligible platform revenue

As eligible platform revenue increases, the Buyers and Creators Reward Pools can increase.

### More qualifying participants

New wallets can enter the top 100 rankings.

### Changes in buyer volume

Existing buyers can increase their qualifying buy volume and change their ranking.

### Additional creator migrations

Creators can complete additional successful migrations and change their ranking.

### Ranking changes

Participants can move into or out of the top 100.

Because of these changes, an estimated reward shown during an active reward period is not guaranteed.

Only the permanent reward snapshot determines the final reward.

## End of the reward period

When a reward period reaches its scheduled finalization point, RugFreePump finalizes the completed period.

During finalization:

* Temporary leaderboard information is finalized
* Final buyer rankings are determined
* Final creator rankings are determined
* Eligible reward-period revenue is calculated
* The Buyers Reward Pool is calculated
* The Creators Reward Pool is calculated
* Final reward amounts are calculated
* The permanent reward snapshot is created
* Reward distribution records are prepared
* The next reward period is initialized

This separates the completed reward period from the new reward period.

## Snapshot consistency

RugFreePump ensures that the final reward calculations are based on a consistent state for the completed reward period.

This means that the final:

* Buyer rankings
* Creator rankings
* Qualifying activity
* Eligible revenue
* Reward pools
* Reward amounts

are finalized together as part of the permanent snapshot.

After the completed period has been finalized and the next reward period begins, temporary leaderboard snapshots resume for the new period.

## Reward distribution

After a permanent reward snapshot has been created, rewards are distributed to finalized eligible participants.

Rewards are distributed automatically.

There is no separate reward claim process required from participants.

Distribution information is recorded so completed rewards can be verified.

RugFreePump does not promise an exact payout time after finalization, as distribution may be processed in multiple controlled batches.

## Distribution batches

Reward distributions are processed in batches.

The current configuration supports up to:

**17 reward transfers per batch**

Buyer rewards and creator rewards are processed through separate distribution batches.

Batching allows larger reward distributions to be processed in an organized and recoverable manner.

## Reward distribution status

Distribution information associated with finalized rewards includes:

* Whether the reward has been distributed
* Distribution timestamp
* Distribution transaction signature

This provides a record that can be used to verify completed distributions.

## Verifying a reward

After a reward has been distributed, its associated transaction signature can be used to verify the distribution on the Solana blockchain.

The permanent reward snapshot provides the finalized record of:

* The reward period
* The participant's final ranking
* Qualifying activity
* The calculated reward amount
* Distribution status
* Distribution transaction information

## If fewer than 100 users qualify

The Reward Program can select up to 100 buyers and up to 100 creators.

It does not require exactly 100 participants in either category.

For example:

* If only 70 wallets have qualifying buyer activity, up to 70 buyers may be finalized
* If only 35 creators successfully migrate tokens, up to 35 creators may be finalized

Only participants who meet the applicable qualification requirements can receive a reward.

## Separate reward categories

Buyer and creator rewards are independent.

A wallet can potentially qualify in both categories.

For example, the same wallet may:

* Generate enough qualifying buy volume to enter the Buyers leaderboard
* Successfully migrate one or more tokens and enter the Creators leaderboard

If the wallet qualifies in both categories, its buyer and creator rewards are calculated independently.

## Leaderboard position can change

Being visible in a temporary leaderboard does not guarantee a permanent reward.

Before the permanent snapshot:

* Rankings can change
* New participants can enter
* Existing participants can leave the top 100
* Estimated rewards can increase
* Estimated rewards can decrease

A wallet receives a finalized reward only if it qualifies in the permanent snapshot.

<Warning>
A temporary top-100 position should not be treated as a guaranteed reward.
</Warning>

## Estimated rewards are not guaranteed rewards

Temporary reward amounts are estimates based on the current state of the reward period.

They can change until the permanent snapshot is created.

Participants should not make financial decisions based solely on:

* Current ranking
* Temporary leaderboard position
* Estimated reward amount
* Estimated reward pool size

The permanent reward snapshot determines the final reward amount.

## Reward amounts depend on platform activity

Reward pool sizes are not fixed amounts.

They depend on eligible platform revenue generated during the reward period.

As a result:

* Higher eligible platform activity can increase reward pools
* Lower eligible platform activity can reduce reward pools
* Changes in participant activity can change individual reward shares
* Changes in the number of qualified participants can affect the distribution of the reward pools

The final reward amount therefore cannot be known with certainty until the reward period is finalized.

## No guaranteed reward amount

RugFreePump does not guarantee a specific reward amount before the permanent reward snapshot is created.

Even if a wallet is currently ranked in the top 100:

* Its position can change
* Its qualifying activity can change
* The reward pool can change
* Other participants can increase their activity

Only finalized permanent snapshot data determines the reward amount for a completed reward period.

<Warning>
Participation in the Reward Program does not guarantee a reward. Eligibility and final reward amounts depend on qualifying activity, final rankings, eligible reward-period revenue, and the applicable reward calculation.
</Warning>

## Reward Program summary

The RugFreePump Reward Program operates in repeating 28-day reward periods.

### During the active reward period

* Buyer volume is tracked
* Creator migrations are tracked
* Eligible platform revenue is tracked
* Temporary leaderboard snapshots are updated approximately every 60 seconds
* Estimated rewards are recalculated

### At the permanent snapshot

* Buyer rankings are finalized
* Creator rankings are finalized
* Eligible reward-period revenue is finalized
* Reward pools are finalized
* Reward amounts are calculated
* Permanent reward data is stored
* Distribution batches are prepared

### After finalization

* Rewards are distributed automatically
* Distribution status is recorded
* Transaction information is recorded
* The next reward period begins

## Transparency and verification

The Reward Program is designed to maintain a clear record of each completed reward period.

Permanent reward snapshots preserve the finalized information needed to understand:

* Who qualified
* How participants ranked
* What activity qualified
* How much eligible revenue was generated
* How large each reward pool was
* How individual rewards were calculated
* Whether rewards were distributed
* Which transactions were used for distribution

Temporary leaderboard information provides visibility during an active reward period.

Permanent reward snapshots provide the finalized record for completed reward periods.

<Note>
Temporary leaderboard positions and estimated rewards are not final. Only the permanent reward snapshot created at the end of a reward period determines final qualification and reward amounts.
</Note>
