# Reward Calculation

RugFreePump calculates rewards from the platform revenue generated during each 28-day reward period.

The resulting reward pool is divided between eligible buyers and creators according to the current reward allocation.

## Reward Pool

The reward pool is based on qualifying RugFreePump platform revenue generated during the reward period.

The current included revenue sources are:

- **Bonding Curve Escrow Creation Fee**
- **Buy Fee**
- **Bonding Curve Escrow Completed Fee**

These revenue sources contribute to the reward pool used for the corresponding reward period.

## Revenue Excluded From the Reward Pool

The following fees are currently excluded from the reward pool:

- **Token Creator Incentive**
- **Raydium Liquidity Pool Creation Fee**

These exclusions ensure that only the designated platform revenue sources contribute to the Reward Program pool.

For current protocol fee amounts and limits, see [Protocol Fees](/overview/protocol-fees).

## Buyer and Creator Allocation

The reward pool is currently divided as follows:

| Reward Category | Allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Buyer Rewards | 60% |
| Creator Rewards | 40% |

For example, if the finalized reward pool were $10,000:

- $6,000 would be allocated to the buyer reward pool.
- $4,000 would be allocated to the creator reward pool.

The example above is illustrative only. Actual reward pools depend on the qualifying revenue generated during the relevant reward period.

## Buyer Rewards

The buyer reward pool is distributed among the eligible buyers identified by the final reward snapshot.

Eligibility is limited to the **top 100 buyers** ranked by qualifying purchase volume during the reward period.

A buyer's reward is determined from their position and qualifying activity within the finalized buyer ranking.

The final reward amount is established when the reward period is finalized.

## Creator Rewards

The creator reward pool is distributed among the eligible creators identified by the final reward snapshot.

Eligibility is limited to the **top 100 creators** ranked by successful bonding-curve migrations during the reward period.

A creator must have at least one successful migration during the period to qualify.

Creator ranking tie-breakers are applied when necessary:

1. Buy volume
2. Wallet key ordering

The final reward amount is established from the finalized creator ranking.

## Finalized Reward Calculation

The reward calculation uses the final reward snapshot rather than the continuously changing active ranking.

This means that the reward calculation is based on:

- The finalized reward-period revenue
- The final buyer ranking
- The final creator ranking
- The applicable reward allocation
- The participants recorded in the final snapshot

Once the snapshot is finalized, the results for that reward period are permanent.

## Estimated Rewards vs. Final Rewards

During an active reward period, RugFreePump may display estimated rankings and rewards.

These estimates can change because:

- New qualifying purchases can change buyer rankings.
- New successful migrations can change creator rankings.
- The reward pool continues to accumulate qualifying revenue.
- Participants can move into or out of the top 100.

Therefore, an estimated reward shown during an active period is not a guaranteed final payout.

The final reward is determined only after the reward period is finalized.

## Reward Period Independence

Each 28-day reward period is calculated independently.

Activity from one period does not modify the finalized rewards of another period.

Once a period has been finalized, later platform activity belongs to a subsequent reward period and does not change the completed period's reward calculation.

For information about what happens after calculation, see [Reward Distribution](/reward-program/reward-distribution).
