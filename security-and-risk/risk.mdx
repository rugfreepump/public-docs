Using RugFreePump involves significant technical, economic, and behavioral risks, where smart contract mechanics, volatile markets, and aggressive bot activity can lead to a permanent loss of funds. The overview below covers key system dynamics, though additional unforeseen vulnerabilities or external failures may arise.

***

## On-Chain Program Risks

* **Code Vulnerabilities & Exploits:** The smart contract handles token creation, bonding-curve math, and migrations. Bugs or accounting errors can freeze funds without any manual rollback capability.
* **Single-Key Team Controls:** Upgrade authority and parameter configurations are managed by a single developer wallet rather than a multisig governance model.
* **Maintenance & Dependencies:** Maintenance freezes can reduce active token sale windows, while external dependencies like Metaplex create permanent metadata constraints upon launch.
* **Execution & Lifecycle:** Bundled transaction atomicity on Solana means any instruction failure reverts the entire operation.

## Bot & Market Dynamics

* **Adversarial Bots:** Sniper and MEV/sandwich bots actively monitor curves to front-run user orders.
* **Slippage & Limits:** High price impacts, dynamic per-block pricing, and minimum USD purchase requirements can cause transactions to fail or execute at unfavorable rates.

## Project & Quality Risks

* **Dev Dumps & Abandonment:** Creators can sell off their initial allocations to crash curves, and platform presence does not constitute a team endorsement.
* **Impersonation:** Token metadata, social links, and tickers can be spoofed by malicious actors.

## Oracle & Migration Risks

* **Pyth Oracles:** USD-denominated curves rely on Pyth price feeds; stale prices or volatility spikes can freeze trading operations.
* **Raydium Transition:** Hitting 100% on a curve triggers a Raydium migration via CPI, after which open-market volatility and external pool risks apply.

## Fees, Vaults, & Infrastructure

* **Non-Refundable Costs:** Failed transactions still incur network gas fees, and unexpired vault balances are subject to rent-exemption and wallet-tracking constraints for refunds.
* **Wallet Security:** Users are entirely responsible for private key safety, phishing awareness, and recognizing RPC or frontend display lags.
