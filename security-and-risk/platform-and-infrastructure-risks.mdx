---
title: Platform and Infrastructure Risks
description: Understand the risks associated with RugFreePump's dApp, off-chain services, infrastructure, and third-party dependencies.
---

RugFreePump uses off-chain infrastructure to provide the dApp interface and supporting services around the on-chain protocol.

These systems improve usability and provide information that is not stored exclusively in the blockchain.

They also introduce a separate category of availability, integrity, and operational risk.

## On-chain versus off-chain systems

The RugFreePump architecture contains an important distinction between:

**On-chain state**

and

**Off-chain presentation and infrastructure.**

The on-chain program processes protocol instructions and maintains the relevant Solana state.

Off-chain systems may provide:

- Web application delivery.
- Token metadata handling.
- Indexed information.
- Search and discovery.
- Platform analytics.
- Realtime updates.
- Supporting APIs and services.

A problem with an off-chain service does not automatically mean that the underlying on-chain state has changed.

## dApp availability

The RugFreePump website may become temporarily unavailable because of:

- Hosting problems.
- DNS issues.
- CDN problems.
- Deployment failures.
- Infrastructure outages.
- Network connectivity problems.
- Third-party service failures.

A temporary dApp outage does not by itself invalidate transactions that have already been confirmed on Solana.

## Indexing and display delays

Information displayed by the dApp may depend on off-chain indexing.

An indexing delay can cause the interface to temporarily display:

- Older sale information.
- Delayed transaction activity.
- Delayed statistics.
- Delayed token metadata.
- Delayed realtime updates.

When an important decision depends on current blockchain state, users should verify the underlying transaction or account state directly on Solana where practical.

## Realtime service risk

Realtime features may depend on WebSocket or other event-delivery infrastructure.

A realtime outage can cause the interface to stop receiving updates even though the underlying blockchain continues operating.

Users should distinguish between:

**No realtime update**

and

**No on-chain state change.**

These are not necessarily the same condition.

## RPC provider dependency

The dApp and supporting services may use Solana RPC infrastructure to read or submit blockchain information.

RPC providers can experience:

- Outages.
- Rate limits.
- Latency.
- Incomplete responses.
- Temporary synchronization problems.

An RPC issue can make the dApp appear unavailable or cause a transaction to fail without indicating that Solana itself is unavailable.

## Third-party services

RugFreePump may rely on third-party services for specific functionality.

Examples can include:

- Blockchain RPC.
- Oracle data.
- Metadata storage.
- Content delivery.
- Analytics.
- Liquidity infrastructure.

Third-party service failures can affect specific platform functions.

RugFreePump cannot guarantee the availability or behavior of every external service.

## Metadata and content availability

Token metadata and media may be stored or delivered through systems outside the Solana ledger.

A metadata provider or delivery service can become temporarily unavailable.

This may affect how a token is displayed without necessarily changing the underlying token mint or on-chain ownership state.

## Infrastructure security

Off-chain systems can be exposed to conventional security risks, including:

- Credential compromise.
- Dependency vulnerabilities.
- Unauthorized access.
- Deployment mistakes.
- Configuration errors.
- DNS compromise.
- Supply-chain attacks.
- Denial-of-service attacks.

These risks are distinct from vulnerabilities in the RugFreePump on-chain program.

## Service availability is not asset custody

RugFreePump's dApp infrastructure should not be treated as a substitute for the user's wallet.

Temporary platform unavailability does not transfer ownership of assets to RugFreePump.

The user's wallet and Solana account state remain the relevant systems for asset ownership and transaction authorization.

## Dependency changes

External services can change APIs, infrastructure, pricing, availability, or technical behavior.

Such changes can require RugFreePump to modify its integration.

A dependency change can temporarily affect a specific platform function without necessarily affecting the underlying on-chain protocol.

## Security versus availability

A service being unavailable does not necessarily mean that the system has been compromised.

Likewise, a service being available does not prove that every component is free from vulnerabilities.

Availability incidents and security incidents should therefore be evaluated separately.

## User guidance during an outage

If the dApp behaves unexpectedly:

1. Do not repeatedly submit transactions without understanding the failure.
2. Check the wallet transaction history.
3. Check the official RugFreePump status or announcement channels.
4. Verify confirmed transactions directly on Solana where appropriate.
5. Avoid links supplied through unsolicited support messages.

> **Important:** Off-chain infrastructure exists to provide the RugFreePump user experience and supporting services. The underlying blockchain state and the availability of the dApp are separate concerns.
