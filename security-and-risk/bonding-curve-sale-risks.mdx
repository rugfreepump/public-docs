---
title: Bonding-Curve Sale Risks
description: Understand the economic and execution risks associated with participating in a RugFreePump bonding-curve sale.
---

A RugFreePump bonding-curve sale is a market mechanism in which the token purchase price changes as the sale progresses.

The mechanism provides deterministic protocol rules for calculating purchases, but it does not guarantee that a token will increase in value or that a participant will recover the amount spent.

## Price movement

The bonding-curve price changes as the sale progresses.

As additional SOL is contributed, subsequent purchases may execute at different prices from earlier purchases.

Two users purchasing the same token at different points in the sale can therefore receive different effective prices.

A later purchase is not entitled to the price available to an earlier purchaser.

## Buying near the beginning or end of a sale

The position of a purchase within the bonding curve can materially affect the price paid.

Users should not assume that:

- An early purchase will be profitable.
- A later purchase will be more or less profitable.
- Reaching the end of a sale guarantees a particular market value.
- Sale completion guarantees a return of capital.

The bonding curve defines the purchase mechanism; it does not predict future demand.

## Price impact

A user's purchase contributes to the progression of the bonding curve.

This means that the transaction itself can affect the price conditions for subsequent purchases.

The displayed token amount should therefore be considered in the context of the current sale state and the amount being purchased.

## Sale completion

A bonding-curve sale can reach its completion conditions.

Completion does not mean that every participant has earned a profit.

It means that the sale has reached the protocol conditions required for its next lifecycle stage.

When the relevant completion conditions are reached, liquidity may become eligible for migration to the configured external liquidity environment.

## Expiration and cancellation

A bonding-curve sale may expire or be canceled according to protocol rules.

When this occurs, eligible contributors may be able to withdraw their contributed SOL according to the on-chain withdrawal conditions.

Withdrawal eligibility does not mean that every amount previously spent by the user is automatically recoverable.

In particular, previously charged fees and Solana transaction fees are separate from the contribution amount subject to withdrawal.

## No guaranteed return

RugFreePump does not guarantee:

- Profit.
- Capital preservation.
- A minimum token price.
- A specific market capitalization.
- A successful migration.
- Post-migration liquidity.
- Future trading volume.
- Future demand for the token.

A token can lose substantial or all of its market value after purchase.

## Market and liquidity risk

A bonding curve is not the same thing as a permanent market.

After migration, trading takes place in an external liquidity environment.

The existence of a bonding-curve sale does not guarantee sufficient liquidity or demand after migration.

## Creator-related risk

The protocol's bonding-curve mechanics do not determine the future behavior of the token creator.

A creator may make decisions that affect the token's reputation, demand, or market value.

Users should evaluate the token and creator independently rather than treating the existence of a RugFreePump sale as an endorsement.

## Transaction execution risk

A purchase may fail before confirmation because of:

- Invalid transaction conditions.
- Oracle conditions.
- Insufficient balance.
- Network congestion.
- Slippage or sale-state changes.
- Program validation.
- External dependency failures.

Users should inspect failed transactions before retrying.

## Information displayed by the dApp

The dApp may display estimated prices, token amounts, sale progress, and other information.

Displayed information can become outdated as the sale changes.

Where possible, users should verify important transaction details against the wallet confirmation and on-chain transaction result.

## Risk of total loss

Participation in a token launch can result in a complete loss of the amount committed to the purchase.

This can occur through market value decline even when every protocol component operates correctly.

Users should only participate with funds they can afford to lose.

> **Important:** RugFreePump provides the bonding-curve mechanism and associated protocol infrastructure. It does not provide an assurance of token value, profitability, liquidity, or future project success.
