---
title: Liquidity and Migration Risks
description: Understand the risks associated with bonding-curve completion, liquidity migration, Raydium CPMM, and post-migration trading.
---

When a RugFreePump bonding-curve sale reaches its completion conditions, the completed sale liquidity can be migrated into a Raydium CPMM pool.

Migration represents a transition from the RugFreePump bonding-curve environment to an external liquidity and trading environment.

That transition introduces additional risks.

## Migration is a lifecycle transition

Migration does not represent a guarantee of token value or future market activity.

It changes the environment in which liquidity is available.

The documented lifecycle is:

**Bonding-Curve Sale → Completion → Liquidity Migration → Raydium CPMM**

Each stage has different technical and economic conditions.

## Completion conditions

A bonding-curve sale must satisfy the protocol's completion conditions before liquidity migration can occur.

Users should not interpret a sale approaching completion as a guarantee that migration will succeed.

A transaction can fail because of program validation, account conditions, network conditions, or external dependency failures.

## External protocol dependency

Liquidity migration interacts with the Raydium CPMM environment.

Raydium is an external protocol and is therefore outside the complete control of RugFreePump.

An external protocol outage, integration issue, network condition, or unexpected change can affect migration.

## Migration transaction risk

Migration requires a blockchain transaction.

The transaction may fail because of:

- Invalid or unexpected account state.
- Incorrect migration conditions.
- Network congestion.
- Insufficient transaction resources.
- External program errors.
- Changes in external protocol conditions.

A failed migration transaction does not necessarily mean that the underlying bonding-curve state has been lost.

Users should inspect the actual transaction result before drawing conclusions.

## Post-migration liquidity

After migration, token trading takes place through the external liquidity environment.

The existence of a liquidity pool does not guarantee:

- High trading volume.
- Stable pricing.
- Low price impact.
- Continued liquidity.
- Future market demand.

Liquidity can change over time.

## Price changes after migration

The token's market price can change substantially after migration.

The post-migration market may price the token differently from the final bonding-curve conditions.

Users should not assume that the migration price represents a guaranteed market value.

## Slippage and price impact

Trading in an external liquidity pool is subject to market conditions.

A trade can move the effective market price depending on available liquidity and transaction size.

Users should review applicable slippage settings and transaction details before signing.

## Liquidity does not eliminate token risk

A migrated token can still lose value.

Migration does not guarantee:

- Project success.
- Creator activity.
- Market demand.
- Trading volume.
- Price appreciation.
- Capital preservation.

Liquidity is a market mechanism, not a guarantee of economic performance.

## External market risk

Once a token is trading in an external market, users are exposed to market participants and conditions outside RugFreePump.

These may include:

- Large trades.
- Liquidity changes.
- Market sentiment.
- Arbitrage activity.
- Broader cryptocurrency market conditions.

## Migration and user expectations

Users should distinguish between:

**Sale completion**

and

**Successful post-migration market performance.**

The first is a protocol lifecycle condition.

The second depends on the external market.

These are not equivalent.

> **Important:** Liquidity migration does not guarantee a liquid market, stable price, profitable trading, or continued project activity.
