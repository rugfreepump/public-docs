---
title: Fees and Refund Risks
description: Understand RugFreePump fees, transaction costs, withdrawal conditions, and the limits of refunds.
---

RugFreePump transactions can involve multiple types of costs.

Users should distinguish between **protocol fees**, **Solana network transaction fees**, and funds that may become eligible for withdrawal under the bonding-curve cancellation or expiration rules.

A later sale expiration or cancellation does not automatically reverse fees that were already charged.

## Protocol fees

RugFreePump may charge fees for eligible protocol operations.

The applicable fee should be reviewed before approving a transaction.

## Fees are not automatically refundable

A fee already charged by the protocol is not automatically returned because a bonding-curve sale later:

- Expires.
- Is canceled.
- Fails to reach completion.
- Produces an unfavorable economic outcome.

Users should treat fees as incurred costs once the corresponding transaction has successfully charged them unless the published protocol rules explicitly provide otherwise.

## Contributed SOL versus fees

A user's contributed SOL and the fees associated with the transaction are separate concepts.

If a sale expires or is canceled, an eligible contributor may be able to withdraw the contributed amount according to the on-chain withdrawal rules.

That does not mean that every amount previously paid during the sale is refundable.

Previously charged protocol fees may remain non-refundable.

## Withdrawal fee

RugFreePump does not currently charge a separate RugFreePump protocol withdrawal fee for eligible withdrawal operations.

The withdrawal transaction is nevertheless a Solana transaction and therefore may incur the applicable Solana network transaction fee.

## Solana network fees

Solana transaction fees are paid to the network rather than treated as RugFreePump protocol revenue.

These costs may be incurred when:

- A transaction succeeds.
- A transaction fails after being submitted.
- A user retries a transaction.
- A user performs another wallet operation.

Network transaction fees should therefore not be considered automatically refundable by RugFreePump.

## Priority fees

Depending on the transaction and network conditions, a wallet or application may use a priority fee.

Priority fees are separate from RugFreePump protocol fees.

## Failed transactions

A failed transaction can still result in network-level transaction costs depending on how the transaction was processed by Solana.

Users should inspect their wallet and transaction history rather than assuming that a failed transaction means no cost was incurred.

## Sale expiration and cancellation

Expiration or cancellation affects the protocol's treatment of eligible contributed SOL.

It does not automatically create a general refund right for every cost associated with participating in the sale.

Users should review the specific withdrawal conditions before committing funds.

## No guaranteed recovery of all costs

Users should not assume that an expired or canceled sale means they will recover:

- Protocol fees.
- Solana transaction fees.
- Priority fees.
- Other third-party costs.
- Opportunity costs.
- Losses caused by token price movement.

Only amounts explicitly covered by the protocol's withdrawal rules are eligible for withdrawal.

## Fee changes

Protocol fees may be changed through the applicable protocol administration and governance processes.

## Before approving a transaction

Users should review:

1. The amount of SOL being spent.
2. The expected token amount.
3. The applicable protocol fee.
4. The estimated network fee.
5. Any priority fee.
6. The transaction presented by the wallet.
7. The conditions under which contributed SOL can later be withdrawn.

> **Important:** A withdrawal right is not the same as a general refund right. Users should assume that fees and blockchain transaction costs already incurred may not be recoverable.
