RugFreePump is a **Solana-native, high-conviction crowdfunded launchpad designed to replace unrestricted buy-and-sell bonding curves with a structured fundraising phase**.

Instead of allowing immediate trading and potential short-term sell pressure, the protocol requires buyers to contribute SOL toward a defined funding target. Successful campaigns can migrate liquidity to **Raydium CPMM**, with the resulting LP tokens automatically burned, while unsuccessful campaigns provide a protocol-defined mechanism for eligible buyers to withdraw their contributed SOL.

The result is a launch model built around **committed fundraising, defined outcomes, and protocol-enforced execution**.

## Why RugFreePump Exists

The earliest stage of a token launch can create difficult conditions for both creators and buyers.

Traditional unrestricted bonding curve launches can encourage rapid speculation, automated sniping, immediate profit-taking, and sudden token selling. A creator can spend significant time building a project and attracting a community while newly purchased tokens can still be sold back into the bonding curve moments later.

This can make the fundraising environment heavily influenced by short-term market behavior.

Buyers may feel pressure to react to rapid price movements rather than evaluate a project on its fundamentals, while creators can face continuous sell pressure during the period in which they are trying to build momentum and community participation.

RugFreePump was designed to address this structural problem by changing the role of the bonding curve during the initial launch.

Instead of functioning as an unrestricted early market, the bonding curve operates within a **committed fundraising phase** with predefined rules and a defined path toward completion or resolution.

The objective is not to eliminate the broader risks of cryptocurrency or individual projects. It is to reduce specific risks and incentives created by unrestricted early-stage token trading.

## Built Around Conviction

The defining feature of RugFreePump is its **high-conviction fundraising model**.

During an active sale, the protocol does not provide a standard token-selling operation. Buyers can contribute SOL and receive tokens according to the bonding curve, but they cannot immediately sell those tokens back through the same mechanism.

This changes the nature of participation.

For creators, marketing and community-building efforts can have more durable value because newly acquired tokens cannot simply be dumped back into the bonding curve during the fundraising phase. Capital attracted through the launch remains aligned with the project's progress toward its funding target.

For buyers, the rules make the purpose of the fundraising phase explicit: participation is a commitment to the campaign rather than an unrestricted opportunity to enter and exit the bonding curve.

This structure is intended to create a more focused environment for building a project and evaluating a launch without the constant pressure of immediate bonding curve sell-back activity.

For detailed purchase and pricing mechanics, see [Bonding Curve](/core-mechanics/bonding-curve) and [Slippage & Priority](/core-mechanics/slippage-and-priority).

## Defined Outcomes

RugFreePump gives every sale a defined protocol-level lifecycle.

A sale can ultimately become:

- **Completed** — the sale reaches its funding target and becomes eligible for liquidity migration.
- **Migrated** — the completed sale successfully transitions its designated liquidity to Raydium.
- **Expired** — the sale reaches its time limit without reaching its funding target.
- **Canceled** — a completed sale is canceled through an authorized emergency procedure.

These outcomes provide a predictable framework for how a campaign can conclude rather than leaving its resolution entirely to off-chain decisions.

The complete lifecycle and transition conditions are covered in [How RugFreePump Works](/overview/how-rugfreepump-works) and [Sale Lifecycle](/core-mechanics/sale-lifecycle).

## From Fundraising to Public Liquidity

When a sale reaches its funding target, it enters the completion stage and becomes eligible for liquidity migration.

The designated liquidity is migrated to **Raydium CPMM**, transitioning the project from its structured fundraising phase toward public decentralized liquidity.

As part of the migration, the resulting **LP tokens are automatically burned**. This means the LP tokens created through the migration are not left under the creator's ordinary control after migration.

The migration and LP-token burn are executed through the on-chain protocol.

For the practical creator workflow, see [Migrating Raised Liquidity](/user-guides/for-token-creators/migrating-raised-liquidity).

## When a Sale Does Not Complete

A sale may reach its time limit without reaching its funding target.

When this happens, eligible buyers can manually withdraw their contributed SOL through the platform according to the protocol's withdrawal rules.

Withdrawal is **not a token sell**. As part of an eligible withdrawal, any remaining purchased tokens from that contribution that are still held by the withdrawing wallet are automatically burned.

If those tokens were previously transferred to another wallet, the transfer does not create another withdrawal entitlement. Those transferred tokens have no value or claim within the withdrawal process.

The same withdrawal mechanism applies to eligible buyers when a completed sale is subsequently canceled through an authorized emergency procedure.

For the complete withdrawal process, see [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

## On-Chain by Design

RugFreePump places its core financial and lifecycle rules within a **Solana on-chain program**.

The program is responsible for enforcing protocol operations such as:

- Creating token sales
- Processing purchases
- Applying bonding curve rules
- Managing sale progression
- Processing eligible withdrawals
- Migrating liquidity
- Burning applicable tokens
- Enforcing authority-restricted operations

This creates a clear separation between **protocol execution** and **platform infrastructure**.

The blockchain is the source of truth for protocol state and financial execution. The RugFreePump database and indexer observe blockchain activity and maintain an indexed representation used for features such as search, project discovery, statistics, profiles, rankings, and real-time updates.

The platform infrastructure therefore improves accessibility and performance without becoming the authority over user funds or independently authorizing protocol operations.

> **The blockchain is the source of truth. The platform infrastructure is built around that truth.**

This architecture allows participants and developers to independently verify critical protocol behavior through Solana.

For a deeper technical explanation, see [On-Chain Program](/on-chain-program/overview) and [Proof of Execution](/on-chain-program/proof-of-execution).

## What "Rug-Free" Means

The name **RugFreePump** represents the platform's design objective. It is not a promise that cryptocurrency, individual projects, or participants are free from risk.

Risks can still arise from creators, communities, market conditions, wallets, user decisions, blockchain infrastructure, and the broader cryptocurrency ecosystem.

RugFreePump focuses specifically on making the **launch mechanism itself more structured and constrained**.

Its design is intended to give participants clearer rules around how fundraising works, how a sale can conclude, how eligible contributions can be resolved, and how successful campaigns transition to public liquidity.

Participants remain responsible for evaluating the projects they choose to support and the risks associated with their decisions.

For a detailed discussion of remaining risks and participant responsibilities, see [Security & Risk](/security-and-risk/security-model).

## More Than a Launch Interface

RugFreePump combines its on-chain protocol with infrastructure designed to support participants, creators, developers, and the wider ecosystem.

The platform provides:

- A public interface for discovering and participating in token sales.
- Creator tools for launching bonding curve fundraising campaigns.
- Buyer tools for managing purchases and eligible withdrawals.
- Project and wallet information.
- Search, statistics, rankings, and ecosystem analytics.
- REST APIs for application integration.
- Real-time data through WebSocket infrastructure.
- An ecosystem reward program for qualifying creators and buyers.
- Public documentation and developer resources.

These services extend the usability of the protocol while the on-chain program remains the authority for protocol execution.

Developers can begin with [Building on RugFreePump](/developer-guide/building-on-rugfreepump) to explore the available integration surfaces.

## The RugFreePump Model

RugFreePump can be summarized by a simple principle:

**Creators launch.  
Buyers contribute.  
The protocol enforces the rules.  
Fundraising follows a defined path.  
Successful sales transition to public liquidity.  
Unsuccessful sales have a defined resolution.**

RugFreePump is designed to replace unrestricted early trading with a more structured fundraising model where the rules governing participation and outcomes are established by the protocol itself.

The result is a launch environment centered on **high conviction, defined outcomes, and verifiable execution**.
