RugFreePump supports two token sale models: **Token Unlocked (TU)** and **Token Locked (TL)**.

Both models operate within the same high-conviction bonding curve fundraising environment on Solana. Creators raise SOL toward a defined sale threshold, contributors purchase tokens through the bonding curve, and successful sales proceed toward liquidity migration on **Raydium CPMM**.

The fundamental difference is **how and when purchased tokens become available to buyers after successful liquidity migration**.

- **Token Unlocked (TU):** Buyers can claim 100% of their purchased tokens immediately after successful liquidity migration.
- **Token Locked (TL):** Buyers can claim their purchased tokens gradually according to a predefined unlock schedule.

The creator selects the sale model during token creation. **Once a sale is created, its selected model cannot be changed.**

## TU vs TL: Key Differences

| Feature | Token Unlocked (TU) | Token Locked (TL) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fundraising mechanism | Bonding curve | Bonding curve |
| Tokens delivered during purchase | No | No |
| Purchased allocation recorded on-chain | Yes | Yes |
| Claiming becomes available | After successful migration | Gradually after successful migration |
| Token availability | 100% immediately | 5% every 30 minutes under current settings |
| Full allocation available | Immediately after migration | 10 hours after migration under current settings |
| Buyer initiates claiming | Yes | Yes |
| Sale model changeable after creation | No | No |
| Protocol fees | Same | Same |

The sale model determines **token availability**, not the fundraising rules, protocol fee structure, or likelihood of project success.

## Understanding the Two Models

### Token Unlocked (TU)

**Token Unlocked (TU)** allows buyers to claim their entire purchased token allocation once the sale successfully completes liquidity migration.

During the active fundraising phase, purchased tokens remain in the protocol-controlled token account while each buyer's allocation is recorded on-chain.

After the sale reaches its threshold and liquidity migration succeeds, **100% of the purchased allocation becomes available to claim**.

There is no additional token unlock waiting period under TU.

**Purchase → Reach Sale Threshold → Migrate Liquidity → Claim 100%**

Buyers must initiate the claiming transaction themselves. Successful migration does not automatically transfer purchased tokens into their wallets.

Once claimed, the tokens are under the buyer's control and may be transferred or traded through supported external markets.

### Token Locked (TL)

**Token Locked (TL)** makes purchased tokens available gradually after successful liquidity migration.

Like TU, purchased tokens remain under protocol custody during the active fundraising phase, and each buyer's allocation is recorded on-chain.

However, successful liquidity migration does not immediately make the entire allocation available.

Instead, **tokens become claimable progressively according to the unlock settings established when the sale was created**.

#### Current Token Unlock Settings

The following settings currently apply to newly created TL sales:

| Setting | Current Value |
| --- | --- |
| Token Unlock Amount | **5.00%** |
| Token Unlock Interval | **30 minutes** |
| Unlock Schedule Begins | **After successful liquidity migration** |
| Full Token Availability | **10 hours after migration** |

Under these settings, an additional **5% of each buyer's purchased allocation becomes available every 30 minutes**, beginning after successful liquidity migration.

The first 5% becomes available after 30 minutes, and the full allocation becomes available after 10 hours.

These percentages apply to each buyer's purchased tokens, not to the token's total supply.

#### Example: How Token Unlocking Works

Suppose a buyer purchases **100,000 tokens** in a TL sale using the current unlock settings.

After successful liquidity migration, the allocation becomes available as follows:

| Time After Migration | Total Unlocked | Total Tokens Unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediately | 0% | 0 |
| 30 minutes | 5% | 5,000 |
| 1 hour | 10% | 10,000 |
| 2 hours | 20% | 20,000 |
| 5 hours | 50% | 50,000 |
| 10 hours | 100% | 100,000 |

The table shows the cumulative unlocked allocation, including any tokens already claimed.

**Buyers do not need to claim tokens every 30 minutes.**

Unclaimed unlocked tokens remain available for later claiming.

For example:

- A buyer who waits five hours without claiming can claim **50,000 tokens** in a single transaction.
- If the buyer previously claimed 10,000 tokens after the first hour, another **40,000 tokens** would be available after five hours.
- A buyer who waits until the full unlock period has passed can claim the entire remaining purchased allocation in one transaction.

Claiming tokens does not accelerate the schedule or make future locked tokens available early.

#### Token Unlock Settings Are Permanent for Each Sale

The protocol administrator configures the token unlock amount and interval used for newly created TL sales.

When a TL sale is created, **the applicable unlock settings are permanently recorded on-chain for that specific sale**.

These settings cannot be changed afterward, including by the creator or through later changes to the protocol's global configuration.

For example, a TL sale created with **5% unlocking every 30 minutes** will retain those conditions even if the protocol administrator later changes the settings for future sales.

This distinction is important:

- **New TL sales** use the protocol's current unlock settings at the time of creation.
- **Existing TL sales** retain the settings recorded when they were created.

The values published in this document represent RugFreePump's current configuration for new TL sales. Buyers should review the actual unlock settings of the specific sale they intend to participate in.

## For Token Creators

Choosing a token sale model determines how contributors will receive access to their purchased tokens after a successful launch.

Creators should select the model that best suits their project's intended token distribution approach and communicate its conditions clearly to potential contributors.

### Choosing a Token Sale Model

During token creation, RugFreePump provides a **Select a token sale model** section where creators choose between TU and TL.

The selected model is recorded on-chain when the sale is created.

**This choice is permanent.** An existing TU sale cannot be converted into TL, and an existing TL sale cannot be converted into TU.

For TL sales, the current unlock amount and interval are also recorded at creation. Creators cannot customize or change these settings for an existing sale.

Creators should review the selected model carefully before confirming the creation transaction.

### When to Choose TU

TU may be suitable for projects that want buyers to have full access to their purchased tokens immediately after successful liquidity migration.

Creators may prefer TU when they want:

- Immediate availability of the entire purchased allocation after migration.
- No gradual token unlock restrictions following the launch.
- A straightforward claiming arrangement for contributors.
- Buyers to have full control over their purchased tokens once claimed.

**Consideration:** Because the full allocation becomes available after migration, buyers may transfer or sell substantial amounts of tokens shortly after claiming them.

TU provides greater immediate token availability, but it does not guarantee favorable market conditions or price stability.

### When to Choose TL

TL may be suitable for projects that prefer a gradual distribution of purchased tokens following successful liquidity migration.

Creators may prefer TL when they want:

- Purchased tokens to become available progressively rather than all at once.
- A clearly defined unlock schedule that contributors can evaluate before buying.
- Token availability to be distributed across a period following migration.
- A launch arrangement where buyers understand that access to their full allocation will take time.

**Consideration:** Gradual unlocking does not prevent selling. Buyers can transfer or trade tokens after claiming them, and external market activity may still affect the token's price.

TL does not guarantee reduced volatility, sustained demand, or long-term project success.

### Creator Responsibilities

Creators should clearly communicate their selected sale model when presenting their projects.

For TL sales, this includes explaining the applicable token unlock amount, interval, and the fact that unlocking begins only after successful liquidity migration.

Creators should not describe locked tokens as immediately available or suggest that gradual unlocking guarantees price stability.

The protocol enforces the selected distribution rules, but it does not guarantee the quality, credibility, or future success of any project.

For the complete creation process, see [Creating a Bonding Curve Sale](/user-guides/for-token-creators/creating-a-bonding-curve-sale).

## For Token Buyers

The sale model directly affects when buyers can access their purchased tokens.

**Before contributing SOL, buyers should verify whether the sale uses TU or TL and understand the corresponding claiming conditions.**

### Buying Tokens Under TU and TL

The purchasing process is the same for both models.

During an active sale, buyers contribute SOL through the bonding curve. The protocol calculates the purchased token amount, applies the applicable buy fee, and records the buyer's token allocation on-chain.

**Purchased tokens are not immediately transferred to the buyer's wallet.**

Instead, they remain in the protocol-controlled token account until the applicable claiming conditions are satisfied.

For purchasing instructions, see [Buying Bonding Curve Sale Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/buying-bonding-curve-sale-tokens).

### When Can Buyers Claim Tokens?

Token claiming depends on successful liquidity migration.

When a sale reaches its fundraising threshold, its status becomes **Completed**. However, this does not necessarily mean liquidity migration has already occurred.

The sale must successfully complete liquidity migration before claiming becomes available.

After migration:

- **TU buyers** can claim their entire purchased allocation without an additional unlock waiting period.
- **TL buyers** can claim the portions of their allocation that have become available according to the sale's unlock schedule.

For TL, the unlock schedule begins when liquidity migration succeeds, **not when the fundraising threshold is reached**.

Buyers should therefore distinguish between the time required for liquidity migration and the additional token unlock period under TL.

### How Token Claiming Works

Both TU and TL require buyers to initiate claiming transactions.

Tokens are not automatically deposited into buyers' wallets when migration succeeds or when a TL unlock interval passes.

For TU, the buyer can claim the full purchased allocation after migration.

For TL, the buyer can claim the currently unlocked amount or wait until more tokens become available and claim the accumulated amount later.

There is no requirement to submit a separate claim transaction for every TL unlock interval.

RugFreePump does not charge an additional protocol fee for claiming. Buyers are responsible for the applicable Solana network transaction fee and any necessary token-account creation costs.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Claiming Purchased Tokens](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/claiming-purchased-tokens).

## Shared Protocol Rules

TU and TL differ in token availability, but both operate under the same fundamental RugFreePump fundraising rules.

### Fundraising and Liquidity Migration

Both models use the same bonding curve mechanism, sale threshold, and standard protocol fee structure.

Neither provides a standard bonding-curve sell-back operation during the active fundraising phase.

Successful sales proceed toward liquidity migration on Raydium CPMM, where the resulting LP tokens are burned by the protocol as part of the standard migration process.

The selected sale model does not change these core mechanics.

### Expired and Canceled Sales

If a sale expires without reaching its fundraising threshold, eligible contributors can withdraw their contributed SOL through the protocol.

The withdrawal mechanism also applies when a finalized sale is canceled through an authorized emergency procedure.

These rules apply to both TU and TL.

For details, see [Withdrawing Contributed SOL](/user-guides/for-token-buyers/withdrawing-contributed-sol).

### Important Risks and Limitations

**Token availability is not a guarantee of value.** Neither TU nor TL guarantees market liquidity, price appreciation, project success, or protection from financial loss.

**TL does not eliminate selling pressure.** Gradual unlocking controls when purchased allocations become claimable. It does not prevent trading of already claimed tokens or other tokens available in external markets.

**Migration may be delayed or encounter failures.** Reaching the fundraising threshold does not guarantee immediate liquidity migration or immediate access to purchased tokens.

**Claiming requires user action.** Buyers must submit transactions to receive claimable tokens, and applicable Solana network costs remain their responsibility.

**Existing sale terms remain fixed.** Buyers should review the selected model and unlock conditions recorded for the specific sale rather than assuming all existing TL sales follow the current settings.

## Making an Informed Choice

RugFreePump offers two approaches to purchased token availability while preserving the same structured fundraising environment.

**TU prioritizes full token availability immediately after successful liquidity migration.**

**TL prioritizes gradual token availability through a predefined unlock schedule.**

Neither model is inherently safer, more profitable, or better suited to every project.

Creators should choose the model that aligns with their intended token distribution approach, while buyers should evaluate whether its conditions are appropriate for their participation.

**Creators select the model. Buyers evaluate the terms. The protocol enforces the claiming rules.**
