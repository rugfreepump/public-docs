Every RugFreePump sale follows a defined lifecycle enforced by the **RugFreePump Solana program**.

The lifecycle begins when a creator creates a sale, continues through the **Live** fundraising stage, and eventually reaches a defined outcome. A sale can successfully reach its hard cap and become **Completed**, continue to **Migrated** liquidity and public trading, or reach **Expired** and enter the withdrawal path.

An authorized emergency procedure can also move a completed sale to **Canceled** when intervention is required.
